# Sen-1 Compact30 Sitka spruce forest floor (first-party, original)

Created for Scenario One #836 (forest-floor pass), 19 November 2026, under the
Manager #824 design verdict on #838 research. Presentation only: this folder
carries no ecology, save, world, collider or gameplay behaviour.

Everything here is originally authored for CCF. The textures are procedurally
generated by `gen_floor_textures.py` (stored with the task evidence, see
provenance below); the meshes are generated in the Unity Editor by the task's
one-off authoring tool; the materials are URP Lit. No third-party photography,
scans or store assets are used.

## How it is used

- The ground is the existing 30 × 30 m `Ground` cube. Only its material reference
  changed; its geometry, the BoxCollider and the 30 m world extent are untouched.
- The base map is sampled once per 4 m, so the litter texture flows continuously
  across the plane with no per-object UV restart. The normal map runs at a
  different rate (1.6 m) so close-range relief does not simply repeat the albedo.
- The macro map is the only large-scale layer: it darkens damp hollows and lifts
  the litter slightly toward the stand margin, which is why the albedo itself is
  deliberately homogeneous above ~20 cm (a 4 m tile cannot carry large shapes
  without a visible repeat).
- `Sen1_SpruceFloorScatter` holds the two groundcover meshes. Both are opaque, have
  no colliders and no LOD. Moss patches cast shadows; twigs do not (they are
  millimetres thick and the shadow would be noise).
- The 46 `LowCover_*` perimeter mounds and the outside continuation plane are
  re-materialled so the floor does not stop abruptly at the property line.

## Reuse contract for later scenarios

- Materials are shared assets. Instance a material per scenario and tint there
  rather than editing these, or Enlarged80 and Reference Future will change.
- The material pair is tuned for this scene's sun and ambient. A different site
  lighting rig needs the `_BaseColor` and `_OcclusionStrength` re-checked against
  a render, not just reused.
- The moss/twig meshes are metre-scale world-space meshes, not prefabs. Reuse the
  textures and materials, and rebuild the scatter for a different stand footprint.

## Limits

- Compact30 is flat, so slope fitting is not demonstrated.
- The 4 m albedo tile repeats 7.5 times across the stand. It is deliberately
  homogeneous so the repeat is not readable, but it is still a repeat.
- The moss/twig scatter is sparse and low-contrast by design; it is close-range
  detail, not a mass groundcover system.
