Requested authored lift targets: 2.5 / 5 / 6.5 m. Actual values use the minimum of each retained whole branch system across all three LODs. Recent and Healed share the same retained wood/foliage; only scar treatment differs.

**Ordinary pole and first-thinning supported. Bent, cavity, forked, broken-top, coarse-defect and seedling families are not revised in this package.** The brief prioritised the ordinary plantation bases. In particular, existing bent/cavity sets still need independently authored low-branch treatments compatible with their stems; no silent fallback/second-tree overlay is supplied.

All 140 branch systems have three separable LOD exports, even above walking height, to support whole-system selection at other scales. All modules share the tree ground origin and contain no complete trunk. Dynamic world-height selection remains integration work.
