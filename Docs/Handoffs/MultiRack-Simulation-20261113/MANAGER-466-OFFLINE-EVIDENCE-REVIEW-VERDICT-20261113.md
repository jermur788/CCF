3. **Treatment uniformity cannot be inferred from similar averages.** At the old calibration grid every destination is partly comparable, some tiles differ substantially and site/soil/wind/machine-access evidence is UNKNOWN. The old tolerances are sensitivity knobs, not Irish forestry thresholds. Two tile verdicts change across years, so source staleness remains a real decision topic.

The report shows Years 0–6 actual saved economy/biology; it does not validate compact training-site prices, whole-rack productivity or human work time.
