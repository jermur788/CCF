## 3. Research questions — produce concrete alternative designs, not just restate intentions

### A. Define the authoritative spatial units and identity
- Inspect current C# implementation before asserting truth: `StandGeometryModel`, actual Rack V2 confirmed centreline/entrance plan, `ScenarioOneRackLayout` / coverage/MFM geometry, `ForestTree` IDs, ecology 5 m cells, world boundaries, Stand Map, historic sample IDs, saved orders and prescriptions. Read [Unity Project Overview](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Docs/Project/unity-project-overview.md); source and verified Unity evidence outrank overview.
- Establish a **source rack-served management polygon**, per-destination rack/area polygon, disjoint coverage masks and explicit corridors/clearing/edge exclusions; quantify net hectares **without double-counting** overlap. Every eligible tree/cell belongs to a defined management scope; do not silently force nearest-rack assignment of boundary/outer strips.
- Keep **new world/rack/prescription/sample identifiers separate** from old Legacy40/Enlarged80 and compact save identities. An algorithm may reference actual tree IDs as *evidence*, never copy source tree IDs or treatments onto different trees by ID coincidence.
- Test how **one representative whole rack**, the earlier fixed 24 m teaching sample, and **multiple representative rack/stand units** differ. Document whether any topology requires a new approved spec rather than silently generalising the five-rack model.

### B. Formalise comparability before extrapolating
- Identify which **currently measured/simulated observable** inputs can justify a similarity decision: eligible live species and stages, density/stems per net hectare, DBH distribution, basal area, competition, crowns/canopy, local stand structure, retained work, current juvenile/broadleaf patches, and *real* site/terrain/wind evidence **only when actually modelled**.
- Specify **not-similar/unknown/insufficient-data** as first-class outcomes. Different species, age/maturity, stocking, wind exposure or regeneration constraints cannot inherit Sitka adult prescriptions by assumption. Unmapped soil/terrain classes are **UNKNOWN / MANUAL**, not secretly equal.
- Compare 2–3 bounded explainable algorithms, e.g. source-derived **treatment-intensity range** within validated similar stand units, structural/spacing proposals with explicit candidate confirmation, or multiple representative samples with manual exceptions. For each, show inputs, unit/denominator and uncertainty, source-year baseline, no double counted tree/cell, patient/negative examples, and failure to transfer. No arbitrary universal percentage of trees removed or fabricated Irish thresholds.
- Before proposing numeric thresholds, inspect D-065's **existing cover/fit calibration and its evidence** and #448's proposed source→destination **BA cap/intervention-window** options. Those values/rules remain DRAFT or gameplay calibration unless separately accepted, regardless of how mathematically convenient.

### C. Preserve player agency, real work and learning
- Source workflow (the player's real individual decision-making in 3D): inspect **all relevant trees for the selected whole-rack teaching area** under newer Objective 2 intent, distinguish **Crop Tree** (retained desirable candidate) from **Fell** (actual removal), observe retention/competition/seed/deadwood and correct simple mark handling. Do not infer quality from diameter or spacing alone; do not auto-tag unspecified trees as Crop Trees.
- Destination workflow: explain **why** each rack/area does or does not match; show a proposed treatment intensity, candidate locations, exclusions, expected current-state BA effects and cash/time exposure. Keep `Fell` proposals **distinct** from marks and work; manually designated player trees and pending/approved work take precedence. The player may accept, adjust, inspect selected candidates in 3D, restrict or reject an area, or perform fully manual management.
- Approval order is binding: proposed treatment → deliberate prescription approval (creates **Pending** orders with provenance) → **ordinary Work Plan approval** → actually resolved tree work at the authoritative annual boundary. No automatic felling/harvest/budget/credit merely because a rack is Covered, an annual skip runs or a map overlay displays a treatment.
- Repeat cycles: after actual annual change, **re-observe and re-test comparability**. A prior source rack/Year1 prescription is not automatically suitable in Year5 or another rack. Investigate one representative rack per cycle versus multiple samples when heterogeneous, spatial and calendar provenance, reassessment, D-074 substantive episode conditions, D-077 skip stop gates, changes in all *treated* areas and S1-C observational truth.
- UX: show whole-rack source work without exhausting the player; compare mark/inspect count, explanations, number of required confirmation clicks, map status and 3D navigation for full vs partial vs manual areas; compare **estimated interaction workload** separately from **observed user playtest time**. Existing `extend-treatment` ID and old step credit must remain valid unless an approved/save-safe migration expressly changes it.

### D. Economic, ecological, persistence and verification validity
- Source/tree-level simulation remains authoritative. Extrapolated work must ultimately affect each specific actual tree in each approved rack's area, producing genuine crown/light/competition/regen/volume/cash/history outcomes after each year, not multiplication of one rack's aggregate result or cached projected Year5 outcomes.
- Distinguish **existing forestry harvest €2,500 minimum per commissioned job/visit** from an invented *per rack* charge. Inspect actual quote grouping, assortments, retention/deadwood, net revenue, contractor vs owner work and safeguards against duplicate tree IDs/payment. Do not transfer compact **training-site non-profit/small-tree-surgeon option** wholesale to larger multi-rack forest commercial logic without user decision.
- Annual Sitka/Oak/Beech comparable modelling depth must remain; D-065 Sitka-adult quantitative transfer cannot silently operate on Oak/Beech without accepted separate species-aware rules. Edge/cell boundaries are significant, as #460 small-property scratch demonstrated, but that study did **not** measure multi-rack edges or wind.
- Explicit save fields needed for source rack/polygon, source year/condition snapshot, prescription version, fit rationale, destination membership, manual exclusion and approved/executed order linkage; do not choose schema or alter existing saves without save-owner approval. Negative matrix for v1–v24, Legacy40, Enlarged80, frozen Reference Future v1, old five-rack practice IDs, devices' PlayerPrefs and compact namespaces.
- Recommend proportionate **FAST offline pure C#** (geometry/fit/order dedup/policy/negative controls), **focused Unity feature** (actual world generation, reach/inspection, map, true load/annual settlement), **one full integrated** demo candidate at milestone only. Distinguish evidence source receipts from proposed benchmarks. No permanent T-0 test framework/refactor or Unity lease granted by this research.
