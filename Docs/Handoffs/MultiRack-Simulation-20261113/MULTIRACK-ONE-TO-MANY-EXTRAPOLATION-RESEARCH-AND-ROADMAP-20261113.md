## 1. Executive determination

**Recommended research route (conditional, not ratified):** support three *separate player contexts* with one reusable principle but no universal rack or species formula.

1. **New compact one-rack introductory Scenario One:** physically manage a bounded standing parcel on both sides of ONE real access rack, repeat genuine annual work/review cycles, and do **not** extrapolate to phantom racks. This is the latest accepted owner *direction* for the first external playable, but the detailed compact four-file specification remains DRAFT, its independent [#463](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/issues/463) review is pending, and no compact world/save/contractor route has implementation acceptance.
2. **Existing Enlarged80 five-rack world / Objective 2:** for the newer [#439](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/issues/439) user intent, the player surveys/manages the **entire finite standing area served by one real rack**, makes actual individual Crop Tree / competitor / retention choices, authorises and executes source work, then **evaluates** four other rack areas with disjoint condition-specific subareas. Only genuinely comparable subareas receive *proposals*. Every external Crop Tree must still be judged by the player in 3D under approved D-065; no copied source IDs or automatic quality grading. A second independent Work Plan authorises actual jobs. Unrepresentative units stay Manual/Excluded.
3. **Later larger forests, including future multi-compartment scenarios:** allow **multiple** separately evidenced representative sources by genuinely comparable stand condition, rather than presume one rack represents an entire property. Do not now create an estate engine, a rack-level biological simulator, cross-scenario budgets or additional species coefficients.

This is a **scope split**, not a supersession decision. The newer whole-rack Objective 2 direction conflicts with accepted D-065's **first teaching 24m × 18m three-Crop-Tree sample** and D-076's instruction that the original first sample remains unchanged. Only the user/#425 can reconcile the first-teaching contract and approve new objective/save semantics. Meanwhile old saves, old prescriptions, historical teaching credit and frozen Reference Future v1 keep their original meaning.

**Core rule:** a sample communicates *intent*; it does not prove destination tree quality, stand comparability, a universally transferable removal quota, or completion. Source and target work must be real individually addressed work, costed and resolved by the ordinary world simulator. Unsupported site/wind/soil conditions are UNKNOWN, not silently “similar.” One rack may legitimately cover zero of four others.

**Research readiness:** evidence and design alternatives are provided below. Actual five-rack per-rack geometry, source/destination BA, heterogeneous fit, queue sizes, player minutes, cumulative cap and full-rack runtime proof are **NOT MEASURED / NOT RUN by #464**. No standalone Tier-1 test, Unity test, rendered UX test or economic settlement was executed by this research manager. The historical compact #460 measurements are authentic research for **different scratch profiles**, not a five-rack dataset.

### What practice evidence actually justifies

The 2006 Forest Research UK case-study report records site differences (soil/access/stand conditions), instances where feller-select was not suitable, and other cases where a **marked example area** guided feller-select and later inspections. Its executive summary sections 14 and 5 directly support a sample plus monitoring/recordkeeping, not universal extrapolation. The report also says site conditions can preclude the method; the scheme should therefore refuse unmatched areas rather than treat “all other racks” as automatically served. [Source](https://cdn.forestresearch.gov.uk/2022/02/ipin_1306_ccf_case_studies.pdf). Forest Research's 2026 handbook treats stand/site suitability, thinning stability and regeneration as separate decisions [source](https://www.forestresearch.gov.uk/publications/transformation-management-continuous-cover-forestry/). Pro Silva Ireland distinguishes retained-quality, biodiversity and removal/access marks; timber quality is a judgement, not a DBH-only calculation [source](https://prosilvaireland.com/about-ccf/tree-marking/).

The supplied project [Delegated Marking and Operator Selection](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Docs/Project/research-index.md#delegated-treatment--sample-to-comparable-area-evidence-d-065) research packet further cites Irish thinning control plots, limited first-thinning sample racks and UK examples, with qualifications about comparable homogeneous units and operator discretion. Classification: **IRISH/UK PROFESSIONAL ANALOGUE**, with **DESIGN INFERENCE** for translating it into player approval/proposal workflows. It does not supply a validated one-to-four transfer factor, numerical rack-width sampling rule, probability of representativeness, or permission to automate uninspected Crop Tree suitability.

## 3. The correct units, geometry and identity

**Authoritative units are individual identified trees and ecology cells; a rack is access infrastructure; a rack-served treatment polygon is a derived management grouping.** Keep these three roles separate. Current stand geometry defines Legacy40 = 40×40m, 64 cells and Enlarged80 = 80×80m, **256 cells, 5m cell resolution, starting density 2,100 stems/ha and target 1,344 starting Sitka** (source policy, not an observed current per-rack distribution): [StandGeometryModel.cs lines 10–60](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/research/multirack-sim-extrapolation-20261113-v1/Assets/ForestPrototype/StandGeometryModel.cs#L10-L60). #460 independently reproduced the 1,344-tree control in Unity, but does not report the per-rack comparison requested here.

**Proposed whole-rack geometry definition (design, must be accepted/verified):**

- Read the existing persisted **confirmed** five-rack plan with stable numeric rack IDs, true finite entrance and far endpoints, real corridor width and settled geometry version. Display ordering is not ID authority. Reject an absent, changed, overlapping, crossing, degenerate or otherwise unsupported plan; never build phantom racks.
- For each rack, define a finite service envelope from its actual entrance-to-far *projected longitudinal segment*, with explicit ends and optional end treatment **OPEN-1**, intersected with the legitimate property polygon. Use midpoint boundaries between neighbouring parallel rack centrelines in the common normal direction as the **interior cross-rack separator**. Ties on boundaries have an explicit deterministic half-open ownership convention; no tree belongs to two standing treatment units. The outermost strip beyond half the interior gap is explicitly Manual/Unserved pending OPEN-1; do not assign an entire perimeter by nearest rack.
- Subtract the **union**, not sum, of recorded corridor polygons, western headland, southern feeder, access/path and real clearance/site masks, protected exclusions, and known unreachable/other nonmanaged regions. Keep the geometric concepts separate: net standing area versus net eligible Sitka-adult area versus all property area. An exclusion around an object must use its actual authoritative footprint, not an invented circle. Unknown legal/physical accessibility is UNKNOWN/Manual; none of this claims that walkable implies harvester-safe.
- Suggested new **coverage tiles**: finite length bands entrance→far, each left/right of rack (for illustration four along-axis bands × two sides), with explicit stable geometry-versioned IDs, mask fractions and no empty/underpowered false fit. **Four bands, tile sizes and net-minimum stems are NOT accepted.** The old low/centre/high 15 area IDs describe an anchor ±12m and must be kept for old MFM prescriptions; they cannot silently change meaning in saved history.
- Calculate physical net hectare A = area of **disjoint** eligible standing polygon in m² / 10,000. Density = living eligible stems / A. BA = sum over unique eligible tree IDs of π × (DBH_cm / 200)² m²; BA/ha = BA / A. Do not subtract the corridor twice, treat an executed stump as a live stem or use property hectares when quoting standing-area BA/ha.
- Tree centres belong to exactly one active tile, one rack management grouping or an explicit remainder/unknown area. A 5m ecology cell may straddle management polygons: use overlap weighting for *read-only reporting* when defensible, not duplicating its biological state or inventing a new per-rack ecology. Exposure to neighbours across polygon boundaries must remain in the biological competition calculation; a rack boundary is **not** a competition cutoff.

**Stable identities needed:** scenario/world/profile and stand geometry identity (new compact distinct from historical five-rack), actual saved rack ID, source-rack and source-polygon-version ID, sample-year and pre/post work-phase, source-recipe version, finite tile ID, source Crop Tree IDs/removal event IDs, each target actual tree ID, coverage choice and reason, generated order ID, prescription ID, resolution year and later review provenance. Positions/IDs from the source are **evidence**, not an index for selecting same-numbered target trees; IDs can repeat across unrelated worlds. Old Reference Future and Legacy40 must remain on their own historical path.

**What cannot presently be asserted:** exact valid full-length service envelopes, outer remnant sizes, net hectares, number of eligible adults per rack, or any real 5-rack corridor/forest competition “fit.” The pure code's existing midpoint strip can supply a testable starting algorithm but not a full-rack native proof.

## 4. Similarity before extrapolation

A valid sample teaches a **treatment method** for stand conditions, not an unconditional instruction to remove the same fraction of every other rack's trees. The algorithm must compare **current** source and destination conditions at a consistent time, with transparent reasons and positive/negative examples.

### 4.1 Observable evidence categories (proposed input policy)

| Input group | Source truth / candidate observable | Safe policy |
|---|---|---|
| Geometry and scope | confirmed rack endpoints, corridor width, real hard masks, standing tile and net ha, stable IDs | Necessary hard gate; invalid/empty/unknown topology = Manual/NotComparable |
| Species/age/stage | actual species identifiers, life stage, known age/diameter class, living eligibility; current juveniles or broadleaf patches | First quantitative transfer **adult Sitka only**. Oak/Beech manual or explicitly excluded until new accepted species-rule version; same model depth does not justify same coefficients |
| Stand stocking and size | living eligible density per net ha, BA/ha, mean AND distribution/quantiles of DBH, adult count | Check source/destination distribution, not just a matching mean; area too small/stems too few = InsufficientData |
| Competition / local structure | current authoritative Hegyi/CompetitionShare neighbourhood, crown/light, crop/competitor grouping, unevenness; cross-boundary neighbours remain visible | Compare central tendency and troublesome upper tails where robust. Do not manufacture “tree quality” or claim all matched-mean crowns are equivalent |
| Regeneration and site features | current planted juveniles, natural-cohort/cell state, browsing protection, clearance, deadwood, retained habitat and exact local work | Real constraints/partial exclusion. Cells can contribute to comparative observation but not auto-felling or invented ecological quality |
| Work/continuity | current manual Crop Tree/Fell marks, pending/approved/unresolved orders, prior releases, storms/mortality, already committed BA | Manual/committed state always takes precedence; count distinct stems once; conflicting proposals unavailable |
| Site risk | measured and genuinely modelled soil moisture/stability, exposure, wind/wet slope/access where the current profile actually stores validated distinctions | **UNKNOWN** where no source fact exists; if material to decision, return Manual/RequestInspection, not a fictitious site class |
| Time/provenance | source baseline and executed year, current destination year, rule version, interventions/annual changes, authentic historical sample state | Compare like phases; stale source invalid or needs fresh demonstration/explicit revalidation under OPEN-6 |

**Existing calibration, not new authority:** MFM03's 20% density / 15% mean DBH / 20% mean competition and 30 minimum adult stems were measured against **one old geometry/fixture**, and 16m candidate spacing is a separate gameplay parameter. They should enter a sensitivity grid as candidate settings, never auto-promote to new whole-rack production defaults. The old single sample covering about 13/14 old areas is not evidence of independent representative rack sufficiency. No formal statistical confidence intervals or Irish field probabilities are justified.

### 4.2 First-class outcomes

- **Covered:** sufficient comparable current data and no hard constraints; proposal generation may begin, still subject to human candidate confirmation and all approvals.
- **Partial:** rack has some Covered finite tiles, with explicitly mapped NotCovered/Excluded/Manual tiles; never label its entire rack Covered.
- **NotCovered:** measured material dissimilarity (species/stage/diameter distribution, density, local competition, management history), with named failing metrics.
- **Excluded:** actual protection, existing work, unusable access corridor, nonstand, juvenile/habitat protection or other known prohibition.
- **Manual:** player opt-out, unresolved ownership, conflicting or unknown measurement, unsupported site hazard, underpowered tile, bad lineage, stale sample or uncertain machine access; leave ordinary individual-tree management available.
- **InsufficientSourceEvidence:** entire source may have been authentically surveyed but lacked reliable **executed** positive release data. Record honest no-release judgements and complete inspection where justified; do not force extra Fell marks just to manufacture a transferable numeric rule.

Each tile and containing rack shows: effective net ha; sample and destination density/BA/structure; current and prior work; which dimensions pass/fail, why a mark is merely suggested, confidence as **qualitative measured/boundary/unknown**, and the number of unconfirmed candidates. A fit is not a probability of correct harvest.

### 4.3 Source release rule and cumulative safeguards (candidate algorithms)

The newer Objective 2 [DRAFT FR-07](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/docs/objective2-446-delta-corrections-20261113-v1/specs/scenario-one-objective2-whole-rack-sampling-v1/spec.md#fr-07-derived-rule-new-namespaceversion) correctly distinguishes a surveyed Crop Tree needing no intervention from a verified **positive executed release**. A usable source record should contain the real pre-removal neighbour denominator, actual executed competing stem IDs, for each player-retained Crop Tree; if two Crop Trees benefit from removal of the *same stem*, its contribution may enter both **competition-share** explanations but the physical stem/BA/job is counted once. Zero or missing denominator, source work still Pending, missing stamps or incomplete source reconstruction = no transferable release statistic.

**Not ratified:** positive-release count **r ≥ 3** (gameplay calibration), choice of **minimum / lower quantile / median** observed genuine positive release Shares, and any target quantile percentile. A higher quantile may ask for more removal than a source's most cautious example; a minimum may be dominated by one weak release. Compare all three by deterministic replay and proposed BA/CI safety; do not label one scientific. Never require release around every Crop Tree. No automated target tree quality judgement.

**BA ledger:** for tile T and containing rack R, record unique eligible source-time tree IDs and pre-intervention eligible living Sitka BA. Distinguish baseline phase/year, already executed in-window felling, manually Fell-marked/Pending/Approved, target proposals, and mortality. A proposed cap check has the form unique in-window removal BA / authenticated same-window pre-intervention eligible BA ≤ candidate cap; **also** check tile, containing rack, and individual Crop Tree-neighbour severity. A 40% maximum is the old accepted *sample* gameplay safeguard, **not yet an accepted whole-rack tile/rack cap**. A good rack-wide mean cannot excuse a catastrophic concentrated tile. Treat the canonical book p75's general at-most-20% preharvest BA advice as contextual management guidance, **not** a substitution for the game rule.

**Temporal cap alternatives remain OPEN-3/OPEN-6:** (A1) year-N pre-source-resolution destination baseline and count all work in N..N+1, (A2) post-N/current destination preparation baseline and count target stage N+1 after that point; or (B1) pre-N long campaign accounting through M with full history versus (B2) fresh pre-M-only eligible BA with separate source staleness validation. Every choice needs a defined denominator and distinct manual work inclusion. Check at prescription preview/approval and **again immediately before actual annual resolution**, using live available BA, new mortality/growth, cancellations and original campaign ledger. A now-unsafe approved set must fail closed and require explicit reapproval/replanning; no “approval-time pass forever.” No stale-approved orders may silently wait through multi-year skips.

## 5. Three implementable DESIGN ALTERNATIVES (none approved)

|---|---|---|---|
| Source and destinations | One authentic full finite source polygon, other four independent disjoint rack-served areas/tiles | One or more independently executed and reviewed source polygons, each assigned to a measured homogeneous family; no overlapping recipe ownership | Each tree/area is separately inspected; map only filters, groups, navigates, quotes |
| Human work | Whole source inspection and actual manual Crop/Fell/retain decisions; target candidate inspection/confirm/reject for every suggested Crop Tree | More source walking/work but fewer falsely comparable destinations; each matched target still individually confirmed | Greatest manual mark workload on large properties but strongest explicit judgement and minimal inference |
| Rule | Closed, source-derived positive executed release statistic and unit BA safeguards; fit measured against source and destination | Family-specific rule with provenance, hierarchy/conflict resolution; source A never silently applies in family B | No derived numeric propagation; optional read-only assessment guides human choices |
| Safety/comparability | Hard-gated Covered/Partial/NotCovered/Excluded/Manual, positive source proof, local cap and independent Work Plan | Extra source sampling for heterogeneity; an unmatched area remains manual | No prescription transfer errors; conventional per-tree/work protection still required |
| Storage/history | Source rack/polygon/year/phase, versioned rule, tile membership and reasons, target confirmed IDs, order links, actual annual events | Same plus multiple source/recipe ownership, conflict and validity windows | Existing tree/order/history primarily; new profile/teaching evidence if instructional flow changes |
| Player approvals | Source Work Plan → source execution/review → coverage/candidates → prescription approval (Pending) → target Work Plan → target execution/review | Same, independently per family, serialised or explicitly combined only through approved conflict checks | Ordinary Work Plan, each year's execution/review; no prescription approval |
| Biology/economy | Actual per-tree work and same authoritative annual simulation; annual aggregate report derives from world | Same; more heterogeneous meaningful outcomes | Same; more human authoring, less algorithm complexity |
| Main risks | Whole source may take too long; one sample can be atypical; first/second annual timing disputed; hidden quality substitution | More onboarding burden; recipe collisions, stale sources and broad save complexity | Repetitive five-rack gameplay; training intent for scaled operation not met |
| Best use | Preferred **five-rack Objective 2 starting design** IF native coverage and candidate workload qualify and OPEN-1..6 are accepted | Escalation for demonstrably heterogeneous larger worlds/future O8 compartments; possible explicit exception in five-rack if one source fails | Compact introductory one-rack and any NotCovered/manual areas; always available as override |

**Recommended option:** A **with B as a conditional extension, C as the unconditional safe fallback**. This is smallest compatible with the owner's latest five-rack learning goal while protecting real tree-level judgements. Reject any promise that all four destination racks *must* receive treatment. A demonstrably heterogeneous target may be **zero proposal**, and the player can still visit/mark it manually. B requires a separate scoped DRAFT if first release needs multiple simultaneous sources, because it introduces recipe ownership and chronology. Compact training uses C intentionally, rather than a degenerate A with four fake empty racks.

**Alternative B is not automatically better just because more samples exist:** determine stand-family boundaries from data; repeated samples in one homogeneous family can be redundant while a materially different small patch still needs its own review. ### User interaction cost, not invented playtest time

This formula counts *decision opportunities*, not measured clicks/minutes; if a tree is visited twice count separately as interaction, not a unique tree. Count unique source/target tree IDs and separate inspection, paint, reading and acceptance UI actions in a real playtest. Under the approved D-065 default, each target suggestion must be confirmed/rejected in 3D; a proposed area-level **batch/no-inspection** shortcut is OPEN-5 and cannot silently replace the user as arbiter of visible quality.
