### Annual order and intervention timing

`ScenarioOneManager.cs:1348–1357` resolves approved work/financial settlement before one ecology annual step, then planted juveniles/understorey/deadwood. `ForestEcologyController.AdvanceOneYear:458–499` increments ecological year, creates annual RNG, resolves a pre-growth storm, updates competition, recomputes canopy, grows adults, relaxes crowns, applies density deaths if growth≥1, recomputes canopy, prepares regeneration-model-2 competitor state, grows existing regeneration, updates mast, computes seed rain, establishes cohorts, promotes them, updates suitability, decays openings, invalidates storm context and logs. Density mortality thus precedes that year's new promotions. Natural deaths trigger invalidation/rebuild after the victim batch; surviving trees do not receive a second DBH release step that year.

Felling before annual advancement changes that coming step's neighbour set. Source `OnTreeFelled` invalidates CI; the next annual update uses survivors. Removing a neighbour after a completed growth step affects the next one. A lab annual transaction must specify this boundary, not reinterpret P0 calendar advance as a biological year. Storms, juveniles, light, regeneration, browsing and promotion are **NOT IMPLEMENTED in P0 or by this audit**; a closed adult-only comparator omitting them is not full-world CCF parity.
