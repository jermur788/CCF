### Evidence vocabulary

**[E]** empirical measured observation; always specify geography, scale and study population. **[M]** model or mathematical definition/structure, not independent empirical validation of a new use. **[C]** proposed simulation/game calibration and acceptance choice, not a forestry standard. **[G]** gap, unavailable/unproved source property or transfer outside evidence. **[T]** transfer from British/continental literature to Irish stands. Source keys below refer to the bibliography, and each row's measurement is a *requirement for future data*, not an assertion that the Multi engine supplies it.

**Read-across:** “COMPARABLE” is **treatment-specific and time-specific** (e.g., suitable for *the proposed crop-tree release in the same current year*), never a permanent property of two forests. “DIFFERENT” requires a credible observed contraindication under a later Manager-accepted rule; “UNKNOWN” includes both absent data and uncalibrated comparison, and does not silently become DIFFERENT. Until that rule exists, **none of the rows establish an automatic match**.

### Distinguishing observations from simulations

The external papers establish *what variables can matter*, not that CCF's Treecode exposes those variables or that its individual-tree dynamics reproduce Irish empirical response. A synthetic tree coordinate/DBH may be a real **source field [M]** after #842 proves file:line, but it is not a field observation **[E]**. COFORD/GROWFOR [S2,S5] is an Irish even-aged stand calibration/forecast framework, not an independent field validation of Multi's spatial individual-tree or CCF model. Conry & Clinch [S4] is Irish yield-class/site evidence, **not** a density maximum equation. In particular, Broad & Lynch 2006b inspected data representativeness; it did **not** independently validate out-of-sample final dynamic forecasts [S5].

## 2. Conservative comparator architecture hypothesis — NOT APPROVED

1. **Source gate before ecology:** accept only exact committed experiment/model/fixture/year/revision, distinct nonoverlapping management scope identity, complete exact live-tree membership, safe area denominator and known measurement provenance. If these fail, G1 has **no target** to compare. No original P1/100-tree subdivision.
2. **Declare intended treatment:** mark sample *per-tree* intent and crop/seed/quality reasons, management objective, intervention type. Comparison criteria vary with purpose: release of a crop tree needs local competition; regeneration-related intervention also needs seedbed/browse/light; stability-sensitive thinning needs exposure/soil.
3. **Per-factor classification:** for each required factor record source field, units, scale, observation/model/calibration origin, precision, missingness, and **support / conflict / unknown**. Observed hard constraints or genuinely incompatible contexts are grounds for DIFFERENT; missing or uncalibrated evidence stays UNKNOWN. **No numerical tolerances, weights or count-based match rule are chosen here.**
4. **Per-target tri-state:** **COMPARABLE** only if *all treatment-critical factors* have source and accepted calibration support, with no conflict; **DIFFERENT** on an accepted explicit incompatibility/forbidden operation; otherwise **UNKNOWN**. Both DIFFERENT and UNKNOWN yield **no automatic G1 proposal**. Display factor-by-factor audit and the particular source revision; preserve Manual/NotCovered. This is a proposed fail-closed ordering, not an accepted comparator or an assertion that every factor is in the engine.
5. **Only for a qualified target:** separately select actual target live candidate trees based on target-local spatial/context facts, produce exact-ID *hypothetical* suggestions with explanatory evidence. **Do not treat target-level similarity as blanket authority for every tree.** Preserve per-area/per-tree player accept/reject/override, separately reviewed prescription before PendingWork, explicit single whole Work Plan approval and separate G2 Execute. Proposal ≠ fell ≠ observed next-year effect.
6. **Invalidate on change:** source year, digest, tree live/dead status, area boundaries, feature version, treatment/revision, accepted prescription and current availability changes ⇒ re-evaluate; never replay old proposals. Source-specific shortfalls require new design/approval rather than a fake pass.

**Risk trade-off:** false positives (proposing unsuitable tree/area Fell) are more severe than false negatives (requiring manual player marking). Consequently, abstention / UNKNOWN is the defensible default. But a “strict comparator” with nearly everything unknown also fails Multi's feature goal; **the answer is better source/fixture evidence or explicit owner acceptance of a clearly labelled synthetic teaching approximation, not silently weakening safety.**

## 3. Proposed independent fixture and calibration / holdout protocol — NO fixtures created

**Precondition:** #842 must first prove source field availability and area semantics, units/precision and whether a new larger independent fixture is technically and physically feasible without touching the protected original. #841 G2 event/atomicity is an independent implementation gate. If either fails, stop at source/evidence contract.

**Candidate minimum cases** (coverage requirement from #828, *not current real areas*): one distinct sample area **S**, **two separately constituted target areas T1 and T2** with independent source-backed tree memberships and genuinely evaluated matching evidence, and **one deliberate different area D** plus, if feasible, a separate **unknown-data area U**. These letter names are placeholders, not claimed engine IDs. T1 and T2 must not be clones or one synthetic 30m area split by a visual stripe. They need distinct tree/tree-neighbour distributions and per-area source stamps; comparable means compatible for a specified treatment, not identical.

**Calibration sequence proposed for later approval:**

1. **Freeze input provenance** before labels: separate new manifest/fixture version, geometry, tree IDs, source year/digest, sensor or model source, area memberships and every missing feature; retain original 100-ID no-work oracle unmodified. Track how independence across management units is actually achieved.
2. **Pre-register qualitative criteria** and expected *case-specific* decisions from forestry review, blind to comparator output; specify management objective and treatment-critical features per case. Do not assign a numeric distance/weight/cutoff from intuition, 170mm G2 metadata, or rack spacing.
3. **Derive only from evidence-supported fields:** explicit source-to-target feature comparison with named conflicts/unknowns. If no site/wind/soil/source scope exists and the treatment would need them, those cases cannot be field-defensible matches; pause or obtain real independently authored evidence. Source-simulated toy fixtures can exercise code but cannot prove empirical transfer accuracy.
4. **Keep holdouts separate:** use T2 and D/U as withheld label/evidence checks after defining the rule from independent exemplars, and add a second differently authored fixture/stand-year for external validity before claiming a stable comparator. Avoid tuning on the same T2/D/U cases then calling them independent. Vary stand structure independently of same species/age.
5. **Record per-area confusion/abstention:** proposed COMPARABLE/DIFFERENT/UNKNOWN versus prespecified independent assessor labels; false-positive/false-negative/abstention reasoning, coverage, changed inputs, local per-tree selection effects, edge/scale uncertainty and next-year repeat behaviour. A target with missing evidence must never be scored as a true comparable merely to increase coverage. No magic acceptance percentage.
6. **Sensitivity, negative controls and review:** vary feature precision, density heterogeneity, age/history, height/site, slope/soil/exposure and missingness one at a time where the source genuinely permits. Replay deterministic committed frames; inspect edge/tiny-sample artefacts; independently review rejected/unknown cases. Escalate to Manager for **actual numerical/ordinal game-calibration choices, test acceptance tolerances and product scope**. Do not have the research worker choose them.

**Calibration boundary:** one synthetic S/T1/T2/D/U fixture might verify label handling, source identity and hypothetical proposal mechanics, but **cannot measure a biological response predictor's skill**. Longer-term response calibration would need independent measured Sitka stands under comparable, specified interventions (or a defensible benchmark with domain limitations), baseline and after-treatment repeated inventories, explicit site/wind/soil, and withheld stands/years. Irish even-aged GROWFOR and old UK demonstration sites cannot be silently promoted to an individual-tree irregular-CCF validation oracle.
