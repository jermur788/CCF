The assertion of hundreds of Sitka-occupied observations is **not supported by that dataset**. Do not confuse *Picea abies* (Norway spruce) with *Picea sitchensis* (Sitka spruce).  
   Source: https://www.slu.se/globalassets/slu.se/om-slu/organisation/institutioner/skoglig-resurshushallning/riksskogstaxeringen/dokument/dokument/info-data-for-calibration.pdf , species footnote 2.

   Primary sources: same SLU PDF, https://www.slu.se/en/environment/statistics-and-environmental-data/environmental-data-catalogue/swedish-national-forest-inventory-sample-plot-data/ ; catalogue notes annual/current data and exclusions.
