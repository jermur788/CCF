## Existing source reused, numerical limitations stated
- [Unity ForestEcologyController.cs](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/ForestEcologyController.cs) (readback blob 0290a7beab639e48585f97c6af8cf993959b9f37) already computes 5m-cell centre canopy/light from original living tree coordinates, real radius and height: reach = radius * 1 + half-cell; lateral = clamp01(1 - distance / reach); vertical = clamp01(height/8); combined gap = product(1 - lateral*vertical); canopy = 1-gap; light = gap. Annual sequence recomputes before and after grown crowns and after batched Fell. Its k10a10 reach = 1 is **gameplay calibration, NOT validated PAR**. Source dead/stump policy must be inspected before reuse.
- [Unity ForestEcologyCell.cs](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/ForestEcologyCell.cs) stores Canopy and Light. Unity TreeSpeciesDefinition includes *juvenile* light-response curves for a future task; they are NOT a ground-light calibration.
- [Standalone 2D AnnualRules.cs](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/a692e1bf5d21304d06e696db1f47d1988ff80ee1/Tools/MultiRackSitkaP1Lab/src/Core/AnnualRules.cs) handles adult competition, DBH/height, crown evolution and mortality, but has no forest-floor light field. ## Accuracy is checked during feature development
1. Source/fixture baseline: exact two-area coordinates, radius source, actual polygon, tree status, units, area identity, geometry used by the light code rather than focused SVG artwork.
2. Offline Unity-math crosswalk: 0, 1, 2 overlapping trees, boundaries, different heights and dead/felled trees, numerical agreement with original Unity proxy; 5m versus 2.5m resolution sensitivity. Parity with Unity proves porting fidelity, **not** physical light fidelity.
3. Field crosscheck: compare BA, stocking, treatment and time against [Hale et al. 2009 UK Sitka canopy transmittance](https://doi.org/10.1093/forestry/cpp020), [COFORD Irish CCF trial](https://www.coford.ie/media/coford/content/publications/projectreports/CCF.pdf), [Forest Research light note](https://cdn.forestresearch.gov.uk/2004/12/fcin063.pdf) and attached project growth/light composites. Ballinagappoge canopy openness 8.3/11.3% immediately after thinning and 5.5/7.2% after two years is SITE-SPECIFIC; **openness is not measured PAR**, not a universal target. The ~20% Sitka seedling RLI guideline is context-specific and belongs to later regeneration coupling.
4. Compare either faithful Unity **modelled light index** (candidate U), or a genuinely fitted **relative light transmittance** model (candidate V) when actual field inputs support it. Do not invent LAI, extinction coefficient, crown opacity, full directional solar radiation or percent PAR just to produce a heatmap. If neither is validated, display an honestly labelled **light index**, not false precision.
5. On approved source only, compare same-area no-work vs >10 actual Fell years 0/1/3; confirm clipped polygon UNION canopy cover (not sum of crown disks), area-local light and unchanged Work Plan, deterministic seeded recomputation, finite 0..1 values and one real browser overlay. No broad Q1, P0/strace, Unity/Windows testing.
