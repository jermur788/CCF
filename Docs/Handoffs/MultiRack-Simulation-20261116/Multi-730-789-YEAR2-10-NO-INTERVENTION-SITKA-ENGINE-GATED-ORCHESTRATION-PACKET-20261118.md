## 0. Explicit owner product choices accepted for this work

Owner direct approval on 18 November 2026 of four recommended policies is binding *product intent* for this narrow no-intervention slice, not approval of code or unchosen schema names:

- **D-1 zero living:** terminal state. If zero living adults remain, another annual advance is refused with an explicit typed reason and **no state mutation**; no fabricated empty-year chronology or NaN. A singleton is different: living growth permitted, with last-survivor density-boundary protection per accepted source rule; hazard handling remains governed by source.
- **D-2 dead row:** at death commit, retain the final post-growth DBH, height, crown and age, plus source-supported cause/mechanism and ecological deathYear. No later growth, age increment, repeat death or resurrection. A hypothetical age-30 initial tree dying during the second step retains age 32 with deathYear 2; this is **not** a claim any actual Year2 tree died. Pre-inventory dead history never invents cause or death year.
- **D-3 horizon:** ten one-year sequential no-intervention steps (Year0→1→…→10) are a **verification horizon only**, not a game lifespan cap, fast-forward mechanic, or Irish forest forecast. No ten-year jump pretending to replace ten commits.
- **D-4 interventions:** **none** in this implementation slice. No marks, thinning, felling, recruitment, planting, approved or executed Work Plans, persistence, save/load, Host or gameplay marking integration. Future work changes living populations only through a separately approved executed Work Plan boundary, not mere marks/proposals.

**Not adopted by the owner's four-item agreement:** the public runner/API command name, error-code literals, state lineage/historical digest/codec, cross-process resume semantics, future persistence, calibration coefficients, Year2–10 biological goldens or arbitrary numeric semantics. Future technical details require a coherent spec and appropriate approval; the orchestrator may propose safe private interfaces but must not invent approved public contracts.
