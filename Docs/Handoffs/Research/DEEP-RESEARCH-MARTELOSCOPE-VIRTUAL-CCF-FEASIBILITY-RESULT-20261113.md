The most important lesson: a marteloscope is **not a precomputed future forest**. Integrate I+ primarily scores and visualises an *immediate virtual felling exercise* using a current spatial inventory. The separate martelosim/SAMSARA path adds model-generated future trajectories, which require their own validation.

The reported scientific/educational value is a **hybrid**: reconstruct authentic plot inventories, check the accuracy and provenance of immediate calculations, compare player choices to non-unique human marking decisions, and then develop guarded synthetic stands and separate species/site growth models.

## 3. Calculations: current observation, deterministic marking, forecasts and actual outcomes

| Output | Class | Input/method and limits | Reproducibility verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tree ID, x/y, species, current DBH/height, status, TreMs | **Measured current inventory** | Physical measurements/recording at dated field visit; DBH sampling inclusion criteria are dataset dependent (Integrate inventory reported \`>7.5 cm\`). | Check source units, coordinates, date, duplicates and measurement errors; public source fields do not imply crown shape or future conditions. |
| Basal area (BA) | **Immediate deterministic calculation** | Sum \`π × (DBH_cm / 200)^2\` per stem; area must be correctly defined for BA/ha and masking; felling marks select removal/retention sets. | **Best first exact regression benchmark** from tree DBH and consistent plot area. |
| Standing/removed timber volume | **Immediate method-assisted calculation** | Original species/taper/height methods and local stem quality; may require species-specific factors. | Exact published equation/version necessary; matching generic number with a different model is not proof. |
| Current timber financial value | **Immediate estimate** | Quality sections and lengths, taper volumes, period-/market-specific prices; Integrate records visual quality classification per stem. | Reproducible only if section data, price year and original formula are available; not an actual future sale price. |
| TreM/habitat points | **Current-state scoring index** | Observed tree-related microhabitats weighted by configured type, size, rarity/development terms. | Needs full scoring rules/version, not a direct biodiversity population count or long-term ecological outcome. |
| Fell/retain summary | **Deterministic outcome of current virtual marks** | Filter tree IDs by user/forester mark set; report counts, current BA, volume, value and habitat scores; Integrate exports exercise results in CSV/JSON. | Good match for CCF Work Plan preview and order provenance. **No tree has been physically felled by the virtual exercise.** |
| Competition index | **Current spatial model** | Depends on coordinates, species, neighbour radius/crowns and particular declared index. | Can benchmark but must **not silently substitute** I+ parameters for CCF's existing authoritative \`CompetitionShare\`. |
| Instantaneous openings after virtual removal | **Computed geometry** | Retained/removed trees with positions and crown extents; otherwise assumed crown geometry. | A projected gap map, **not measured future canopy**; quantify uncertainty due to missing crowns. |
| Annual/dynamic growth, ingrowth, mortality, regeneration, future light/carbon/economy | **Forecast** | SAMSARA2/martelosim with declared model/site/species parameters and future treatment strategy. | Demonstrates a forecast *workflow*; never field validation by itself or proof of Irish species/domain applicability. |
| Alpine rockfall/snow protection | **Site-specific forecast** | SAMSARA/MOSAIC-protection modelling. | Not a justified generic Irish windthrow/climate risk. |
| Same-stem future DBH/height/survival after real felling | **Observed longitudinal outcome only if remeasured** | Requires real baseline → real intervention → same-tree measurements for following years with site and treatment provenance. | **No named publicly downloadable paired longitudinal dataset was verified** in this investigation. Marteloscope remeasurement capability ≠ actual accessible time series. |

The report identifies **martelosim's** architecture as particularly relevant: it converts current workbook plus recorded field marks into SAMSARA2 input for repeated management simulations and produces dendrometry, regeneration, light, harvest, carbon, biodiversity, protection and financial forecasts. The documentation explicitly cautions that model parameters calibrated over other plots do not guarantee plot-specific quantitative predictions.

The report also cites British marking-agreement research: **36 experiments at 12 sites**, with only slight-to-fair overall agreement in tree selection in that 2018 study. A forester's stem list is therefore **an instructive comparison, not an unambiguous correct-answer key**. This matters when evaluating any CCF automated proposal against human decisions.

### How real marking decisions could help the deferred OPEN-3 logic

The report supports an inspectable, conditional transfer model, **NOT** a universal transfer coefficient:

1. **Local decision patterns**, using only genuinely observable source-stem and competitor state: species, DBH, local dominance and distance, crown relation, habitat role where actually recorded. Treat human examples as diverse choices, not unique truth.
2. **Residual-stand conditions**: per-actual-treated-area retained BA, residual diameter distribution, local Crop Tree competitor release, true canopy opening geometry and recorded TreM/habitat retention.
3. **Cautious intensity references**: a lower release quantile may be tested, but is explicitly **gameplay/model-derived, NOT established forestry practice**; no lower percentile, minimum-positive-example count or local cap is approved by the report.
4. **Refusal/noncomparability**: under materially different species mix, site/development, density, diameter, current competition or habitat constraints, show **Manual/NotCovered** rather than copy mark counts or invent quality.
5. **Account honestly**: share a felled competitor's benefit among multiple Crop Trees if appropriate, but count the physical stem/BA/work order exactly once; include existing manual/open work and local finite polygons, and don't hide an intense local opening behind a safe five-rack mean.

The earlier source-first-execute/N+1 transfer contract is **superseded by the latest user direction** for the *future large-stand feature*: the player previews editable source and target proposals, may manually override each rack, commits through one Work Plan, and all approved rack work executes in **one management year**. Until actual execution, the source's “after treatment” values are **predictions of immediate tree removal**, not observed stand development. Proposed system halos, physically painted player choices, approved work and historical execution must remain distinct.

For a smaller first scenario, the entire large-stand extrapolation engine may remain deferred. **OPEN-3 is still unresolved**, not silently approved by this report.

### Meaningful evaluation metrics and their limitations

- Agreement/precision/recall against a named forester's marking decisions: comparison only; low agreement does not prove poor forestry, high agreement does not prove optimal forestry.
- Real treated/masked residual BA per hectare: useful overcut measure but different species/site/age conditions constrain portability.
- DBH distribution: structural summary; can hide spatial clusters and crown pattern.
- Crop Tree competition shares/release: local management outcome, dependent on the declared model and denominator.
- Retained TreM/habitat points: protects recorded structures; *not* a biodiversity population forecast.
- Canopy opening/gap geometry: useful preview but sensitive to unmeasured crown dimensions.
- Removal/retention BA/volume and deduplicated IDs: strong deterministic test for CCF planning and Work Plan bookkeeping.
- Explicit justified **no-release** cases: survey decisions must not be converted into fictitious mandatory removals or a misleading zero-intensity transfer target.

| Step | Required action/evidence | PASS | STOP / FAIL |

Minimum import schema: \`tree_id, species, x, y, dbh, inventory_year, alive/status, plot_boundary, plot_area\`. Height strongly desirable; crown base/shape, TreMs, quality, terrain only if **actually documented**. Reconstruct tree coordinates at an unambiguous map origin and units. Crown canopies require independently declared shape assumptions, not invented measured diameters.

**Rights- and field-completeness alternative:** If Prélenfrey's dataset rights remain ambiguous, start with a real **named, individually inspected** CC-BY Integrate/GBIF plot. **Further critical checks:** The martelosim guide notes some omitted fields may be populated by defaults, virtual saplings, or approximated Z. Inventory values, generated values and model-filled assumptions must be labelled separately. A reproduced BA or habitat score validates bookkeeping only, not multi-year ecological simulation.

### Rights and science NO-GO criteria

Stop a **shipped plot-derived** scene for unclear commercial/derivative redistribution, untraceable owner or missing coordinates/tree IDs/area. Stop the **specific reproducibility claim** for hidden model variables, non-reproducible source scoring and missing version. Stop the **scientific transfer claim** if plot species/stage are noncomparable, marking examples too narrow, or expert disagreement treated as ground truth. Stop **long-term validation claims** if no same-stem real remeasurement. Stop any modification which silently breaks protected Enlarged80/Legacy40/frozen Reference Future or old saves.
