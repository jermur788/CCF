## 1. Research question and product motivation

The user asks whether **real, pre-existing marteloscopes** (field tree-marking teaching plots, sometimes accompanied by individual-tree maps and calculated economic/ecological scores) have enough accessible information to serve as an **authentic starting world and ground-truth/calculation benchmark** for the CCF game. Contrast that with the currently proposed **user-driven representative-rack decision transfer**: walk and mark the middle rack, interpret the player's reasons for Crop Tree choice and competitor removal, evaluate **predicted residual-stand condition** (basal area, diameter distribution, competition, spatial canopy openings and only genuine modelled risks), extrapolate plausible **proposals** to other comparable racks, then let the player accept or manually refine each rack. All accepted work executes in **one management year**.

The larger idea is a **generalised virtual marteloscope**: a walkable, explorable forest in which actual candidate trees can be marked and decisions reviewed, but where species mix, stand layout, stand size, terrain and climate can be varied *within evidenced and computationally supportable limits*, and where longer-term consequences are generated by a properly scoped simulation rather than assumed to have been measured in a static field plot.

**Do not research just the definition/history of marteloscopes.** Determine precisely what data and methods can be reused, what is reproducible, what is proprietary or missing, and what it would take to use one dataset as a calibrated baseline before generating meaningful variations.

### B. What is actually calculated or observed
- Inventory **marteloscope exercise software, published algorithms, scoring and reports**: timber volume/value/assortments, economic and habitat/biodiversity-value indices, retained/felled basal area, residual stem structure, competition indices, tree IDs and marking patterns, map views, instructor/participant comparison, instantaneous canopy gap/volume estimates.
