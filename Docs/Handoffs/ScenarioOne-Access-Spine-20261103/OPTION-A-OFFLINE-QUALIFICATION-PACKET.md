## Specific offline questions

1. **Additive derivation validity.** Propose deterministic family grouping for two mirrored orientations; test input-order invariance, near-collinear/overlap cases, wraparound, transitive chaining (members A–B and B–C near tolerance while A–C outside it), duplicate ids, near-zero lengths and invalid/edge cases. Give exact provenance of any proposed angular tolerance; do **not** select one merely by copying the current 4° UI snap window.
2. **Single-owner spatial partition.** Recreate the #251 studied reference where naive side strips claim **1,575 m²** twice before ownership disambiguation. Specify partition/edge tie rules and test the conservation identities for every stem and unit area; ensure corridors are counted once, excluded masks win, remainders are conservative and no prescription or Fell work can be duplicated. Quantify sensitivity to ownership and coordinate resolution. Keep source path + JSON key for every published quantity.
3. **Sample and segment meaning.** Use one manually demonstrated 3D sample across sides as an **unapproved working hypothesis**; measure actual masked source and candidate unit metrics, evaluate comparable-area density/mean DBH/competition fit without changing accepted thresholds, and identify any orientation-dependent sample-transfer problems. Preserve `NotCovered`, `Excluded`, `Manual` and reasons; if another sample or new calibration is necessary, mark a product decision, never automate it.
4. **Reach and accessibility.** Examine the #251 reported **2,246 m²** beyond 9 m and **830 m²** beyond 20 m from owning rack, and whether using those cells as “rack-served” would be misleading. The **9 m teaching half-width is not automatically a reach threshold**. Analyze bounded alternatives, explainable cost/access factors and a conservative NotCovered/Manual fallback; do not adopt unbounded reach or an invented empirical Irish practice limit.
5. **Lifecycle safety, independently of Option A.** Design atomic validity checks at **Confirm and every authoritative mutation of an already-confirmed plan**, or an explicit reopen/reconfirm transition. Specify how a loaded previously-confirmed invalid plan must **load without loss of user data**, surface precise errors, suspend **new dependent MFM/work commitments**, and permit repair without rewriting completed locked rack work or histories. Cover save validation, rule-version mismatch, undo/edit, completed work and reopening. Do not simply reject old saves or bypass `Derive` red status.
6. **Save, hash, rule-version and prior reference compatibility.** No new field does not guarantee old hashes: `ScenarioReferenceArchive.CurrentWorldHash` hashes values, and `R{id}{L|C|H}` IDs may retain spelling while changing polygon meaning. Test or specify exact legacy branch selection, how rule/calibration text versions are chosen per derivation, load/round-trip hashes, previously approved prescriptions, pre-v23/v24 formats, Legacy40 and frozen Reference Future v1. No changes to protected archives.
7. **Decision gates for other systems.** Explicitly carry unresolved illegal spine entrances, Workbench yard conflict, R1/R2 overlap, turning and rendered/map/minimap consistency forward. Do not attempt to “solve” by creating new path planks, blanket tree removal or relabelling Track as Headland. A proposed route must not change MFM masks or world/save merely by being drawn.
8. **Proof method.** Provide reproducible offline scripts/property tests, fixed-claim adversarial cases, exact file/line source references and scope/accuracy limitations. State whether A is still the least-risk route after conservative reach and compatibility checks; allow **NEEDS CHANGES/NO-GO** instead of coercing pass. Review representative numerical output against raw JSON/CSV at publication.
