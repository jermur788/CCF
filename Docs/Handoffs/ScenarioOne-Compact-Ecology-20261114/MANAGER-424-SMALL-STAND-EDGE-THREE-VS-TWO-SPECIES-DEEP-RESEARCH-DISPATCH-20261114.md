## A. Binding user question and product context

The owner proposed the **same initial compact forest played twice**, first as a guided example showing defensible forestry judgement and then as an independent management replay with optional help. The forest is managed at multiple substantive **decision/reassessment opportunities** (not compulsory Fell events), each followed by authentic annual growth/review; the later years allow the player to see developing Oak/Beech and compare outcomes. A long-term comparison must not imply the guided run is the one correct answer. Years 0/5/10/20/30/40 are **research time points**, not approved intervention or final-completion requirements. The playable parcel might be approximately 20, 25, or 30 m, but no production dimensions are selected.

Now the owner asks us to **investigate**, before accepting the design:
1. Is an extremely small compact site a biologically credible place to demonstrate tree competition, thinning, light, seed dispersal, recruitment, survival and longer-term CCF development? Which boundary artefacts matter, and how might a compact **playable management area within a larger simulated surrounding forest** change them?
2. Can the present authoritative CCF model and art credibly show Sitka, Oak, and Beech at 20, 25, 30 or 40 simulation years under repeated non-compulsory management? What can/cannot be shown without new growth, vegetation, rendering or seed-neighbour behaviour?
3. **Would two species make the effects easier to observe, teach and simulate in such a small forest?** Compare **Sitka + sessile Oak** and **Sitka + Beech** separately with the existing **Sitka + Oak + Beech**. Do not assume the two broadleaf options are biologically interchangeable or that dropping one species solves edge effects. Evaluate if all three species could remain in the model yet be visually/teaching phased (another potential route). The premise remains a Sitka plantation transforming towards CCF; Oak + Beech without Sitka would not be a like-for-like option.
4. How would each option affect the **same-start guided versus independent replay and side-by-side/alternating comparison**: fair environmental conditions, independent saves/identities, distinguishability of management effects from random events, explanation of trade-offs, lesson clarity and real player workload?
5. What is the **smallest credible, evidence-backed next local/Unity qualification study** needed to close the remaining non-paper uncertainties, and what would cause us to recommend two-species or three-species first-demo scope?

**Authority boundary:** accepted [D-059](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Docs/Project/decision-log.md) requires comparable Sitka/Oak/Beech modelling depth before first external Scenario One demo; D-060 requires the **complete Scenario One loop**. **Matrix 2 — species:** compare **Sitka+Oak+Beech**, **Sitka+Oak**, **Sitka+Beech**, and a possible **three-species model with staged introduction/teaching focus**. Show three separate axes: *ecological credibility*, *pedagogical contrast/legibility*, and *production engineering/art/testing cost*. Do not conflate model simplification with clearer visual learning. Examine successional stories, differential shade response, seed-source realism, juvenile survival, growth stage/silviculture, space for distinct cohorts, browsing, planting and protection, retained adult Sitka, emergence of management trade-offs. Explain whether Sitka + Oak may generate stronger treatment/light contrast than Sitka + Beech, **if the evidence supports it**; distinguish actual model capabilities from external biology. **Matrix 3 — time and outcome:** years **0, 5, 10, 20, 25, 30, 40** under three observation/reassessment opportunities without compelled felling. Research whether data-backed height/DBH/crown/competition, oak/beech age-stage changes, planted/seeded cohorts, mortality, stock and annual review provenance can be represented legibly. Distinguish **source engine theoretical capability**, **executed native evidence** (only through Year 9 in #460), **external empirical expectation**, **3D asset/LOD gap**, and **unknown without controlled qualification**. **Matrix 4 — paired comparison:** identical initial forest and **identical per-run starting genetic/seed/model data** for guided vs player *within each species version*, independent saved histories, recorded treatment choices, consistent environmental forcings to isolate management effects where technically and scientifically honest. Assess RNG/event-stream divergence, storm exposure and game-data provenance; the aim is a trustworthy **educational comparison**, not a proof of causal optimality or "beat the expert." Without actual user trials, teaching clarity is **hypothesis** not observed effect.
