## 2. Why this arrangement, and its most important risk

It offers a clear player sequence **arrive → park → enter woodland on foot → inspect → plant/manage trees → review outcomes → collect timber legitimately if management generates it**. A winding path can help navigation and make the two broadleaf groups findable without consuming 72m² on the earlier 2.4m ×30m straight rack. The entrance can serve as a teaching landmark and seasonal staging point. None of this is a measured Unity UX improvement yet.

**Truck size is the major spatial risk.** At **30×30m = 900m²**, a clearing large enough for full-sized articulated lorry approach, secure stack/load, manoeuvre, turnaround and separate car parking could consume a large part of the training woodland. Do not claim a full-size timber lorry can enter and turn. Teagasc [harvesting access guidance](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/advice/timber-harvesting/harvesting-and-selling-timber-from-conifer-forests/prepare-for-harvesting-operations/) requires adequate entrance, loading, turn and exit; its [forest road guidance](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/advice/management/construction-of-forest-roads/) warns against public-road loading and notes a boundary bellmouth can suffice on some smaller sites. [Coillte haulage site safety rules](https://www.coillte.ie/driverportal/docs/Coillte-Code-of-Practice-for-Timber-Removals_2023.pdf) require turning areas free of parked cars while loading. This user-approved *scene concept* must be qualified against real roadside position/rights and vehicle/surface/safety, **not substituted by a nice-looking clearing**.

**Recommended working interpretation:** a *shared-use* entrance clearing at different times, with a suitable site-specific collection vehicle, parking during ordinary visits and closure of the relevant zone during timber work. A smaller truck is a **possible future vehicle choice**, not yet owner-approved as equivalent to an actual articulated timber haulier. If the proposed full-size truck cannot fit, present options to the owner: different permitted collection method/vehicle, enlarged/edge-adjoining yard if land rights allow, a larger training plot, or simplified timber-handling abstraction. Never silently scale safety away.

**Starting-tree provenance risk:** #460's original **189 Sitka** are pre-layout raw geometry; the research's **170 post-rack** inventory is also not the new proposed opening/path world. If some source stem positions fall inside the authored clearing/path, they need an explicit starting-layout omission or historic-work account (subject to approved game/save policy) — not a fraudulent player Year1 Fell and not a claim all 189 remain.
