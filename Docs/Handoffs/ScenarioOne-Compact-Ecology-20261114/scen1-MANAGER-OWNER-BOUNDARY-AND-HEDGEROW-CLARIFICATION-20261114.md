## What this clarifies

1. **Do not assume a small finite forest is an artificial or ecologically wrong setting.** An actual bounded stand with open fields/roads outside may naturally be edge dominated. In that case #484's finite Hegyi competition index is **correct for the declared surrounding-tree absence**, rather than a flaw requiring correction. The hypothesis that extra outside Sitka stems would increase CI is conditional and should not be imposed on open landscapes.
2. **Do not confuse a hedgerow seed source with a full surrounding conifer stand.** A native hedgerow can potentially supply or mediate some woody species recruitment, depending on actual donor species, reproductive maturity, spatial reach, browsing and site; there is **no measured donor inventory** for the scratch plot. Current `ForestEcologyController.ComputeSpeciesSeedRain` iterates living trees represented in the actual model, not automatically external hedgerows. Representing a hedge visually is not proof of seed input. An externally parameterised, *source-cited* donor mechanism, if ever necessary, would need design/approval and negative controls. Do not invent outside Oak or Beech reproduction or guarantee either will colonise without planting.
3. **Choose the simpler credible implementation** for first-demo research: a compact **separately bounded woodland with a clearly described real-world boundary condition** is the provisional preference because the current source has a bounded `ForestEcologyController.StandBounds`, and `StandGeometryModel` only knows preserved Legacy40/Enlarged80. The #460 20/30 m configurations were temporary **research overrides**, not a supported save geometry. Adding a genuine forest surrounding a smaller player-managed polygon needs new participation and management-eligibility, cell/geometry, economics, maps, save identity and protected reference rules. Given the inspected code, **the larger-forest implementation is not demonstrably easier**; no engineering runtime-cost comparison has been performed. If later evidence shows an unexpectedly simpler source-safe larger-world route, it remains eligible after Manager/owner decision.
4. **Do not automatically commission a buffered-neighbour Tier 2 Unity study.** #484 already resolved the arithmetic conditional: with outside trees, CI near the edge can differ; without them, there is no missing-neighbour effect. No live choice of outside landscape warrants pretending external trees are present.
5. **Keep the actual main next uncertainty separate:** whether the existing three-species annual model and visuals deliver believable and understandable Sitka/Oak/Beech development through elapsed years ~30–40 and different defensible management choices. The first qualification should use a truthful **finite bounded scratch world**, choose one authentic #460 source layout (30m preferred for witness candidate, still **not selected for product**), run genuine annual ticks and produce objective biological and 3D evidence. Do not overtest every plot×mixture×context combination, and do not authorize future gameplay save/profile or a new cadence controller.
