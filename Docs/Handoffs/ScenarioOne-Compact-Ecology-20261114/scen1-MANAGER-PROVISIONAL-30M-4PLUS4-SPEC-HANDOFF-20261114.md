## 1. Latest owner intent and correction to my initial choice

The owner delegated a reversible “pick something now, record your reasons, create a spec; split browser and T3 Code.” The owner had already accepted:
- New small training woodland starts **all Sitka spruce** (no initial Oak/Beech of any age).
- First lesson is **player-led early Oak AND Beech planting**, protection/care and later real forestry decisions; natural Oak/Beech recruitment by elapsed Year40 is not mandatory.
- A scripted second planting/replanting lesson is NOT currently required; owner will decide during personal player test if it adds learning.

**Initial Manager thought:** a 30×30m all-Sitka forest, one 2.4m central rack and eight individually planted early broadleaves (4 Oak +4 Beech), split into two mixed pockets. Original #460 research had made that rack tractable.

**Owner challenged the access premise at 17:00 UTC:** “Does there need to be an extraction rack running through such a small stand?” **My corrected provisional choice is NO mandatory central rack.** The Teagasc [small forest harvesting practices](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/advice/timber-harvesting/timber-harvesting-in-farm-forestry/) describe manual and small-scale extraction alternatives for suitable sites, while its [forest roads guidance](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/advice/management/construction-of-forest-roads/) emphasises safe access/loading; [small-site road guidance](https://teagasc.ie/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/CCWEP_energywood_guide-3.pdf) observes some small sites need only a suitable off-road entrance rather than internal roads. **No source says every 0.09ha site can extract timber from any boundary without a route or legal permission.** Boundary access/ownership/safety and a timber-handling method must be checked, not assumed.

The earlier rack would occupy **2.4×30=72m², 8% of this 900m² property** and in the #460 research removed 19 of 189 Sitka. It is disproportionate as a *compulsory tutorial step* before planting absent proof that it is actually needed. The normal forest may contain tightly stocked stems and require **some** legitimately marked selective opening work to make planting/light and field inspection meaningful; **“no rack” does not mean “no management felling”, “safe extraction guaranteed”, or “every one of eight saplings will survive.”**

The approved historical Enlarged80/five-rack Rack V2 and D-071 remain untouched; this corrected choice concerns the **new proposed compact teaching property only**.
