### Reproducible method

For each planting point Q and each consecutive segment from A to B: set v=B−A; t=max(0,min(1,dot(Q−A,v)/dot(v,v))); the closest point on that finite segment is P=A+t v. Calculate d(Q)=min over the seven segments of hypot(Q.x−P.x,Q.z−P.z). A straight symmetric physical path of width w has **centreline-to-path-edge clearance E=d−w/2**. Subtract a shelter outer radius r and independently an outward verge / display-exclusion radius v to obtain **H=d−w/2−r−v**. A nonnegative H is a *source-plane nonintersection condition only*. This assumes rounded/continuous corridor ends and no spline wandering, branches, widened junctions or terrain displacement.
