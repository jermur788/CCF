## Cancellation, replacement, recovery, continuity

1. A **Pending** G1 order that is explicitly cancelled must retain its historical audit entry but lose current group completion eligibility; cancellable status semantics require source-capability check. Replacing a failed/cancelled G1 order is allowed **only via the player's explicit new legitimate exact-position designation**, re-evaluated stock/space/cost. Replace affected `groupOrderIds` reference (at most four current designated IDs); retain cancelled/failed old work in existing ordinary history, no fabricated success.
2. A failed or partial first annual resolution does **not** fail the entire teaching record: preserve genuine successes and resume for replacement orders in later true years, then acknowledge the first covering year after **all four current designated** orders complete. No proof of surviving saplings is required beyond successful creation; subsequent browsing/mortality is a separate ecological lesson.
3. Save/reload during arrival, mid-assessment, after 1–3 Pending marks, after approval but before annual advance, after 1–3 completions, or after report opening but before acknowledgement must round-trip exactly **within Compact's own new save slot and preferences**, with no inferred credit. On load validate all work-order/juvenile/species/report links against authoritative retained source/history or refuse before world/Prefs/file mutation; no silent repair/copy/migration from historic O1 or O2.
4. **Guided navigation skip** may suppress tips or move the UI view, but may not manufacture arrival/assessment, place/purchase four stock items, resolve Work Plan, advance a year, or mark a review acknowledged. If owner decides skip can acknowledge a text introduction, that change needs a named accepted control/state transition and signed CG-TRIG revision; it is **not** default.
5. Subsequent genuine intervention(s) and no-work/Annual Reviews are **full scenario** history/completion matters under OPEN-C5; they never change this early teaching evidence or count as substitute `PlantJuvenile` rows. If user revisits a finished report, preserve first covering acknowledgement year without false duplicate events.
6. First G1 quartet includes four separate juvenile IDs, one current eligible teaching group, no borrowed other-profile credits. Invalid/missing explicit profile/world envelope or cross-slot backup never loads under signed isolation requirements.
