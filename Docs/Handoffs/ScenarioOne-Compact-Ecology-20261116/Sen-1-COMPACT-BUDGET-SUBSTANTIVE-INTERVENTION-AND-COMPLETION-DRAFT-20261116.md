## What “later substantive intervention” could mean — AUDITABLE PROPOSED classifier

The first four true `PlantJuvenile` orders and arrival lesson provide establishment history, **not** themselves enough for a *complete* multi-cycle demo. Subsequent mere tree inspection, opening the Work Plan, time advancement, no-work decision or token replant of one failed juvenile do **not** automatically satisfy “substantive management”. **Proposal for review** (not code-authorised, no fixed size/year/fell count):

1. **Distinct later intervention:** normal authoritative Work Plan episode **after** the initial G1 teaching resolution, with a different recorded work/order-year group, actual approval and executed work on real standing-tree or regeneration-care condition (e.g. justified competitor release, selective retention/felling, protection/ground-vegetation action where source supports it). Work must be a *nontrivial management response* connected to the observed earlier state; do not require a Fell mark, sale or universal thinning percentage.
2. **Resolved outcome exists:** at least one real `Completed` eligible management order with actual labour/material costs and an auditable state transition (tree status/removal, crown/competition/light, protection, or established vegetation/regeneration state as supported). A `Failed`, cancelled, draft, merely proposed MFM selection, or pure Work Plan approval does not count. Distinguish **actions** from consequential ecological changes that may be delayed or absent.
3. **Review and reason:** a subsequent genuine Annual Review presents the intervention, costs, status and supported ecology/provenance; player explicitly inspects/acknowledges it under reviewed UI semantics. Another meaningful choice follows (could be intentional **no-work** documented by a real advance + review), demonstrating adaptive management rather than a single deterministic recipe.
4. **Bounded completion:** reach a comprehensible owner-approved completion state **after** initial planting, a later substantive episode, a documented later review/no-work choice and observation of all three represented species through the correct management loop. Do **not** invent a mandatory three interventions, a 3-year elapsed threshold, basal-area/canopy quota, profits, four trees surviving or Oak/Beech natural seedlings by Year40. Actual objective wording and allowed evidence classes must be ratified first.
5. **Identity and persistence:** use authoritative unique event/order/juvenile IDs and year-linked settled history (annual harvest grouping label `harvestJobId` may not uniquely identify a job); do not count the four G1 planting events again as later substantive work or reuse old historical two-cycle/rack teaching latch. No new schema field is authorised in this draft; if durable completion state needs storage, separately review and sign save changes.

**Owner decision still OPEN:** whether one later substantive intervention plus an assessed later no-work/response is sufficient, or whether two different later interventions are mandatory. Alternatives must be assessed against existing natural behaviour, cash and pacing; do not silently turn teaching quality into a numeric felling quota. A player may choose no work repeatedly; that is a legitimate management decision and review, not fabricated “Completed work”.
