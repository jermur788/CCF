## 2. Outcomes and non-goals

Goal: a single player can start an **actual independent Compact profile**, walk the existing arrival and footpath, assess current Sitka, personally retain three promising Crop Trees, source four owned planting stock, designate each at a legitimate G1 position, make and approve a priced Work Plan, resolve honest annual planting and ecology, read the real Annual Review, inspect and adapt to later outcomes, execute substantive later forestry work, make a subsequent meaningful act-or-no-work judgement, and reach a legible, durable completion state *only when its evidence is true*. Each year remains an authoritative simulation year. Progress depends on judgement and evidence, not a prescribed successful canopy, guaranteed seedling survival, a timber profit target or a fixed chronological quota.

Non-goals: internal Compact racks, five-rack first lesson, MFM sample extrapolation, orange rack marks, a machine corridor along the footpath, a physical manual-felling minigame, invented exterior seed donors, hard-coded broadleaf Irish density rates, new grants/subsidies, automatic new Oak/Beech recruits, a mandated second planting group, fixed three-Fell or three-cycle quota, optional C6 crown opening overlay, optional C10 rewind/replay comparison, speculative new art rights or any physical new-world signature from this draft.

## 4. Proposed player state machine and gates (Compact only)

**C0 — entry, current-world preflight:** menu New/Continue and all direct loads choose a profile before any world/scene/Prefs mutation. If unbound/unsigned/unsupported content is offered, Compact is not enabled and no fallback into old Scenario One occurs. E1 environment inspection and E2 full gameplay qualification are distinct.

**C1 — arrive:** show the real H1 location, player within H1 facing the footpath, active world identity and usable interaction. The player explicitly acknowledges arrival; mere spawn, proximity, menu dismissal or return from another profile is insufficient. Follow #677 signed arrival signal; existing controls must be source-inspected, and a new UI control must be reviewed if required.

**C2 — assess and retain:** the player inspects a **current-world/year stand assessment** of living Sitka, growth/stock/competition and available money; explicit affirmative acknowledgement after actual inspection is required by the signed technical teaching policy. Independently, the player marks and retains **three individually selected Crop Trees** (blue, mutually exclusive with red Fell) as an authored scenario-management requirement, with reasons/crop release inspection, not an automatic T1 save-envelope conjunct. Merely opening a map or a cached report does not prove assessment.

**C3 — purchase/designate G1:** four legitimate owned nursery plants and sufficient eligible work method are available; choose 2 sessile Oak + 2 European Beech at the four exact signed coordinates, no drag/nearest-site substitution, no duplicate/mismatch/occupied stem/path/H1/edge violation. Make **four distinct Pending PlantJuvenile orders**, each with real stock reservation, editable actor/optional protection/cost, and group binding. CIP-016 individual per-tree choice remains mandatory. Marking an order does **not** create a juvenile or debit a fictitious grant.

**C4 — Work Plan commitment:** quote costs/time/stock/eligibility and let player choose contractor or landowner simulation; show Pending, Approved, Completed, Failed and Cancelled distinctly. **Only explicit Work Plan approval commits work.** A player may correct Pending before approval with appropriate re-quote. **Approved orders are immutable at every UI/service/bulk setter**: user-confirmed cancellation removes from active execution and is audited; any changed treatment requires a fresh new Pending order, price and explicit new approval. Never hidden Approved→Pending demotion.

**C5 — normal annual resolution:** every real calendar-year advance resolves ordinary Approved orders and updates cash, actual material/owner-time/contractor costs, timber and accounting, adult/juvenile light/competition/growth, browsing/understorey/protection, mortality/deadwood and snapshots exactly once in the existing authoritative year order. Four Completed actual order outcomes atomically link each order→created juvenile ID/species→real completion/report year; Failed/cancelled/partial work cannot write a positive planting claim. No guarantee any planted broadleaf survives later years.

**C6 — covering Annual Review:** Y* is the latest *actual resolution/report year* for the four designated successful planting rows. The existing **single Annual Review acknowledgement control** is the covering control. Only after the real Y* report has been opened/read and real 2+2 work is complete may that existing action atomically write its one-per-year event 9 **and** Compact reviewAcknowledgedYear=Y*. CT-3 blocks **all** further annual advance and skip routes once final 2+2 CT-1 is true and CT-2 false, including after save/load. Viewing/dismissing report, earlier-year event, backdate, synthetic event, field-only write or second ack button is not credit. Later genuine mortality does not erase successful work history.

**C7 — ongoing adaptive years:** T1 early teaching completion is *not full scenario completion*. The player walks the changed site, uses read-only S1-C historic annual/intervention review and provenance, checks new canopy/juveniles/costs, decides later work or justified no-work and repeats. The approved S1-D concept continues, but **Compact cannot import five-rack first-sample/MFM teaching rules**, old v24 guided latch or historical two-cycle credit. A meaningful later stand-level retention/release/treatment has an actual Approved→Completed work outcome linked to its site and an examined subsequent annual consequence. A later explicit reassessment and act-or-no-work choice is reviewed. See §7 for unresolved exact classifier.

**C8 — completion / post-completion:** only a reviewed, accepted **Compact-specific durable predicate** may complete once, show reasons and distinct observed/derived/calibrated claims, persist without duplicate credit, and permit legitimate subsequent observation/management. Exact M-A-SCOPE numerical/spatial qualification and the durable storage amendment remain **UNSIGNED**; before their approval the game must not silently use the old rack/two-year predicate or award Compact completion.

## 5. Finance, work and forestry realism

Use **D-084 F-A €12,000 finite balance**; no automatic grant, subsidy, free labour, instant credit for H1 omitted trees or guaranteed positive profit. On every order distinguish **estimate/quote**, explicit approval, **actual settlement**, failed/cancelled status, stock debit/refund and genuine retained timber/deadwood/sale entries. Existing illustrative source game prices (2 Oak stock €11 +2 Beech €9; optional four shelters €20; four contractor planting tasks ~€30) are **research examples, not approved Compact tariff or Irish retail statistics**. Old €2,500 contractor harvest minimum is a legacy source behavior to inspect if applicable, not a new mandatory Compact planting charge or discount. If a chosen action is unaffordable or ineligible, visibly block/quote truthfully rather than silently subsidise or make the owner cost-free. Work Plan remains the source of commitment; the footpath is not a lorry route. Staged small-truck collection uses H1 only when visitors/cars are excluded; no public road loading or actual professional vehicular certification claimed.

Use accepted Sitka/Oak/Beech species-aware mechanisms at distinct evidence depth; source-driven light, juvenile browse and bramble/bracken competition, natural regeneration and standing/deadwood, quality/timber and worker costs must be shown honestly. A no-work year can be a valid thoughtful choice, not a Completed intervention. Avoid making juvenile survival, broadleaf recruitment by Year40, money balance growth or mandatory thinning percentages automatic objectives.

## 6. Time, review, pacing and teaching parity

Normal advance is one year. D-077 optional skip to a *suggested management review year* is a bounded **navigation aid**, allowed **only after Compact T1 teaching complete** and no Pending/Approved work, unread mandatory review, active modal or reference preview. Suggested 4-year reassessment and maximum 5 real ecological years per invocation are **gameplay calibration**, not mandatory CCF intervention spacing. Execute every intervening actual annual tick, stop at the first storm, consequence, objective, Year25/100, lesson, mandatory report/decision or failure; no synthetic review acknowledgements. One-year advance must always remain possible for care/protection and earlier work. Distinguish: (a) direct N yearly advances with no acknowledgements → exact state/hash oracle; (b) ordinary UI per-year acknowledged route → *semantic* outcome parity after its genuine event differences, never require identical raw history hashes. Real S1-C annual history remains read-only for past years and credible provenance is retained.

## 7. Completion classifier / open policy

**Accepted semantic minimum (D-084 M-A):** later actual substantial, *spatially auditable* stand treatment or retention/release decision; valid Approved→Completed work; an observable reviewed later consequence; then a later inspected adaptive **act OR no-work** decision with real review. Three player-retained Crop Trees are a separate earlier learning choice, **not** a three-Fell quota. Initial 2+2 planting, optional shelters, pruning, H1 pre-world omission, isolated token Fell, no-work, unexecuted proposal and elapsed years do not **by themselves** substitute for later substantive treatment. Forest health/survival, finance/profit and natural Oak/Beech recruitment are not guaranteed completion conditions.

**Still unapproved / required from Manager-owner:** OD-16 M-A-SCOPE determines the precise smallest genuinely meaningful acted-on set and local-change threshold; exact spatial linkage/observation tolerance, what counts as sufficient work response, and any durable S1 save amendment all require separate accepted text and nonauthor review. This draft makes **no numeric qualifying tree/area/BA/time threshold**, no fixed three substantial cycles and no compulsory profit or continuous-sapling count. A provisional classifier may be implemented for *internal read-only diagnosis* only after specific approval; it is not live grading or an excuse to store unapproved fields. Manager must either approve a precise acceptance classifier or leave Compact completion gate blocked.
