## 2. OD-16: two implementable, falsifiable substantive-work classifiers

### 2.1 Shared candidate contract (applies to both A and B; PROPOSED)

**World and chronology.** Apply only to a future **signed and actually admitted** Compact profile/geometry. `T1=true` must be proved by signed §4.2/#677 evidence, not old guided-v24, two-cycle/rack history or legacy PlayerPrefs. Let **Y1** be T1's genuine covering review year. A fresh player act **after T1** must open/read the live current-world/year assessment and examine explicit standing tree identities/competition/retain-vs-remove choices. Record a prework observation snapshot `P0` tied to profile + signed world binding, ecological year, assessment interaction, stable tree IDs, affected cell IDs and current living area. Tree marks alone (red/blue) do not prove this read act. Neither initial H1 omission nor original 2+2 G1 orders are eligible later work.

**Eligible executed treatment.** Both options deliberately choose the narrow **existing `FellTree` work family**, NOT a sixth `ReleaseTree`, `RetainTree` or manual-physical order. The player may personally assess retained standing candidates (a blue `CropTree` mark is helpful but not compulsory for the M-A retained set); the operational release is achieved by **completed removal of identified actual competitors**. Proposed/sampled MFM candidates, red Fell marks alone, `Pending`, `Approved` without resolution, failed/cancelled orders or work in another year/profile never qualify. Verify original order→Approved Work Plan decision→successful Completed resolution→real per-order `WorkResolved`/settlement against distinct target IDs and one stand plan. Quote is compared with the actual **resolution-year** settlement; do not pretend approval, year advancement, quoted estimates and settled charges are the same event. Operator versus landowner is an existing execution choice; no preferred worker can bypass status, work expense or eligible tree checks. Felling a genuinely eligible living standing tree differs from tree mortality and from H1's pre-world omissions.

**Stable spatial basis.** The signed new world is 30 × 30 m, but **900 m² is only the outer geometry, not automatically the productive standing-area denominator**. Use the separately signed live stand/H1/path and ineligible/open masks to obtain eligible standing tree/cell area; clip each proposed footprint to that mask and the half-open world boundary. Confirm exact source cell size (draft says 36 cells/5 m); no assumed five-rack/MFM area, 40 m rounding factor, property-wide ratio, invented tree IDs or replacement cells. A footprint is stored/derivable as a deterministic ordered set of *actual eligible cell IDs* plus retained/felled stable tree IDs and original coordinates, not a loose label like "north area." Cross-world IDs are never valid simply because numerically equal.

**Work effect versus later consequence:** At work resolution **Yw**, verify `P0` against the factual pre-execution standing inventory and compute **removed basal area** from genuine removed tree observations (unit m² within the footprint). The denominator is `preWorkLiveBA` in that same footprint and same pre-work snapshot, >0, with actual source DBH for every included living tree; the numerator counts only qualifying actual removal targets once. If source lacks a lossless prework/postwork observation boundary, the future signed S1 contract must capture it rather than estimate from revenue or replay a guessed baseline. This direct removal effect is necessary but **not** a substitute for a *later* ecological consequence. After ≥1 normal real annual ecological tick beyond Yw, the player opens **Yr > Yw** Annual Review + same-world live observation and explicitly acknowledges the real report. Compare actual **post-resolution** local state with **later Yr** in the same tree/cell footprint; show a provenance-labelled difference (e.g. standing BA, model-derived light/canopy, focal target condition, live juvenile/cohort stage, recorded death, deadwood), including an adverse/unexpected effect. To avoid awarding pure floating-point jitter, candidate criterion is at least one persisted/countable event or a change across **two source display quantisation steps** of the field in consistent units; the exact fields/quantisation must be independently fixed and approved before coding. If no source-provable change exists, show "no detected later change" and **remain incomplete** until another real observation. Never assert treatment *caused* all post-treatment growth or mortality: one can report an observed difference without exclusive causal attribution. This numerical detection test is a **GAMEPLAY CALIBRATION PROPOSAL**.

**Subsequent adaptation:** Only **after** reviewing Yr, player inspects another current real stand assessment and deliberately selects **ACT** or **JUSTIFIED NO WORK**. For ACT, the proposed work must be genuinely player-authored and follow the ordinary Work Plan; an accepted `FellTree`, `PlantJuvenile`, `RemoveRegeneration`, `PruneTree` or `SalvageDeadwood` may demonstrate *adaptive follow-on care* if eligible/actually Completed and subsequently reviewed (it is not automatically a second substantive treatment). It must show relevance to actual current evidence, not just profit. For NO WORK, the player must inspect the relevant live condition, explicitly choose a reason tied to it (e.g. "release sufficient—monitor" or "regeneration still under observation"), have **no Pending/Approved operation awaiting that year's execution**, advance a genuine quiet year and open/acknowledge its Annual Review. A no-work click without such observations/reason/review is no evidence. Year of final adaptive review **Ya > Yr**; a same-year replay, automatic skip acknowledgement or historical review-open-only is not a separate decision. Valid normal care in intervening years remains allowed; no work lock or universal absolute spacing rule. D-074's multi-year coaching remains player-facing, while D-077's 4-year navigation suggestion/5-tick press limit is never a treatment test.

### 2.2 OD16-A — local neighbourhood/retained-tree release (RECOMMENDED PROPOSAL)

Define `retainedJudgementIds` as **at least two distinct** current living, standing, individually inspected and deliberately retained tree IDs in `P0`. They need not be the total-three blue CropTrees from M-4 and need no synthetic "retain work order"; their judgement is recorded as **assessment evidence** only if a future accepted interface can prove the player's action. At least two different **living, eligible** source `FellTree` competitor targets must be within **5.0 m planar distance of at least one retained candidate** at `P0`, and their removal neighbourhoods must be spatially connected through the 5 m-radius disks around the retained candidates. Define patch `Apatch = standingEligibleMask ∩ union(disks of radius 5.0 m centred on those retained candidates)`. It must be one connected patch, eligible area **≥25 m²**, with all source cell/tree edges clipped. `preWorkLiveBA>0`; actual distinct Completed `FellTree` targets counted once must remove **at least 8% of the patch's prework live BA**; both assessed retained targets remain standing through resolution. The two work targets need not be three marked trees, and the player need not fell three. **All 2, 5.0 m, 25 m², 8% are new calibration candidates.**

A legal, spatially important **single very large stem** may nonetheless fail the proposed two-competitor minimum. This is a deliberate teaching/robustness tradeoff for owner choice, not proof that one-tree CCF work is ecologically insignificant; a future owner could select a one-stem alternative **only with additional outcome proof**, not by silently weakening A. Negative: 1 token Fell or 2 Fell outside the retained target neighbourhood => **`INSUFFICIENT_CONNECTED_EFFECT`** even if a sale is profitable; 2 nearby low-BA Fell below 8% => same. Multiple marks without Approved→Completed => `WORK_NOT_COMPLETED`. A 9% operation followed only by current-year viewing, without later changed state and reviewed adaptation, still fails.

**Why A:** aligns the player's deliberate retention/release judgement with observed local ecological consequences and the project's physical-forest-first loop. It does **not** force all three advisory blue CropTrees to survive until the final year. **Risks:** more assessment evidence; precise neighbourhood/eligibility geometry and snapshot provenance; target loss after resolution needs truthful, nonrevoking history. Signed evidence schema likely needed.

### 2.3 OD16-B — bounded two-cell stand treatment (ALTERNATIVE PROPOSAL)

At `P0`, player inspects a stand treatment spanning **at least two edge-adjacent, real 5 m ecology cells**, with **≥50 m² eligible standing land** in the connected cell union; count partial boundary cells by **actual eligible area**, not nominal 25 m² when clipped. Inspect at least **two distinct living/standing retained survivor tree IDs** somewhere within those cells (not necessarily marked CropTrees or equally spaced); remove **at least two distinct valid living FellTree targets** from those cells in one genuine Approved→Completed work plan; `actualRemovedBA / preWorkLiveBA >= 0.06` for the selected eligible patch, same year and identity definitions as above. Only the tree/cell set that was **actually assessed and approved** is eligible; no after-the-fact enlargement to lower or raise the denominator. **All two-cell, 50 m², two-target and 6% values are proposed gameplay calibration, not Irish forestry criteria.**

This option is easier to display as an actual bounded stand/map cell set and easier to verify from cell records; it may admit operations that are not as clearly a local competitor release around specific promising trees. It must still prove two distinct retained judgements, real work, local removal effect, later reviewed source-backed change and later adaptive review. Negative: a tiny Fell across one cell, clipping that leaves under 50 m² eligible, discontiguous cells or a selected patch quietly enlarged *after* work => **fail**; two nontrivial Fell in qualifying cells may pass when A fails its 5 m target link.

### 2.4 Why other shortcuts are rejected now

Neither option adopts a **mandatory three full thinning cycles**, **three Fell order quota**, 3-year enforced work spacing, automatic crop replacement, a fixed profitable balance, a guaranteed surviving 2+2 or natural broadleaf recruitment. D-077's navigation-only FellTree year is not a M-A treatment. A raw sum of felled stems / 900 m², old area integer scaling (900/1600 rounding to 1) or changed total cash is not local meaningful-work evidence. One valid `PlantJuvenile`, shelter, prune or `RemoveRegeneration` can be important **care**, yet in both A/B it cannot **by itself** be the later *substantive* episode because doing so would collapse initial teaching/maintenance into completion; a later ACT choice may use genuine care after treatment. A sixth "retention" work type would be an unrelated unapproved source feature. No internal racks or mandatory MFM sample areas exist in new Compact.
