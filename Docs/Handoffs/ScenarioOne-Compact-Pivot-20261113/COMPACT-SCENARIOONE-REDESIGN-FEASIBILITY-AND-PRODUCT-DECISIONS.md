## 1. Executive verdict

**Recommend Option A, subject to explicit product approval:** make a separately identified, compact, physically traversable *complete-loop* first Scenario One world with **one genuine access rack as the starting design hypothesis**, while retaining the present five-rack 80 m world and the historical 40 m world as protected, distinguishable products/fixtures. Keep the authoritative tree/ecology/management simulation and the Sitka spruce, Oak and Beech modelling depth. Make normal direct marking and inspection of the *entire practical management area* the task; do not pretend that the one-rack world demonstrates multi-rack extrapolation. Treat the latter as a separately specified later **large-stand** capability, not as a blocked prerequisite for the compact player demo.

**Why:** the current World 80 m source has 1,344 initially standing Sitka trees and a five-rack Objective 1, while the MFM derivation is explicitly five-rack-only and its Objective 2 candidate review, persistence and teaching create significant coupled work. A smaller one-rack world can reduce the player's demanded search/marking territory and defer that coupling. Yet it must still teach live spatial judgement, Work Plan commitment, actual annual growth/regeneration/browsing, money, history, genuinely spaced later interventions and an understandable completion. Removing multi-rack automation does **not** permit removing any of those systems.

**Important qualification:** Option A is the best *product-fit and isolation* hypothesis, not the cheapest code change. It needs an approved independent world/slot identity, compatible scenario-specific objectives, one-rack plan and lesson state, tested ecology/financial calibration and a fresh rendered qualification. Option B is the lowest-change technical fallback if world/slot cost defeats the compact vision. **Do not implement an in-place replacement of the historical world by reusing its saved geometry identity.**

## 3. Exact implementation coupling found by repository reading

1. **World geometry and generation.** [\`StandGeometryModel.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/StandGeometryModel.cs) currently recognises only \`Legacy40\` and \`Enlarged80\`; \`StandGeometryPolicy.NewGameModel\` is \`Enlarged80\`. [\`ForestStartingStand.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/ForestStartingStand.cs) has a distinct larger-world branch, preserves 336 legacy IDs in the enlarged core, and generates 1,008 outer trees. It is deterministic but **not generic to arbitrary stand dimensions**. Any new compact identity requires a separately authored generation/fixture contract, not the claim that changing a number from 80 to 40 safely creates it.
2. **Save slot and scenario ID.** [\`ForestSaveController.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/ForestSaveController.cs) writes a single \`forest-save.json\` (with \`.tmp\` and \`.bak\`), and starts \`LoadData\` mutations only after \`FindLoadProblem\`. [\`ForestSaveData.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/ForestSaveData.cs) is **currently v24** and has a nested \`scenarioOne.scenarioId\` default \`scenario-one\`. The inspected \`FindLoadProblem\` checks geometry, species, ecology/record validity, but does **not** itself enforce an explicit intended new compact-world/save-slot identity. **D-081's per-scenario save slots and cross-world pre-mutation rejection are product policy, not verified shipped infrastructure.** New route should be an explicit mode/identity and isolated file path, with legacy \`forest-save.json\` neither redirected nor overwritten; reject a mismatched scenario/world ID *before changing live world or disk*. No version number is selected in this report.
3. **MFM and rack count.** [\`ScenarioOneRackSampleGeometry.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/ScenarioOne/ScenarioOneRackSampleGeometry.cs) states \`TeachingRackCount = 5\`, positions sample on *third* confirmed rack, derives half-distance strip coverage and refuses inputs not containing five racks. [MFM V1 approved spec](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/specs/multiscale-forestry-management-v1/spec.md) preserves three Crop Trees in a 24 m teaching envelope and requires prescription/coverage after Lesson 5. A compact one-rack world must use its own **authored manual lesson path**, not call this model with an invented four racks, automatically copy its three-tree credit, or repurpose one unit as all five.
4. **Work commitment.** [\`ScenarioOneManager.PrescriptionApproval.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/ScenarioOne/ScenarioOneManager.PrescriptionApproval.cs) explicitly keeps proposal state read-only and distinct from ordinary pending orders, and separates prescription approval from Work Plan approval. The compact path can simply bypass the prescription pipeline while retaining *existing* manual marking, pending orders, Work Plan review, annual resolution and event history. Do not create Fell marks from predicted/proposed cuts.
5. **Map/visual state.** [\`StandMapSource.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/UI/Map/StandMapSource.cs) reads a single current tree/rack/mark source for full map and minimap. [\`StandMapCanopyRaster.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/UI/Map/StandMapCanopyRaster.cs) renders live crowns; [\`StandMapReviewPlan.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/UI/Map/StandMapReviewPlan.cs) explicitly distinguishes spacing-only candidate squares and prescription proposal triangles. A **new projected-opening view is not proved implemented** by these files. Any compact preview must be read-only, labelled as conditional *if the proposed work executes*, and never presented as a finished annual ecological forecast without validated model parity.
6. **Completion is not geometry-independent.** [\`ScenarioOneDefinition.cs\`](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Assets/ForestPrototype/ScenarioOne/ScenarioOneDefinition.cs) derives extensive targets using the accepted 40/80-area factor, retaining an unscaled 25-year completion horizon, 100-year review, ~€2,500 annual commissioned-harvest minimum and €12,000 starting cash. Smaller harvest volume can be materially less solvent at an unchanged commissioned minimum. Neither raw counts, 25-year completion, staged interventions nor financial targets may be naively scaled or waved through.

## 4. Proposed compact-first concept (for product review, not production specification)

**Standing area:** one authored, physically legible private Irish Sitka plantation parcel. A **single extraction rack** connects the existing legitimate arrival/service access to a practical band of trees on its sides. *The rack is a machine access corridor, not the area a forester manages.* The player surveys and marks trees in the whole deliberately small usable parcel, including suitable trees away from the rack centreline, without walking five separate corridor systems. The stand remains bounded and visitable.

**Size:** **do not select exact metres, count or corridor geometry yet.** The verified 40 m / 336-tree historical implementation is a useful lower-area **comparison fixture**, not the new compact world and not proof that individually inspecting 336 trees is quick. The 80 m / 1,344-tree example is the verified larger reference. Choose an independently identified compact footprint only after a read-only count/route/target study demonstrates (i) meaningful retained-tree and competitor choices, (ii) one functional rack with physically sensible servicing, (iii) no loss of local competition/recruitment representation from severe edge effects, and (iv) a tractable inspection/marking session measured with the target UI. **No invented compact size, workload minutes, density treatment rate or meaningful success threshold.** Keep the initial identity/species and age context coherent: existing age-20 Sitka starting stock is a model precedent; Oak/Beech planted juveniles and later age/size changes must be genuinely simulated. Any pre-authored broadleaf adults or mixed age cohorts alter the story/site/product identity and need explicit acceptance.

**One-rack teaching and Work Plan sequence (new-world only):**

1. Player arrives, sees the actual property bounds and access, reads concise first objective and orients in 3D/map/minimap. Show an existing service route as *existing*, a potential extraction rack as *proposed*, and a confirmed rack as *approved geometry*; do not fabricate disconnected roads. Choose whether player draws/confirms the one rack or an already-authorized rack is supplied—an **open product choice**.
2. If planning is retained, one 3D entrance tree is physically painted at the confirmed rack entrance; pending clearance orders are still only pending. The objective counts **one actual rack**, not \`1 of 5\` or old fixed Rack 1 ID, and normal Work Plan approval is mandatory before clearance. No deleted/executed rack geometry is revived.
3. The entire manageable standing parcel is available for **direct tree-by-tree inspection**. The player marks selected Crop Trees, competitor Fell choices and explicit habitat/retention decisions where existing game state supports them. Red Fell and Blue Crop marks remain exclusive, reversible before execution; retained trees can remain deliberately unmarked with explanatory inspection/plan context. Marking a tree is not harvesting it; no hidden bulk Crop Tree quality prediction.
4. Read-only map/minimap present **actual player marks**, current crowns and status. A separate clearly labelled optional *conditional opening preview* shows where current **pending/approved proposed removals** might create a near-term canopy opening **if** executed. Do not call that a tested “next-year ecosystem forecast” unless a sandbox/replay using the actual annual model and independent parity verification exists. Never mix authored marks, prospective opening, MFM suggestions, executed work or past recorded state. If no extrapolation runs, **no fake proposal halos**.
5. The normal Work Plan shows proposed removal volume, plausible settlement/cost, residual basal area, canopy and legitimate protection/planting/pruning options, plus contractor versus landowner simulation, consent and expected financial effect. These are model-derived estimates with source/provenance labels; they are not identical to executed results. Player confirms and executes through the existing authoritative annual resolution rather than executing by painting or map click.
6. Walk the changed 3D stand. Actual rack corridor/gaps, retained trees, deadwood, planted Oak/Beech, understorey and regeneration react from the existing ecological state; then advance the complete one-year engine and review measured/result events, current observations and conditional/modelled comparison with correct D-073 history provenance.
7. Continue through genuine no-work observation years and later **spaced** silvicultural decisions. *Advance 1 Year* remains universally available; *Skip to Next Management Review Year* is subject to D-075/D-077's eventual approved/verified eligibility and **loops each real annual step**, stopping for work, unread reviews, achievements, storms or user decision. Do not imply a time skip is already shipped or hide pending work. Distinguish necessary intervening planting/protection/pruning from substantive thinning for D-074 periodic teaching.
8. On second and later cycles, resurvey the **changed same parcel**, revisit retained Crop Trees, competition, recruitment, browsing, economic result and habitat, mark/plan/review again. Show first intervention versus current state versus proposed next work in S1-C/S1-D without invented causation. Give a clear **Scenario One completion** after appropriately qualified multiyear management and species/ecology evidence; permit continued forest observation/management where current policy allows.

**Completeness test:** a tester can make and revise an initial human judgement, approve/resolve actual work, see growth/regeneration/finance consequences, return years later, make a materially different later decision and reach genuine completion **without** touching an unmodelled second/third/fourth/fifth rack. If the reduced world cannot sustain that, **STOP** rather than weakening the loop.

### Why species parity survives reduction

- **Sitka:** still the original working crop with adult growth/competition, felling, stocking, mortality, seed supply, canopy effects and timber economics. Protected pure-Sitka arithmetic must remain exact on protected old worlds; recalibrated compact results require a *new* acceptance witness rather than reusing old hashes.
- **Oak and Beech:** preserve species-appropriate ordinal/transfer-limited adult/juvenile growth, browsing, establishment, site/light response and timber rules; planting is at exact player-selected ground sites with persistent juveniles and history. No shortcut makes a newly planted broadleaf instantaneously commercially mature. The compact scenario must contain opportunities for **meaningful distinct early-life and long-term responses** within its horizon, not merely unlock planting icons.
- **Scale-dependent ecology:** small-world boundaries can change light, seed dispersal neighbourhood, mortality and local density; canopy, seedbed, substrate, deer/browsing/vegetation and regeneration outcomes should remain based on state, not hard-coded narrative. If the compact forest is so thin that every stem is effectively an edge, redesign geometry or represent the surrounding influence honestly before approving it.
- **Source evidence:** the Irish growth and CCF research supports continuing management and regeneration bottlenecks, but no source makes this proposed one-rack geometry an empirical Irish prescription. Specific size, species mixture, corridor and completion thresholds remain authored **GAMEPLAY CALIBRATION / PRODUCT DESIGN**.
