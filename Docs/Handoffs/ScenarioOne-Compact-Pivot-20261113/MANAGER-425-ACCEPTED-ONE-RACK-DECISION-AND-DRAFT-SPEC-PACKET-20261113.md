## 1. Accepted product scope and supersessions

### Accepted for compact first Scenario One
- **Compact physically navigable standing forest**, one extraction rack with plausible machine access. A rack is the cleared corridor; the actual player management area is the surrounding standing forest on both sides, within a deliberately manageable stand boundary.
- **Direct manual tree decisions** throughout the compact area. Player can inspect all relevant trees, select Crop Trees, remove/undo/replace spray-painted player Crop/Fell marks **while planning**, choose habitat retention and other work where authoritative game features support them. No first-choice lock-in, no simulated automatic felling on a spray action.
- **Single ordinary Work Plan** and management year: pre-approval preview and costs; only approved work resolves with authoritative annual step, retaining honest predicted versus executed distinction.
- **Whole real forestry simulation**: competition, adult/juvenile Sitka spruce/Oak/Beech at comparable modelling depth, regeneration, browsing, mortality/habitat where modelled, wood products and economics, protection/planting, annual change and read-only history. No species reduced to decorative plants or unsupported empirical precision.
- **Multiple meaningful management cycles**: user may advance one year or eligible skip only through real yearly simulation with work/review stops; return years later, reassess changed trees, decide new intervention, see review/history, and reach intelligible full Scenario One completion. **D-060 S1-C and S1-D remain PRE-DEMO gates**.
- **Graphics and experience:** concentrated, scoped improvements in authentic tree species/shape/crowns, forest floor/understorey, lighting, visual mark legibility, minmap/main map and meaningful before/conditional-after canopy openings. Separate user-authored paint, draft/approved orders and historical actions. Do not promise a biologically validated next-year crown forecast from static marks.
- **New safe profile/save identity** for compact world. Existing **Enlarged80 five-rack**, **Legacy40**, frozen **Reference Future v1** and users' old saves remain distinguishable, loadable under preserved rules, and never overwritten or reinterpreted as the compact scenario. No old world hash rebaselining.
- **Later larger experience** may reuse existing five-rack world/technology and prior product research (middle reference, per-destination acceptance/manual overrides, a proposed halo and map gap preview, planned-once executed-in-one-year philosophy), but **OPEN-3 transfer mathematics and later multi-rack UX remain unresolved future spec questions**, not first-demo blockers.

### Superseded/deferred FOR FIRST COMPACT DEMO ONLY (not deleted)
- Historical Scenario One Enlarged80 **five-parallel-rack player lesson** under D-064/D-071/D-072 is not the compact one-rack curriculum. It remains controlling for old world and saves unless the user separately retires it.
- D-065's existing required first-demo sample→four-target MFM extension and D-076's later representative-rack transfers are **deferred for compact first demo**; the approved old-world MFM spec/source and tests must remain preserved. Compact must not fabricate four phantom racks or pass the old five-rack MFM test.
- [#439 Objective2](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/issues/439)'s earlier full-rack extrapolation details are preserved as **future large-world product research**, not today's new Objective 2 implementation requirements; [#448](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/issues/448) five-rack DRAFT is on HOLD pending supersession/reconciliation, not approved for production.
- The user has **not** authorised replacing the historical world in-place, deleting old code, overwriting saved progression, instant time advance, lowering species parity, eliminating history, arbitrary thinning quotas or a Windows build.
