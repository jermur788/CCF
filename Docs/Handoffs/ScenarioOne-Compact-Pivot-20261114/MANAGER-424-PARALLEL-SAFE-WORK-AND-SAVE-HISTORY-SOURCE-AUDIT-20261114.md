2. **Recorded history is a useful comparison foundation, not a 3D rewind system.** `ScenarioManagementHistory.cs:6–34` is a read-only year-view query layer over persisted management events, annual reports, ecological snapshots, work orders, deadwood and storm records. `ScenarioOneManager.History.cs:4–20` exposes `Interventions` and `HistoryYears`; it makes no new save field. `ForestSaveData.cs:60–68` stores those records. `ScenarioEcologicalSnapshot.cs:20–45` records stand-level living trees, BA per hectare, mean DBH, canopy/light/ground vegetation/deadwood, plus species aggregates (living trees, BA, regenerated cells and planted juvenile counts). These records support **historical numerical/year-level comparison** when two legitimate independent histories exist. They are **not per-year complete tree positions, individual crowns, full ecology cells and visual states** for arbitrary historical 3D years; any "switch to both Year 5 forests" 3D comparison needs explicit snapshot, safe replay or candidate architecture and qualification. Current-world `ForestSaveController.CaptureData` is a whole current-state capture, not a historical series.

The accepted D-073 fact layers and read-only status remain required.
4. **Same initial seed does not yet prove environmental pairing.** `SimulationRandom.cs:1–41` derives random generators and rolls from model, simulation seed, year, salt and/or ID; it deliberately maintains legacy model 0 and newer model 1. This gives a useful deterministic control surface, but a paired environmental schedule and actual management-induced divergence have **not** been audited across storms, recruitment, seed and call order. Do not claim both runs receive identical exogenous forcing or that their differences are causal proof until a focused design/study establishes it.
