## 2. Honest 0.60m transplant silhouette: Oak versus Beech

**Both are slender planted stock, not miniaturised mature trees.** The scene should express one ground-rooted upright terminal leader and only modest attached laterals. The exact branch count, leaf count, crown width, root-collar diameter and age vary with nursery propagation, stock grade, genetics and season; these are **UNKNOWN** at 0.60m and should not be invented as species measurements. **A `1+1` stock code**, if supplied for a specific nursery lot, describes nursery production history; the game source height **alone does not establish that code**, and a plug/cell-grown or bare-root transplant can share nominal height. No exposed floating fibrous roots above the planted soil plane: Teagasc S1 says the collar marks planting depth.

| Character | Sessile Oak `Quercus petraea` | European Beech `Fagus sylvatica` | Confidence and art interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leader | A thin upright, somewhat irregular primary stem; do not thicken into mature oak bark | A fine upright leading shoot with slight zig-zag twig turns possible | **VISUAL INFERENCE** from juvenile form and S1 straight-stock guidance. Equal **0.60m** outer height in the drawing is a game input, not a field statistic. |
| Leaf | Distinct smooth rounded shallow lobes; nonzero petiole; alternate attachment, with open gaps between leaves | Oval/elliptic, pointed tip, gently undulating rim, short petiole, alternate; pinnate vein optional at close LOD0 | **BOTANICAL** S2–S5. Outer outlines matter; vein/hair shader detail is secondary and can disappear at distance. |
| Bud | Small clustered rounded terminal buds; do not use acorns as stock identity cue | Narrow projecting spear/spindle buds; more recognisable when dormant/leafless | **BOTANICAL** S3/S4; actual bud size relative to juvenile twig **not scaled from adult specimen descriptions**. |
| Laterals | Sparse laterals extending unevenly, with leaves physically attached to ends/nodes | Shorter delicate twig sprays/alternating side shoots, some leaves hanging more obliquely | **VISUAL INFERENCE**, not a universal growth law or forced branch count. |
| Leaf-off view | More ambiguous: clustered terminal buds and branching help | Pointed projecting buds, possible retained copper dead leaves | **BOTANICAL + LIMITATION** S3–S5. Colour-only or leaf-only species ID is not guaranteed; truthful Inspect text remains necessary. |
| Collar/stock | Stem enters ground at root collar, no large stump flare or trunk base | Same; no smooth grey adult-trunk trunk exaggeration | **IRISH GUIDANCE** S1 and **ART TUNING**. Root systems exist underground and may differ by nursery production method; no above-ground root flare assumptions. |

**Where this is weaker than a field observation:** We did not obtain measured destructive sample morphometry for Irish 60cm `Q. petraea`/`F. sylvatica` in particular nursery production codes; there is no justified invariant number of leaves, bud dimensions or branch angles. A/B SVGs intentionally avoid displaying fictitious measured plant width. The leafy season view is an illustrative state: dormant winter stock could be leafless or beech could retain dead leaves; **not** a new seasonal game state machine.

**Walking-distance review (not pixel measurement):** At **2m and 3m**, check individual leader continuity and lobed versus oval leaf cutouts even in grey; at **4m and 6m**, assess whether the plant can be found without inventing a colour-only beacon, and whether the existing genuine Inspect species label can resolve ambiguity. Compare at **1280×720 and 1920×1080**. These are required **future native render witnesses**, not test results from this browser.
