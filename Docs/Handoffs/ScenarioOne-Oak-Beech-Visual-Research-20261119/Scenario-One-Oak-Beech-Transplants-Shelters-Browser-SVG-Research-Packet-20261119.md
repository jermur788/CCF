## 3. Research questions (browser/web only, evidence labels mandatory)

**A. Recognisable 0.60m nursery transplants.** What are credible young **sessile Oak** leader, leaf, bud, petiole, lateral-branch, shoot and root-collar proportions, and what visually distinguishes a comparable **European Beech** at the same height? Identify which species cues are actually reliable in small juveniles; if leaves alone cannot guarantee species ID, rely on existing truthful inspect labels rather than inventing unmistakable morphology. Differentiate live seedlings, saplings, bare-root transplants and container stock without asserting an age from height. Compare cases at nominal player eye distance 2m, 3m, 4m, 6m; SVG diagrams are **illustrative**, not simulated engine perspective or measured pixels. Keep both visibly 0.60m, not shrunken mature 2m tree prefabs. Avoid excessive leaf density, floppy invalid branch topology or LOD2 detached leaves.

**B. Real-world protection appearance versus existing game protection logic.** Research how newly planted broadleaves are visually protected in Irish forestry and close UK/European analogues (individual tubes, guards, stakes, slots/ventilation, translucency/opacity, ageing, breakage). Make clear which claims reflect actual practice and which are visual design alternatives. Existing game shelter states **absent/effective/expired/failed** are authoritative renderer categories, not a claim that any 1.20m real tube alone is deer-proof or that damage effects/annual probabilities have been measured. No rewriting browse efficacy, tube lifetime, stocking, costs, collar size or year-of-planting logic.

**C. Readable state and accessibility.** Can a player tell juvenile species + intact/aged/broken/no guard apart when walking the real woodland, rather than relying only on hue? Compare the already proposed **matte olive slotted A** and **translucent B** looks; discuss risks of hidden foliage, Unity transparency sorting, wire-frame/alpha shimmering, legibility at 720p vs 1080p and no colour-only meanings. Recommend **one conservative visual preference for review**, but label it PROPOSAL; owner still decides. Any 4–6m inspect cue is a separate proposed UX/design choice, not permission to add billboard flags or change reach distances.

**D. Source/rights and implementation integration.** Prefer authoritative Irish sources first (DAFM Forestry and Forest Service guidance, Teagasc forestry/establishment, Irish native planting stock standards/credible botany) and primary botanical treatments; use Forest Research/Forestry Commission, reputable nurseries and peer-reviewed continental sources only as clearly marked TRANSFER. Record URL, title, publisher, publication/access date where available and exactly what visual observation it supports. Nursery/product photographs are **references only**; do not embed, trace, download/rehost, reproduce copyrighted textures or assert that they are licensed game assets. Distinguish **DIRECT IRISH, GUIDANCE, UK/CONTINENTAL TRANSFER, VISUAL INFERENCE/ART TUNING, UNKNOWN**. Do not manufacture numeric planting mortality, guards' deer effectiveness, biological growth, age or standard stock morphology.
