## Rulings on the independent objections

**1 — One school / no invented controversy: ACCEPT.** All six share a co-author, so there are meaningful shared intellectual assumptions, not six independent validations. Do not run a generic 'contested research' procedure without a named competing claim. The most concrete, narrower disagreement to record as context is **how much competitor selection can appropriately be delegated after manager-selected future crop trees**. P1 contemplates trained operators selecting competitors; existing UK transformation guidance warns that machine-cab visibility can make competent crown assessment difficult. Neither necessitates a CCF gameplay change because MFM already distinguishes candidates, player review, proposals and approval. The photographed Irish 'marking standard' pages 84–87 have unresolved containing-publication metadata; their title is evidence of the excerpt, not proof of an official universal national mandate.

**2 — Wind: ACCEPT AS DOCUMENTED MODEL LIMITATION.** P1 contains two-direction wind/stability cautions. New Scenario One games use storm model 0, so **neither removing large neighbours nor leaving dense patches has simulated storm damage consequences** in the default experience. Do not frame crown release as risk-free or invariably high-risk. This limitation should be visible in internal release evidence. Whether a short caveat needs to accompany beginner/external testing **without changing in-game lessons** is reserved for the product owner; no StormModel1 activation or speculative wind warning is authorised.

**3 — Hegyi Share / 'one right tree': ACCEPT WITH CLARIFICATION.** The current Lesson 3 teaching target and MFM ranking are protected calibration, not professional consensus or exact-tree-ID rules. A high-share neighbour is not necessarily *the* only defensible removal; retaining a neighbour for shelter, habitat, long-term species mix or wind concerns is a legitimate discussion, but the game does not currently record all those reasons as authoritative facts. Do not call alternate tree choices universally wrong. Do not change scoring before the demo. Post-demo options: retain 30% as a deliberately limited crown-release lesson, vary intensity with dominance, or allow recorded retention rationale—**all require separate approved product/spec choices**.

**4 — Timing/overlapping UI: ACCEPT.** Lesson/Annual Review/D-072/MFM and some 1280×720 presentation gates are not a safe place for unapproved copy edits. Phase 0 is documentation-only. No Unity work or new qualification chain for this intake.

**5 — Structural metrics: ACCEPT AS TEST HYPOTHESIS ONLY.** The claimed ~27% near-boundary percentage and low juvenile representation signal were not independently reproduced from saves. P3 metrics may have little signal in an 80m, mostly Sitka stand; boundary handling and cohort-stage representation are essential. A later, optional read-only fixture calculation can establish value. Do not create a biodiversity grade, unitless 'ecological success' score or new completion gate.

**6 — Uncertainty presentation: ACCEPT.** S1-C already separates recorded fact, current observation, derived comparison and explanatory teaching. P5's Bayesian uncertainty methods are not a validated Irish posterior and do not justify a probabilistic UI.

**7 — Agreement scale / attribution: RESOLVED BY REVIEW FOLLOW-UP.** P2 Table 3's classification uses a Stoyan-based scale, not necessarily Landis–Koch. The crop-tree-versus-competitor agreement claim is specifically P1; P2 is low versus crown thinning. The reviewer's original objections were withdrawn after primary spot checks. Record the corrected attribution, not the obsolete criticism.

**8 — Review effort: ACCEPT.** Use a **tiered** audit: source provenance and no-overclaim checks for pure explanatory copy; focused deterministic tests for offline algorithms; full spec/evidence/model calibration for production simulation or completion rule changes. Existing index already states marteloscope and other research-proposed features are not adopted.
