### 5. Failed-shelter discrepancy adjudication — candidate calculation CONFIRMED

**Source inspected directly:** `afd5484f:Assets/ForestPrototype/ScenarioProtectionVisuals.cs:79–95`. The cylinder is centred at planting `(x,z)`, scaled to height 1.2 m/radius 0.10 m, positioned at ground +0.6 m, then rotated 28° about Z and translated +0.25 X/−0.1 Y. Its local axial coordinate is centred: **u in [−0.6,+0.6]**, not [0,1.2]. Collider destruction is explicit at lines 81–83.

For the ideal continuous cylinder used by the document, let `c=cos(28°)`, `s=sin(28°)`. The plan projection of its side is:

```text
X = siteX + 0.25 + 0.10*c*cos(theta) - u*s
Z = siteZ        + 0.10*sin(theta)
u in [-0.6,+0.6], theta in [0,2*pi)
```

Changing the tilt sign replaces `-u*s` by `+u*s`. The substitution `u -> -u` maps the entire cylinder's plan projection onto itself. The +0.25 m translation is the same in both cases; vertical translation drops out of this plan-view model. **W:545's sign-invariance sentence is correct for this centred ideal-cylinder footprint.**

Two independently written methods were run: (1) both end-cap circles at 10,000 angles, for each sign; (2) all axial positions continuously, for each of 10,000 angles. Method (2) represents each fixed-angle axial side as a horizontal segment in plan view and computes exact segment-to-route-segment distance, including intersection and endpoint cases. Thus it checks the projected cylinder side between caps rather than assuming the closest point must be an extreme point. Both methods agree at every reported digit:
