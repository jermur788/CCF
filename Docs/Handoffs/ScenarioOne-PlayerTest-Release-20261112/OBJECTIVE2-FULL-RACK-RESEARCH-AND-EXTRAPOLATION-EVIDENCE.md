## Executive recommendation

Replace the initial Objective 2 three-tree teaching rectangle with a **complete representative rack-SERVED STANDING area**: the player inspects the whole reachable standing strip on both sides, selects every *appropriate* Crop Tree they judge worth retaining, and decides which true competitors need release. Appropriate does not mean every standing stem, a quota, or automatic removal. Then review **each of the other four rack-served areas** for demonstrable similarity and propose a derived treatment. No tree identity, exact count or geometry is copied to another rack. This is a **new first-cycle feature**, not merely D-076 later-cycle repetition. Separate player approval and ordinary Work Plan remain essential.

An extraction rack is a cleared machine ACCESS CORRIDOR. The rack-served area is the retained forest it can access. A tree in the cleared corridor is never a Crop Tree for the new objective.

**Recommended first implementable candidate:** keep D-065's individual 3D confirmation of all spacing-only Crop Tree candidates outside the sample, with efficient guided navigation, because no verified stem-quality classifier exists. A lower-click area-level proposal + mandatory destination spot checks is a valid UX *research alternative*, but automatic Crop Tree quality designation or release around uninspected assumed-good trees is **NOT APPROVED**. It needs a separate explicit user/#425 decision that changes D-065.

## 3. Full rack-served area: exact proposed calculation

Given five **persisted confirmed finite rack segments** R_i=(entrance E_i, far F_i, width w_i, id_i), sort across common normal n as RackSampleGeometry does. Interior lateral boundaries occur halfway between adjacent rack centreline offsets. Outer edges require an explicitly accepted half-gap/edge rule and separate leftover stand remainders; no automatic annexation.

Let S_i = bounded stand polygon clipped to rack i's midpoint neighbour-strip; L_i = finite longitudinal reach beside the real entrance-to-far segment with an *approved* turning/end allowance; C=union of actual rack corridors; H=headland/feeder/other access; I=sites/buildings/clearings; P=protected patches/juveniles; U=inaccessible ground. Working retained forest:

**W_i = (S_i intersection L_i) minus union(C,H,I,P,U)**; **A_i = area(W_i)/10,000 hectares**.

Each living adult at (x,z) belongs to at most one W_i. Corridor/mask wins over standing-strip assignment. Ties at shared limits deterministically resolve by stable rack ID. Clip against the actual stand boundary and finite rack ends, not infinite projected lines. A too-short, crossing, or nonparallel rack that cannot be safely partitioned returns **Manual/NotCovered**, not fabricated coverage. Record each masked reason and prevent double subtraction of overlapping footprints. Regeneration/juvenile presence may require localized exclusion, not automatic deletion of all treatment area; exact protection footprint comes from actual game state.

**Source measurements not performed:** published D-072 evidence independently witnessed **1,344 trees in the archived world**, but this docs-only task did not load per-tree coordinates/confirmed rack fixture. Actual per-rack area, reachable trees, Crop Tree count, predicted workload, timber volume, walking time and 4/4 comparability are **NOT MEASURED**. **Never divide 1,344 by five and call it a rack measurement.** Historical ~13/14 covered *subareas* in a single MFM-03 fixture is not evidence that four whole rack areas qualify.

**Offline fixture recipe:** pin exact current integrated candidate + archived 1,344-tree positions and living species/DBH/marks; confirmed rack IDs/segments/widths; all access, built, protected/juvenile and management masks; current year. Emit one row per rack: bruto square metres, net reachable m², Sitka adults, Oak/Beech adults, marked Crop Trees, mean DBH, basal area, Hegyi competition, eligible neighbour pairs, proposed releases, masked reason breakdown, outside remainder counts. Check conservation: living trees in 5 disjoint working areas + corridors/masks + outer remainder/inaccessible = total living world without double counting. Run all five possible sources and an intentionally bad end/edge/mixed-species fixture. Record deterministic output signature, year and fixture provenance. Re-run after annual growth. Do not replace missing measurements with guesses.

## 4. What the player should learn in the entire sample

**Inspect → choose → explain → review** across both sides and full finite length, using optional short map waypoints (e.g. entry/middle/end) as *coverage aids* only, not compulsory exactly-three Crop Tree locations. Judge: vigour/health; stem form and visible faults; crown development; actual neighbours/CompetitionShare; distribution among existing Crop Trees; ecological retained trees/juveniles; access and safety; whether any release is warranted. Player is free to leave a weak stem unselected, retain a bio-tree and designate zero Fell marks when a selected Crop Tree has no justified competitor. **“All appropriate” is a judgement/completion-of-survey requirement, not “mark all trees” or a precomputed answer key.**

Suggested progress states: Whole rack unvisited → reach survey ongoing (both sides, near/far) → eligible-tree decisions resolved → local competitor decisions resolved → sample work planned/executed → sample reviewed → target comparability/proposals reviewed. No inferred player action merely from camera proximity: inspect or explicitly resolve each *promising* tree/sector. Quality is not computed from DBH rank/spacing alone. Explain selected competitor's relative effect; the Pommerening 2021 result supports targeted competitor teaching.

## 5. Transfer algorithm and approval integrity (MODEL DERIVATION / proposed)

For each masked-net area j, density D_j=N_j/A_j stems/ha, mean DBH d_j, mean authoritative local competition C_j, species composition, BA B_j=sum_t pi(DBH_cm(t)/200)^2 m²; include manual marks/open work, browse/regeneration/protection, actual access. **Never invent unseen soil, terrain slope, exposure, windfirmness or tree quality variables**. Compare source and target **before** sample release or on explicitly authenticated comparable before/after bases:
delta_D=(D_j-D_s)/D_s; delta_d=(d_j-d_s)/d_s; delta_C=(C_j-C_s)/C_s. Handle zero/missing denominators as insufficient evidence. The old [MFM calibration](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/integration/scenario-one-c01-research-copy-candidate-20261112-v1/Assets/ForestPrototype/ScenarioOne/ScenarioOnePrescriptionCalibration.cs) uses ±20% density, ±15% DBH, ±20% competition, min 30 stems, 16m spacing-only suggestions and 0.1m area integration: **GAMEPLAY CALIBRATION ON ONE FIXTURE**, NOT production thresholds for a new full-rack source. Re-evaluate and version after new measured five-strip fixture.

Classify each destination **Covered / NotCovered / Excluded / Manual** with visible reason and whether data are sufficiently current. The current 15 subareas may be useful for detecting within-rack heterogeneity: a whole rack is not automatically Covered when only its centre matches. Surface mixed outcomes within each of the four destinations. Exclude non-Sitka quantitative treatment, safety/protected/juvenile conflicts and independently marked trees without silently treating Oak/Beech as Sitka. Failure returns no proposal, with manual/another sample choice.

From actual source Crop Trees K_s and their demonstrated competitor removals F_s, derive a *versioned treatment policy* (source executed release Share distribution, competitor ordering and removal BA envelope). Replay on source and show Player-only / Rule-only / Agreement stems and BA/Share difference; disagreement is **not a forestry correctness grade**. For each destination use **only player-confirmed Crop Trees K_j**, local modelled current neighbour contributions and current constraints to generate release proposals; never clone tree IDs, coordinates or Crop Tree count. Where no confirmed crop trees exist, proposed spacing locations are **inspection prompts**, not quality-certified stems, and cannot trigger their release work. Avoid duplicate competitor selection across multiple Crop Trees.

Protected first lesson values: 8m teaching spacing != 8m Hegyi neighbourhood; 30% local first release target and 40% BA safeguard are GAME MECHANICS, not universal silvicultural recommendations. Re-approve their meaning when many Crop Trees across the full source are considered. For any unit compute removed BA / same-state eligible unmasked baseline BA; include cumulative already-approved manual removals and a conservative separate all-living BA/canopy impact, and never exceed accepted local safeguards. Report aggregate projected 5-rack BA, canopy, volume, cost and work burden before approval. If one source did not demonstrate enough distinct decisions or real release, show **Insufficient evidence** rather than synthesise a strong prescription.

**Hard gates:** source work and fit (existing D-065 requires executed sample before rule); current-state comparison → proposed candidate locations → player individual 3D accept/reject for each under D-065 → proposed release (distinct state, never TreeMarkType.Fell) → at least one external 3D spot check → **prescription approval creates only Pending work** → **separate Work Plan approval commits** → execution → annual review of actual world. A future preliminary preview before source execution may be allowed if clearly advisory and not an executable prescription. Advancing or skipping time cannot approve work.

## 6. Two candidate beginner experiences and burden

**A. Authority-safe baseline (recommended now):** inspect all source standing forest, choose k appropriate Crop Trees and make f manual neighbour decisions, execute/review. Then inspect and accept/reject all m_i spacing-only candidates in each of four comparable racks, navigate efficiently via next/previous and map focus, compare fit/coverage, spot-check, approve prescription, separately approve Work Plan. Minimum human tree decisions **k + f + sum(m_i)**, plus coverage and two approvals. All k/f/m values and time are **NOT MEASURED**, and must be quantified in a real fixture and timed 720p/1080p first-time test. This preserves D-065 but risks repetition.

**B. Conditional streamlined design (NOT AUTHORISED):** user still completes whole rack; game shows four area previews and demands a small, explicit set q of 3D spot-checks with area-level acceptance. Decision burden would approach k+f+q+area approvals **only if** uninspected destination tree-level selection were allowed. Existing D-065 forbids bulk-quality confirmation, and there is no measured q or quality guarantee. Without a new accepted policy B can remain **advice/coverage only**, or apply release around *previously player-confirmed* external Crop Trees; it cannot automatically mark uninspected trees. Ask #425/user explicitly before any B implementation.

**UI:** five map rack-served shapes, not just corridors; source highlighted; four destinations with detailed subarea reasons and uncovered edges; in 3D, real tree Crop/Fell state distinct from candidate and proposal.
