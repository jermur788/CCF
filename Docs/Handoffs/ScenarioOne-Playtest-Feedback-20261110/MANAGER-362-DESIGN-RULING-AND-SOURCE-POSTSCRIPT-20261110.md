### Product-intent direction approved for specification

1. **P0 teaching-zone wayfinding — yes.** Keep the currently accepted three trees, one in each third, at least 8 m apart and from both rack sides **as the interim rule**; do not silently loosen a calibrated requirement on the basis of UX alone. Show the actual third confirmed rack's 24 m × 18 m derived teaching sample and Near/Middle/Far boundaries on Full Stand Map, with an explicit headland-facing near-end, side-of-rack cues and a walking-HUD live progress/checklist, a way to get there without guesswork and feedback for out-of-area/spacing/duplicate/extra picks. No highlighted prescribed 'correct tree', no new save fields and no decision taken away from player. Design against rotated rack layouts, grayscale, 720p and old-world/Reference guards. Scope minimap overlay explicitly if chosen below.
2. **P0 actionable guidance — yes, safely scoped.** Extend the existing single-next-action guidance beyond Objective1, using one **subtle, non-strobing** pulse on eligible non-commitment navigation; use an obvious persistent non-motion cue and reduced-motion control, and in the walked forest offer explicit E/C etc HUD action text when no button exists. **Commitment and potentially destructive controls never pulse** (Approve Work Plan, Confirm Rack Plan, Advance year, Fell), even if listed as next textual step; no focus hijack or skipping confirmations. A full sequence 1–8 plus Treatment extension should be spec-mapped; no automatic work planning.
3. **P1 Annual Review simplification — yes, requiring explicit S1-C FR-4 amendment.** Hide visible `Recorded/Current/Derived/Reconstructed/Session-only/Not recorded` provenance chips by default, provide an opt-in **Show data sources** disclosure; retain provenance metadata, year-specific historical truth and audit tests, and expose material uncertainty *in normal prose* (e.g., reconstructed/estimated or not recorded). Existing S1-C FR-4 currently requires derived/reconstructed values to be labelled; amend the approved requirement without silently deleting a guarantee. **Do not pretend 'Confirmed' was an Annual Review chip**; it may be Treatment review terminology, still to identify from the user's screen if important.
4. **P1 map/diagram clarity — yes, no topology change.** Visually and textually distinguish old foot/forest trail vs existing headland/feeder vs suggested/confirmed/physically entrance-marked/harvested racks, without `V2` terminology for players; group existing/planned/done, use shapes/hatching and grayscale-readable legends. Existing Objective1 figure should present actual five nonuniform suggested rack locations as *suggestions*, not falsely complete solid routes when 0/5 planned. The **HUD minimap existing headland and transient teaching-area overlay** are desired directions but require explicit D-072 minimap scope amendment and map parity/legibility review. No northern connector, false cleared-tree view, or Legacy40/frozen Reference leak.
5. **Existing headland origin — explain, do not pretend built by player.** Scenario One presently authors its headland and feeder as pre-existing, with no player felling or construction event. Plainly describe it as inherited infrastructure when the stand was taken over, without inventing a specific contractor, cost, construction year, permit or historical machine event. Consider old path restyling as a separate *presentation-only* proposal **only if** tree masks, planks/IDs, start, clearing, MFM exclusions, save and Reference remain byte-equivalent; inspect real Unity before approval.
6. **P1 clear Objective2 picture and current-step UI text — yes in design.** Explain the rack orientation, the teaching thirds and the 8 m / both-sides reasons, not just keys and opaque status. Reduce visual clutter without discarding important no-false-prescription caveats.
