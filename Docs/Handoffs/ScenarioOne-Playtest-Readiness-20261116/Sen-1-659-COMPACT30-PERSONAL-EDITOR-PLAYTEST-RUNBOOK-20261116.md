## What this personal test is for

Walk the **new separate 30 × 30 m / 0.09 ha initially all-Sitka woodland**. The southern H1 arrival/parking/collection area is pre-existing in the authored Year-0 world, roughly 12 × 10 m; it is not a harvest. Walk winding pedestrian-only **Route A** (not a truck extraction rack), understand the real standing Sitka stand, and perform the first genuine four-tree planting: **two sessile Oak and two European Beech**, from owned nursery stock through individual Pending planting orders, deliberate Work Plan approval and a real annual resolution. Read and acknowledge the annual report for the year in which the **last** of those four orders actually completed. Then reassess and carry out meaningful later forestry management, review consequences, and make a subsequent adaptive act-or-no-work decision. Cost, browse/protection, survival, history and uncertainty must be truthful.

**What is not required in Compact30:** five extraction racks, the Plan Racks editor, orange rack entrance spray marking, third-rack/three-Crop-Tree sampling, five-rack waypoint/minimap numbering, and rack-based MFM coverage. Those are **historical Enlarged80** workflows. Compact may use ordinary Stand Map and current-world forestry inspection; absence of a five-rack lesson is expected, not a defect. Enlarged80, Legacy40, old v1–v24 saves and frozen Reference Future v1 remain protected **regression targets**, never deleted or migrated to make this test convenient.

**Owner choices already accepted:** D-083 (world concept) and D-084 (Geometry A; teaching T1; finite F-A; later-management M-A; Approved Work Plan rule W-A; optional C6/C10 deferred). Do not reopen these choices. The detailed source/manifest and M-A evidence threshold remain gated.

## 1. First visible launch and orientation — 5–10 minutes if working

1. On the isolated launcher, choose **Compact30** in the neutral no-world profile picker and **New**; verify this is a new independent profile, not an Enlarged80/Legacy40/Reference continuation. On-screen forest must be visibly recognizable, Game view not black, with usable keyboard/mouse focus, a complete initial introduction and a meaningful next step. Record screenshot **C30-01-Boot** and Console state. A blank/black view, wrong scene, existing old rack/corridor/planks, wrong profile or unexpected saved game is **STOP before any save**.
2. Play the introduction without the operator giving away the next button. Test the actual displayed navigation: Right/Left/Enter/Esc, Tab/Shift-Tab and pointer actions **only as appropriate to the built candidate**; do not assume old 3-page text or arbitrary shortcuts. Check that advancing cannot activate destructive commands and that focus remains visible. Capture **C30-02-Intro** at 720p and 1080p, using normal and Reduce motion modes as time permits.
3. Ask: “What should I do next?” The first Objectives/current-step control should be discoverable; a restrained cue should stop when the step changes. If a gentle pulse exists, Reduce motion must provide a static cue instead. Check headings/tabs and that any more-detail folding does not displace a necessary diagram or steal scroll position. Record confusing wording verbatim rather than assuming old E80 copy persists.

**STOP** for persistent black Game view, unsafe focus/click-through, missing usable onward action, wrong world/profile/scene or unexpected user-save access. For minor hierarchy/copy defects continue with screenshots and a P2 note if gameplay remains unambiguous.
