## 3. Research questions — answer in decision order

### R1. Which causal pathways are credible, uncertain, or unsupported?
Build a compact matrix for **management action → immediate change → multi-year ecological effect → player-visible cue / Annual Review**. Include doing nothing, cautious thinning/crop-tree retention, planting Oak/Beech, shelter vs unsheltered juvenile exposure, selective understorey works and later adaptation. For each, identify the necessary causal link, whether **the current code appears to represent it** (source-visible), whether a native study observed the effect (exact scope), and how strongly external literature supports its direction/magnitude.

### R2. Do the combined effects preserve sensible trade-offs?
Challenge simplistic “more planting is always good”, “thinning always improves everything”, or “max timber revenue is best”. Evaluate plausible outcomes for Sitka retention and density/mortality, broadleaf juvenile light vs browse, canopy opening vs windthrow risk, plant survival vs management expense, habitat/deadwood vs extraction. Name cases where **do nothing** or delayed intervention could rationally outperform immediate action. Do not prescribe a uniquely correct forestry choice.

### R3. Are 30×30 m and multi-decade teaching claims framed honestly?
Explicitly distinguish a finite 0.09ha authored woodland from a representative managed large stand. Examine boundary/edge, density/population scaling, planting numbers, self-thinning, what year-20/40 mixed-species forest can and cannot establish without donors. Name **external donor / bramble / wind / protection** dependencies where relevant but do not assume new ecological features. No new small-stand shape/buffer experiment. Assess whether staged teaching language would overpromise natural broadleaf recruitment, instant resilient CCF, or mature economics.

### R4. Which costs/results are meaningful at tiny scale?
Research Irish continuous-cover practice and **economics as explanatory trade-offs**, separating actual current game quote/cash mechanics from real-world data. Identify non-scalable minimum contractor call-out, protection and planting costs, small harvest logistics, retained timber value/deadwood and owner-labour/time caveats. Do not invent retail quotes, grant eligibility or calibrate current € amounts from unstated assumptions. Flag a misleading claim if specific; don't design a new price system.

### R5. What are the **smallest high-value calibration and player-truth checks**?
After evidence review, propose **at most 3–5 sharply bounded experiments or UI-interpretation checks** tied to one concrete plausible failure and a metric/decision threshold. Example categories: normal-enabled-manager deadwood event witness, contrasting work decisions across two actual saved start states, understorey/light response to planting, protective shelter/browse counterfactual, Year+1 Annual Review explanation against engine state. These are suggestions **NOT native execution permission**. Do not require every listed example. Protect original baseline, deterministic seeds and save identities.
