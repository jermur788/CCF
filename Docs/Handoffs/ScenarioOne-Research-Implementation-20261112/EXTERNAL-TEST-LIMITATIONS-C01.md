**Status:** Authoritative *limitation disclosure* for external testing and Manager review, **not** a claim of completed external-demo or Windows-build readiness. **Source lineage:** Research-Informed Management Learning V1, independent source-approved C-01, final source `a314d308315b5894263d30461a1e8b52ca0f3605`. Document published with [Manager disposition](MANAGER-C01-SOURCE-ACCEPTANCE-INTEGRATION-GATE.md).

**The default Scenario One does not simulate wind damage.**

Keeping a neighbour for possible canopy cover, habitat, seed supply or stability can be a legitimate *management intention*. Current Share only measures local DBH/distance contribution; it does **not itself quantify species, height, crown architecture, shade tolerance, forest habitat, microclimate or mechanical wind stability.** The existing 30% Share reduction and 40% basal-area safeguard are explicitly **gameplay calibration**, not a universal Irish thinning prescription.

The game’s current young-growth indicator counts represented places with modelled natural young growth and/or living planted juveniles. It is **not** a count of successfully recruited future crop trees or a guarantee of survival. General canopy/gap-edge/open-ground descriptions in Help do **not** identify or model local site classes or measurable wind shelter.

This note should accompany any future external test instructions after the new UI candidate is integrated and qualified. It does not extend that release gate, certify ecology, authorise wind mechanics or imply the user has completed personal playtesting.
