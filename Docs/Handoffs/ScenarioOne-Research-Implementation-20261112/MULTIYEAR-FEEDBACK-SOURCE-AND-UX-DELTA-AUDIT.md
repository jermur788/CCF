## Verdict

**A real gap remains, but it is chiefly a *connection and timing* gap, not a missing S1-C history engine.** Recommend **Option B: one concise selected-year-to-latest-state review connection** within existing Annual Review. The player should see recorded work in the selected year, outcome snapshot that year, number of subsequent years, what the *latest recorded* forest shows, and a cautious “return and inspect before deciding” prompt. Record different years/source labels and never state that one intervention **caused** later conditions.

**Critical new cadence observation:** The current two-cycle completion check accepts successful work in **any two distinct years**, including consecutive years, provided an intervening review acknowledgment is recorded. It is not specifically a two-stand-thinning-with-multi-year-gap rule. The historical intervention aggregator likewise counts any successful resolved task as an intervention, including planting/protection. D-074 requires a *separate* policy for major stand interventions versus maintenance and any reviewed elapsed period. D-075 requires a distinct batch-annual-step feature (not part of this read-only audit or copy change). See [four-file draft](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/tree/docs/scenario-one-research-followon-roadmap-20261112-v1/specs/scenario-one-management-cadence-and-advance-v1).

## Meaning of natural versus planted young growth

[ScenarioEcologicalSnapshot.cs](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/feature/scenario-one-research-copy-v1-20261112/Assets/ForestPrototype/ScenarioOne/ScenarioEcologicalSnapshot.cs#L5) records per-species `regenerationCells`, `regenerationDensity`, `plantedRegenerationCells` and `plantedJuveniles`, plus stand-level `occupiedRegenerationCells`, mean cover/light and trees. These are **annual aggregate state**, not a traced cohort history at each physical location. [ForestSaveData.cs](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/feature/scenario-one-research-copy-v1-20261112/Assets/ForestPrototype/ForestSaveData.cs#L215) also stores specific planted juveniles and current regeneration-cohort records, with establishment years in model 1+, but those *current* records should not be used to reconstruct a missing earlier population.

Possible honest presentation:
- “Areas with modelled young growth: X in Year A → Y in Year B” (number of represented occupied map squares/locations, not stems/ha or hectares).
- “Planted juveniles currently recorded: N” or per-year per-species count **only where the matching saved snapshot actually records it**; be careful with promoted/removed individuals and model version.
- “Year not recorded” on older saves without the right snapshot/field; empty objective achievement history is not proof nothing was attained.

Do **not** claim seedling establishment success, a specific seedling surviving over years, precise persistent spatial locations or proof intervention caused regrowth from aggregate changes. The source read supports **aggregate-level comparisons**, not a new survival trajectory at specific trees.

## Player-flow design alternatives and decision

A. **No UI code:** existing year browser, management history and 12-year graph can already answer much of the question for experienced players, but they are spread among several cards. Good low-cost fallback; source evidence does not prove novice comprehension.

B. **Recommended minimal improvement:** in the selected Annual Review, a small “Since this management year” view: one row L1 recorded treatment/year/work, one row L3 difference between comparable saved canopy, living trees/young-growth occupancy and cash (clearly dated), one row L2 latest snapshot, one L4 prompt to walk the recorded area and reassess. No new save, no new score, no new standalone screen, no invented paired-tree history. Must not displace mandatory yearly review controls. Use an actual 1280×720 mockup before layout selection; no screenshot test was run in this audit.

C. **Deeper data history:** per-cohort exact survival, sapling progression and origin-by-location comparisons beyond saved annual aggregate requires separate research and likely new data/saving. Not approved by D-073 and should not be smuggled into B.

Three example cases: **worked year** shows recorded completed work and year snapshot; **idle year** shows “no work resolved” but legitimate canopy/young-growth changes without presuming intervention; **later intervened year** shows separate past and current interventions without double-counting or causal linking. **Older save with no earlier snapshot** gets NOT RECORDED rather than a fake baseline. **Oak/Beech** names/counts only where the per-species saved data includes them. Exact runtime demonstrations, rendered legibility and error conditions remain **NOT RUN**.

## D-074 / D-075 coordination and stop

The *related but separate* two-advance-mode feature must present **Advance 1 Year** and **Skip to Next Management Year** in the Work Plan. The latter is **a suggested reassessment point, not automatic thinning**. It must execute *each* annual authoritative tick and stop when an approved/pending Work Plan, mandatory review, required player action, completion/Year100 or unexpected safety condition needs intervention. Do not quietly auto-acknowledge first/yearly teaching review; intermediate quiet-year records remain accessible. Suggested **3–6-year** intervals from Irish CCF practice are educational context, not an unapproved fixed universal number; a provisional four-year target is a **product calibration proposal only**, still unapproved. Check eligibility and exact target before any production release.

Manager should amend the historical S1-D achievement contract in a new **APPROVED** spec rather than silently changing `HasReviewedTwoCycleSequence`. The prior S1-D accepted rule was two distinct years and no fixed interval. D-074 changes future design intent but does not itself authorize retroactive failure of old saves or rewriting frozen Reference Future worlds.
