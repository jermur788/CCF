## 2. Preferred art direction, accepted for design intake only

**A — Dense shaded Sitka floor:** muted non-black dark organic soil with broken/overlapping **fine Sitka needle litter**, a few small twigs/woody fragments, subtle roughness/tonal variation, intermittent subdued moss/liverwort islands and *sparse, site-plausible* low vascular vegetation. Avoid flat monochrome brown, an unbroken fluorescent-green moss lawn, repeated circular trunk discs, a fern garden or invented logging brash.

**B — Natural edges and tree bases:** irregular tree-foot blending and slightly brighter litter/optional isolated vegetation toward actual lighter forest margins, smoothly incorporated into the same ground plane. Variants are **art calibration options**, not claims the starting stand was already thinned or that causal annual-management ecology now changes textures. Keep the observed H1/path/planting join defect visible until repaired at its geometric/mask root; do not camouflage it using moss or debris.

**C — Modular production candidate:** reuse the #786 already-built 30×30 flat surface/atlas as an input; propose modest **opaque base + macro mask + needle microtexture** layers, optional low-cost sparse moss/twig decals and context-appropriate ground scatter only if real scene profiling permits it. Prefer consistent broad material/UV continuity and a few restrained variants over hundreds of per-tree unique transparencies. The proposal's colors, number of detail layers, 1–5m / 5–20m LOD examples and distribution are **game-art calibration**, not measured empirical values or ratified performance targets.

**Constraints carried forward:** Compact30 single continuous 30×30m flat ground; retained 175 simulated Sitka, 226 outside cosmetic-only shells, H1 clearing, four G1 exact planting sites, C0 pedestrian path; no new racks, mesh world geometry, tree placement changes, H1/C0/G1 logical masks, collider, soil model, browsing/regeneration, annual state, work plans, save or frozen Legacy40/Enlarged80/Reference Future changes.
