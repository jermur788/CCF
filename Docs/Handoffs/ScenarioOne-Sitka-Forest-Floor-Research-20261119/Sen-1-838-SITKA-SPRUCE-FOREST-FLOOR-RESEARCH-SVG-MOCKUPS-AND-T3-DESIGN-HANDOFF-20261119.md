## 1. Executive recommendation

**Adopt a needle-litter-first, visually varied but restrained shaded forest-floor direction as a *candidate*, not a gameplay rule.** Irish Sitka spruce closed-canopy vegetation-community descriptions identify a dense canopy and needle layer, generally poor vascular field layer, a real but often inconspicuous bryophyte flora, and bramble/fern/grass that can be sparse or locally stronger in openings [S1]. A flat homogeneous brown sheet looks synthetic; an uninterrupted emerald-green moss or grassy layer is equally misleading as the *universal default*. The core visual should read as deposited spruce needles/twigs over muted dark organic soil, with broken moss pockets, subtle surface variation and sparse woody fragments. Localised moss/bryophyte or vascular cover may be stronger in particular site/light contexts; it is not guaranteed.

Use **one coherent baseline ground material** calibrated at human walking height, plus a small *optional* authored shade/edge/moss/dampness family. Preserve underlying **one continuous 30×30 m flat walkable plane** and its existing H1/C0 masks. Do not introduce a second mesh, foliage-covered fake path, circular bare discs around trunks, artificial grooves or weather/ecological mechanics. Future surface detail should be an **additive/material-composition art pass** coordinated with existing #786 atlas and #836 path/fence work; final routing and source rights belong to #824.

**Owner symptom, not inspected evidence:** user reports that the ground beneath Sitka spruce in first-person Compact30 looks poor and specifically requests visual improvement. The research worker has not accessed a rendered current Unity capture; #830's 71/71 automated checks do not establish aesthetics quality.

## 4. Visual art direction and palette — candidate calibration only

| Element | Candidate working palette | Optical/geometry intent | System/status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic shadowed soil | `#281f1e`, `#392f27`, `#44372c` | Non-black shadow, muted local organic variation, rough rather than glossy | Material/roughness; new art calibration |
| Weathered Sitka needles | `#6f5642`, `#8c6343`, `#a47a55` | Thin warm-brown/tan strokes and tiny clumps, directional and varied; avoid pinecone-scale “needles” | Tileable microdetail / sparse baked decals; new art calibration |
| Twigs / fine branch fragments | `#2d241e`, `#554235` | Few small dark linear accents, never an even carpet of large sticks | Mesh/decal family; new art calibration |

| Brighter margin litter/vegetation | `#776044`, `#74845a`, `#869258` | Slightly warmer/lighter substrate; *optional* isolated fern/grass/bramble silhouettes with context | Authored visual variant; not an automatic treatment outcome |
| Existing C0 worn path / H1 | **preserve #786/#835 candidate source** | Path surface should remain visually distinguishable and walkable. Forest layer blends toward, never replaces, the route/footprint. | Existing separate owner #786/#836 |

Lighting: test **neutral overcast/daylight and brighter side-light** against existing URP exposure/tonemapping. Needles require fine roughness/normal contrast; do not compensate for dark canopy using neon green or universal high emission. Color should remain readable when a player looks down from standing height without requiring a high-noise carpet. Avoid exposed, uniformly glossy “wet mud”; optional damp sheen requires an explicitly approved lighting/weather aesthetic, not invented simulation wetness. **No empirical PBR albedo/metallic/roughness numeric recommendation is claimed.** #786's source report specifies opaque, high-roughness preview material (roughness .92), a 2048² macro atlas and 512² detail texture; treat that as an *existing candidate's documented source*, not a verified current Unity import or a mandatory final material setting.

### Tree bases and environmental gradients

- **Under dense crowns:** finer darker litter with low contrast and few distinct stems. Avoid constant absolute black, and avoid stamping a perfect brown circle at every tree.
- **Near trunks:** subtly greater needles/fines and occasional root/twig/moss variation. Tree-foot visual effects must taper with *irregular broken-up masks*, not force the same radial radius across all 175 simulated spruce and 226 outside-shell trees. Visible roots only where credible/allowed by the existing geometry.
- **At a genuine brighter margin:** maintain same base litter family, gently increase contrast/lighter debris and allow localized sparse vascular silhouettes. This is an **authored visual option**, not an inferred canopy-light or vegetation-cover calculation.
- **On the current flat plane:** apparent detail comes from material roughness, small decals and surface scatter that sits on the existing surface—not unapproved hills, step changes, collidable twigs, separate walkable planes or trenches.
- **Woody debris:** distinguish ordinary twigs/small dead branches (static presentation) from authoritative fallen-deadwood inventory/harvest consequences. Do not manufacture retained deadwood or felled trees in a new-game scene.

## 7. Rejected alternatives and trade-offs

| Option | Decision at research stage | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Uniform vivid green moss / all-over grass | **Reject as starting baseline** | Conflicts with dense-canopy/needle-layer Irish WL5A reference and makes the plantation resemble generic native woodland |
| Flat single brown colour | **Reject** | Owner complaint and visual readability: lacks micro-litter, tree-foot and macro-scale definition |
| Constant ring decals at every trunk | **Reject** | High repetition, geometric stamps and potential poor fit to root/soil masks |
| Layer of dense large fern/frond cards everywhere | **Reject** | Overstates commonly sparse vascular layer; alpha cost and player visibility issue |
| Separate displaced floor/raised berm or drainage trench | **Reject** | D-090 flat one-plane geometry and no site-authored watercourses/ditches |
| Fresh logging brash, stumps, clearfell scar/ruts | **Reject for unverified starting state** | Would depict interventions or traffic absent from authoritative scenario history |
| Automatically update moss/grass when canopy opens | **Defer** | A new causal state or time-dependent relationship requires accepted design, ecology linkage, verification and save considerations |
| Rebuild #786 atlas or steal #836 path material lane | **Reject** | Single-writer ownership; preserve qualified scratch provenance and path/seam treatment |
