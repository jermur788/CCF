## Placement recommendation

| Original case | Proposed role | Distinct proof-of-learning |
|---|---|---|
| **O3 Neglected Broadleaf Wood** | **Primary coppicing lesson**: a small formerly managed hazel (or vetted other species) stand and an adjacent coppice-with-standards area | Identify live stools vs decayed/unviable stools, select small rotational coupes and standards for retention; choose coppice, recover as high forest, or no-work. Commit work through Work Plan; in later years observe *real resprouting*, browsing, stool survival, shade, regrowth, smallwood yield and repeat cutting. Retained standards remain mature trees. Do not imply a normal whole-block clearfell is CCF simply by calling it coppice. |
| **O4 New Farm Woodland** | **Introduction/forward link** to hazel and other site-appropriate productive trees; optional small forest-garden *design observation* within forestry establishment only | Choose appropriate locations, canopy spacing, browse protection and whether non-woodland habitat should remain open. Observe establishment and competition. No full fruit production, yields, orchard pruning, commercial crop processing or general agriculture until Stage 2. Continue to teach temporary nurse planting as a distinct establishment choice. |
| **O6 Atlantic Oakwood** | **Counterexample/constraint**, not a mandatory coppice chapter | Where veteran trees, epiphytes, ancient/historic continuity or sensitive hazel flora would be damaged, recommend monitoring, invasive/browse control or restricted works. Compare cultural coppice legacy and current ecological sensitivity; do not assume historical coppicing must be restarted. |
| **O8 Complete Irish Forest Estate** | **Integration decision** for a bounded estate-local coppice compartment and potential productive woodland edge | Prioritise labour/wood products/browsing protection over years versus mature timber and woodland recovery. Fruit/nut patches can be identified as later opportunities only. |
| **Stage 2 first slice (D-054)** | **Full fruit, nut and forest-garden management after Stage 1 forest scenarios** | Orchard/woodland horticulture, harvests, flowering/fruit set (where evidence warrants), fruit-tree pruning, pests/protection, food processing and income; use the same authoritative site, growth, task→work execution, annual review and finance principles without pretending forestry trees already implement crop yield. |

**Key distinction:** Coppicing is a forestry technique and belongs within Stage 1; **full fruit/nut production is broader land management** and belongs to Stage 2 as already decided. The same hazel or chestnut may have forestry and produce roles, but a nut-bearing tree is **not** automatically an implemented yield/processing system.

## Candidate scenario-3 vignette, no fixed rules

One run-down broadleaf wood includes (1) a small healthy hazel coppice with young stools, (2) an abandoned patch with old declining stools, and (3) mature standard trees/habitat trees. The player surveys stool vitality and browsing, chooses a limited coupe or a hold/rehabilitation route, designates standards, decides what to do with rods/smallwood, and approves the operation. At Y+1 new growth is recorded where credible; at Y+5 it can be visibly browsed/competing; later cycles ask whether cutting, stool renewal or conservation restraint remains warranted. **These are illustrative review checkpoints, not Irish coppice-rotation prescriptions.** Restoration failure must be possible: old stools fail to resprout, young regrowth is browsed, insufficient labour prevents repeat management, or sensitive lichens are damaged.

This requires an **actual saved multi-stem stool identity**, cutting age/regrowth condition, annual shoot development with mortality/browsing, standards/retention state, legitimate coppice product/cost, and 3D render linked to authoritative state. Existing `Fell` marks and automatic mature-tree death do **not** constitute coppicing; a distinct approved coppice task and regrowth path require a four-file feature spec. Do not change S1 or old save semantics.

## Irish evidence and constraints

1. [Teagasc WIS Element 4 — Coppice and Coppice with Standards](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/grants/woodland-improvement-scheme-2023-2027/wis-element-4-coppice-and-coppice-with-standards/) — scheme recognises a site-specific plan, pre-harvest standards marking, coppice treatment, deer management, protection, vegetation management and existing woodland improvement. **Irish management guidance**, not a universal numerical model.
2. [NPWS / Native Woodland Management Guidelines (2017), PDF](https://www.npws.ie/sites/default/files/publications/pdf/Management%20Guidelines%20for%20Ireland%27s%20Native%20Woodlands%202017.pdf), pp. 75–80 and Hazel Woodland section — coppicing may support traditional woodland and smallwood but requires labour and browse protection; very old stools may not resprout; old epiphytic substrates, rare plants and invertebrates can be harmed. **Management guidance**, site-sensitive.
3. [Teagasc Agroforestry Forest Garden Pilot (2026)](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/grants/agroforestry-forest-garden-pilot/) and [Teagasc explanatory article 10 July 2026](https://teagasc.ie/news--events/daily/agroforestry-the-forest-garden-option/) — Irish forest-garden option intentionally includes forest canopy and food-producing understory, with eligibility limits including **up to 40% fruit/nut trees** *within that particular pilot*. **Current policy snapshot only**, not a timeless planting, profitability or model parameter.
4. [Teagasc FT8 Agroforestry](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/grants/agroforestry/) — another forestry/agroforestry route with a different fruit/nut allowance (**up to 15%** for FT8) reinforcing that ratios are scheme-specific, **not** general ecology rules.
5. *Pommerening, Szmyt & Duchiron (2024), Revisiting silvicultural systems*, supplied complete research archive pp. 99–111 — coppice systems and high forest share some principles but have different vegetative-regrowth dynamics; this is a **general conceptual research source**, not a calibrated Irish coppice growth model.
6. D-051/D-054/D-078 in [Decision Log](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Docs/Project/decision-log.md), [Game Brief](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/main/Docs/Project/game-brief.md): forestry-first Stage 1; eight independent cases; fruit/nuts first Stage 2 slice.

## Curriculum gate and next decision

**Recommendation:** include a *small credible coppice-with-standards treatment* as a distinctive O3 management fork, and **recognition/planting design** for suitable fruit/nut trees at O4. Carry full fruit/nut crop simulation as **first Stage 2 feature after forestry maturation**. This places meaningful lessons without a ninth Stage 1 scenario, without pretending fruit growing is already implemented, and without making O6's conservation woodland an indiscriminate fuelwood harvest opportunity.

**Unapproved:** any new tree/stool/save mechanism; specific coppice rotations/market prices; chosen species mix, orchard/forest-garden design, yield simulation, associated scenario objectives or final chapter order. Manager/owner confirmation and proper specifications required before production coding.

**Status:** document-only proposal, no testing/Unity/build. Dashboard/Drive sync not verified. No immediate product decision needed merely to preserve this option for future curriculum critique.
