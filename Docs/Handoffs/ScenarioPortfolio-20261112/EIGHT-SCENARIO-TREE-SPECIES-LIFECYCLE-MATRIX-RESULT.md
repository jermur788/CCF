## 1. Executive finding and authority

The accepted chapter order **O1 → O2 → O4 → O3 → O7 → O6 → O5 → O8** (D-079) can provide a defensible succession of tree-lifecycle lessons. **No suitable *new* species is currently simulation-ready for the first playable O2**: Scots pine, downy birch and common alder exist in the repository as explicit HOLD definitions, not registered playable species. Scots pine has the strongest Irish *adult parameter-design* basis, but that is not a finished individual-tree lifecycle model. **Preferred resolution:** authorise a clearly labelled, **internal** 3-species O2 independent-save/stand proof first; then qualify Scots pine as a real fourth species with causal adult and juvenile growth, site/light/browse responses, persistence, rendered stages and observable multi-year management consequences **before calling O2 a complete accepted scenario**. This separates difficult save isolation from species calibration without silently deleting D-080. It is a proposed release policy requiring the owner/Manager's approval, not an automatic reduction of O2 acceptance. If pine cannot be supported on the authored site or species fidelity is not achievable, use a suitable downy-birch cohort/planting pathway instead, at its actual evidence class, and reassign later novelty.

**Important pre-existing roster nuance:** the live Research Index describes an earlier *Scenario Two species roster* of Sitka/Oak/Beech plus Scots pine/downy birch/common alder. ## 4. Life-stage teaching rubric — PROPOSAL, not owner-approved scoring

A shared explanation vocabulary is useful; species should never be pushed through every stage merely to satisfy a chapter checklist:

| Transition / concept | Causal and persistently testable when a player decision depends on it | Credible observational-only treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Plant or germinate / source** | Persist planted position, species, origin, year and actual survival; for natural seed, a modelled on-map seed source/dispersal/site gate, not an unbounded off-map arrival. | Historic stand record or seed-bearing tree observed without claiming that new seed was actually dispersed. |
| **(b) Establish and survive as juvenile** | Separate seedling *appearance* from height-class progression under browsing/competition/site; keep annual losses, protection and survival state across reload. | Show genuine pre-existing suppressed seedlings if the scenario starts later in time, with authored provenance. |
| **(c) Compete, grow and gain form** | Species-specific or evidence-bounded ordinal light response, DBH/height/crown competition, mortality/form and annual progression. | Observe existing crown architecture or historical stem defects; don't invent growth from artwork. |
| **(d) Recruit/reproduce** | Explicit seed maturity, source, dispersal and recruitment *only if implemented*; then transition juvenile → sapling → grown individual with consistent IDs or traceable cohorts. **A count of germinants is not successful crop recruitment.** | Mature seed-bearing tree is a factual observation, not proof of a functioning seed-dispersal kernel. |
| **(e) Mature, manage, retain** | Harvest/retain/prune/leave actions alter persistent stock, crown/growing space, economics/deadwood/history; record tree fate. | Mature/veteran habitat age and past management may be authored, with no promise to regenerate centuries of continuity. |
| **(f) Replace/regenerate and repeat** | Real future cohort or documented regeneration failure and trade-off after annual advances; compare old and new age/size groups, not only a score. | At end of chapter, identify likely next management step without claiming the complete next century was simulated. |

**Suggested chapter stopping criterion (GAME-C):** a new species should have **at least two distinguishable real age/size stages**, **one meaningful intervention/non-intervention contrast**, **a persisted transition or changed state visible after several annual advances**, and an explanation that identifies what did and did not recruit. This is a *proposed release rubric*, not a numerical law; a short O3 ash-health chapter can assess staged loss/replacement without germination, while O6 can end at oak sapling recruitment and still honestly say veteran continuity is inherited rather than created. Never promise an entire 100–300-year lifecycle within a demo or import UK/European growth increments as Irish probabilities.

**Technical implication, not authorised design:** each newly modelled species eventually needs explicit species ID/schema, safe restoration validation, site traits, juvenile and adult responses, seed/no-seed policy, stage visuals/prefabs and annual-review/history attribution; state coverage should be driven by lesson claims. Species missing a commercial-grade model can be a **genuinely simulated ecological small tree** if its actual juvenile/cover/recruitment effects and state are causal and persisted. It must not be described as a full timber tree simulation. **Do not invent fruit yields** for holly/cherry/hazel in Stage 1; D-054 reserves that production system for Stage 2. [R1][R2][S3]

## 6. Manager decision recommendation and safety gates

**Minimum initial teaching cast (proposed):** O1 Sitka/Oak/Beech → O2 Scots pine → O4 downy birch → O3 diseased/at-risk ash (encounter, not plant) → O7 lodgepole pine legacy stand (Norway spruce on *sheltered* suitable subset only as alternative) → O6 holly → O5 common alder → O8 wild cherry on suitable fertile compartment. This is **a first-encounter ledger, not approval to plant each species everywhere**, nor a promise that all are commercial models. Risk is mostly **HIGH** for future new systems, not merely extra meshes.

**Two genuinely necessary owner/Manager choices:**
1. **O2 delivery policy:** approve **B staged *internal* save/forest proof → genuinely modelled pine before complete O2** (preferred), or require A pine on the very first playable O2, or choose C birch-planting focus. State whether the earlier Scenario Two **six-species roster** still binds the now-defined O2 acceptance. This decision must precede the O2 feature spec; do not present an internal three-species proof to players as finished O2.
2. **Novelty sufficiency:** approve or revise the **proposed** minimum of two real stages + one persisted causal outcome per new species, with ash and ancient oakwood exceptions honestly teaching persistence/retention and long-standing habitat; decide whether a new **encounter-only, ecologically active small tree** (holly) satisfies D-080 without a full timber-yield model. No speculative exact lifecycle “score” is attributed to the unfinished user statement.

**Mandatory engineering gate before *any* O2 code:** APPROVED four-part independent-scenario save/identity spec (spec.md, plan.md, tasks.md, checklists/requirements.md), independent save slots, wrong-scenario pre-mutation rejection, old `forest-save.json` preservation/routing, old-version/Legacy40/frozen Reference Future v1 negative/identity checks, one author per source file set, correct claim and Unity ownership, exact candidate verification. **No new O2 production code released by this report.** After that, a separate approved model/lesson spec qualifies the new species with realistic evidence classes, visuals, save/load and old-save guards.
