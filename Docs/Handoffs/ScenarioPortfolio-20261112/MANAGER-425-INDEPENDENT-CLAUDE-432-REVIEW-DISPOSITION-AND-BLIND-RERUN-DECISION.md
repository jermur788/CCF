### 7.1 Directly checked independent source corrections — now resolved as source facts, not gameplay formulas

1. **COFORD original, Guest & Huss (2012), [Nursing of oak in Ireland](https://www.coford.ie/media/coford/content/publications/projectreports/cofordconnects/SM21-LR.PDF), first-page study summary and page 4 discussion:** primary PDF was opened through public web and its first page visually inspected. It reports simultaneous oak with Scots pine or European larch, opposing observed risks: Scots pine performed poorly as a nurse on unsuitable/heavy sites; very fast European larch could overtop and suppress oak. In the discussion near PDF page 4, the study specifically says simultaneously planted Scots pine was unsuitable as an oak nurse in the observed circumstances because it caught up too slowly. It suggests larch in suitable **robust bands**, and alder/birch as conditional alternatives with trial needs. **This is now primary-source verified**, contrary to #430's overly general implication that pine is a good simultaneous nurse. It does not prove all same-year Scots pine/oak pairings fail, does not validate a larch/birch model, and is not a generalized Irish rate or gameplay rule. The webinar itself remains untranscribed.
2. **[Teagasc Woodland Improvement Scheme Element 4 — Coppice and Coppice with Standards](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/grants/woodland-improvement-scheme-2023-2027/wis-element-4-coppice-and-coppice-with-standards/), official page read 12 Nov 2026:** describes a site-specific Coppice/Coppice-with-standards management plan, standards marking, deer/invasives and possible singling. It does **not name hazel** as the coppice species. It names suitable broadleaf forest categories and free-draining mineral soils as preferred, with grant programme-specific eligibility. Earlier claims that this Teagasc page **establishes hazel coppicing itself** are not substantiated by that source; any hazel teaching is a design choice until checked against primary NPWS/hazel practice. Scheme rules/rates are time-specific, not ecology coefficients.
3. **Source-level confirmation (main):** `ForestSaveController.cs` hardcodes `forest-save.json`; `ScenarioOneManager.cs` logs wrong-`scenarioId` warning without fail-before-state-restore. The ScotsPine/DownyBirch/CommonAlder species assets exist but are **HOLD** for major ecology parameters and not scene-registered/simulation-ready (per `Docs/ScenarioTwoSpeciesFoundation.md`).
