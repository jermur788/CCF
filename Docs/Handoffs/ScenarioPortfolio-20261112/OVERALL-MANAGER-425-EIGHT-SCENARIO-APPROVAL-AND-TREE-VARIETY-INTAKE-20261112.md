## New, additional owner intent: tree variation and the lifecycle of trees

Immediately after acceptance, the owner asked that there *always be some tree variation*, for example at least one new species in the stand or one that can be planted, so later chapters teach not only different habitats but also the development of trees through time. The owner's last clause about the precise **“tree cycle in terms of…”** was unfinished; do not invent a final lifecycle grading formula.

- **D-080 CONFIRMED educational direction:** each subsequent scenario should be planned to introduce **at least one newly encountered tree species or new site-appropriate species the player can plant**, with decisions/observations about biological development, not a scenery-only tree swap.
- This means identify the real *tree stage and learning* per forest: successful seedling or planted stock, browsing/frost/site stress, juvenile canopy competition, crown form, replacement cohorts, mature seed/timber/habitat values, regeneration and appropriate retention or harvest. Not all stages need completion in one scenario; an authentic age/size cohort in a starting stand can carry part of the lesson.
- **Evidence and simulation guard:** a credible species-specific lifecycle must be backed by relevant actual authoritative state, growth/juvenile/regeneration/management transitions at the depth claimed, including honest fallback, not only a prefabricated decorative tree. Current Scots pine/downy birch/common alder are research/asset foundations marked HOLD; a new asset or source citation does not prove readiness.
- **Potential conflict:** previously O2 was considered the smallest new-forest implementation because it could reuse the three Scenario One species. The desire for a novel species in **every subsequent scenario** must not silently be dropped to retain cheap O2, nor silently add an uncalibrated new growth system to S2. First produce a per-scenario species/lifecycle evidence and readiness matrix; present a costed educational choice if the O2 novelty requirement materially risks the first second-forest save/identity proof. The education direction is accepted; specific O2 species, model fidelity, and any staged introduction are OPEN.
