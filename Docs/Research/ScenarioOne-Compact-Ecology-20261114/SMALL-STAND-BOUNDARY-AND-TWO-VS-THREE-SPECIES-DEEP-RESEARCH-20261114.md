## 1. Executive conclusion

**Recommendation with moderate design confidence, low empirical/Unity qualification confidence:** retain **all three species** in the accepted underlying Scenario One and final teaching scope, but **stage the attention demanded of a novice** (first Sitka release and establishment, then Oak's light challenge, then contrast with Beech's shade persistence). Test both **a truthful finite compact training plot** and **a compact managed/visitable polygon in a larger genuinely simulated forest** before selecting site size. Do **not** select 25 m or 30 m as “biologically safe” on geometry alone. A genuinely buffered context may correct missing-neighbour and missing-seed-source mechanisms but needs substantial geometry, persistence, map, management, economics and verification design; decorative woodland does not fix the ecological model.

The older narrative's numeric Year-0-to-Year-40 height table, its “only planting is coded”, “browsing/storm/understorey absent”, “30 m adds external Beech seed”, and its universal “20% broadleaf law” are **retracted**. The actual native #460 scratch study stopped at **Year 9**; Year 30/40 visual, saved-world and growth outcomes are **UNTESTED**. The current source contains real species-specific regeneration machinery, which is not proof of broadleaf natural recruitment in an all-age-20-Sitka start.

A compact parcel is defensible **as a declared teaching abstraction** (analogous to a small marteloscope exercise), not automatically as a self-sufficient ecologically isolated Irish production stand. Nothing in a 20/25/30 m square erases neighbouring real-world woodland, animal seed transport or microclimate. The exact managed-plot scale, curriculum time horizon, amount of surrounding modelled forest and replay design are owner choices after controlled evidence, not derived forestry minimums.

**Why a separate native study is needed:** source audit establishes *capability and limitations*, UK/Irish/German/Czech literature establishes *mechanistic plausibility*, and #460 establishes *short scratch behaviour*. None directly measures the coupled model's 30–40-year trajectories, actual boundary truncation bias, rendered broadleaf canopy, paired RNG fairness or learner comprehension.

### Key terminology / evidence grading

- **DIRECT CURRENT PROJECT/NATIVE EVIDENCE (D):** source-inspected implementation or values actually produced by #460 Unity. State whether code, configured state or executed output.
- **PRIMARY EMPIRICAL (P):** original field study; always country, species, sample and observational horizon.
- **TRANSFERRED UK/EUROPEAN (T):** original British/continental observation or guidance used as a hypothesis for Irish Sitka/Oak/Beech.
- **MODEL-SOURCE DERIVATION (M):** arithmetic/inference strictly from inspected code/native counts, NOT a Unity experiment.
- **GAMEPLAY CALIBRATION (C):** explicit model constants, thresholds, gameplay/economy values; not universal forestry prescriptions.
- **DESIGN HYPOTHESIS (H):** proposed player workflow, visual result, alternative or control.
- **UNSUPPORTED / LOCAL TEST REQUIRED (U):** unexecuted long runs, human studies, 3D witnesses, climate/buffer adequacy.

A technical implementation can be real but empirically rough; a literature-backed ecological effect can be credible but absent from the specific model. The classifications are not interchangeable.

**Missing-neighbour experiment (M/H):** for each identical focal-tree **position, DBH, species and site**, enumerate neighbours within 8 m in finite-only and genuinely buffered worlds. Record `CI_finite`, `CI_buffered`, `ΔCI`, crown shade/light `ΔLight`, seed rain `ΔSeed`, species-band recruitment and annual divergence. Distinguish physical property edge from forest-vs-open-ground landscape edge. Some finite parcels *really are edges*; supplying fictional external trees where the landscape is open would be a different wrong model. The #460 proximity statistic is a **screening prior**, not a demonstrated `ΔCI>0`.

**How much buffering is enough?** An 8 m ring would encompass the **Hegyi** cutoff only for focal trees, *if* external stems are genuinely simulated and remain within the resolver's modelled grid. Crown shade reaches current crown radius plus half-cell [C03], and source seed cutoffs are 50/80/150 m [C05]; full seed kernels may reach farther than a practical small context, while empirical seed dispersal may include occasional long-distance arrivals not represented in current source. Wind is an ordinal/shelter proxy [C07], not a validated physical boundary layer. A “complete 150 m square buffer” is **not automatically necessary or affordable**; compare sensitivity to explicit missing-donor assumptions. Planting is the defensible initial broadleaf pathway without assuming undocumented adult donors.

**External ecology:** a published review of edge gradients suggests above-ground microclimate transition scales often of tens of metres (about 25–50 m on average across reviewed contexts), but these are **not** a measured CCF Irish Sitka margin or a production radius. A “20 m stand has no interior forest ecology” claim is too absolute; it does mean most points are near edges. [P07]
