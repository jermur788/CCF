## Method and decision categories

Weight the judgement for *CCF's scenario requirements*, not marketplace star ratings: **practical relevance/visual quality 30%; actual editable/modular reuse 25%; total acquisition+integration cost 20%; Unity 6000.6 URP compatibility and dependency risk 15%; licence/provenance/long-term resilience 10%**. Scores 1–5 are analyst assessments based on published descriptions and known requirements, NOT software tests. Free does not guarantee low integration effort. A pack's high-resolution scanned materials are not sculptable physical geometry. All listed prices are **product-page observations with mixed original currencies**, before tax, sale conditions change; no checkout, VAT or ownership verification. All packs require sandbox Unity import/provenance/quality inspection before production. Rating below is **relative best expected procurement value**, not validated image quality.

## Modifiability distinctions

**CC0 (maximum):** edit/remesh/retexture/redistribute as governed by CC0 (excluding website logos/preview screenshots), strong archival/provenance; still costly to make seamless and performant. **Fab Standard (high for embedding):** modify/include in game, compatible tools, share privately with collaborators, but no standalone extracted pack redistribution; raw texture and FBX if source tier offered. **Unity Standard Asset Store (moderate):** customise in licensed product, non-transferable and separate seat requirements for extensions/contractors; no repackaging as standalone art catalogue, specific AI-model input use restricted. **Specialist terrain tool (high creative functionality but vendor coupling):** enables editable terrain stamps/heightlayers, not unrestricted redistribution of editor code/assets. Preserve clean project dependencies and do not submit proprietary pack bytes to external AI services without permission.

## Procurement / effort model: sensitivity, not quotations

Treat *total cost* as **licences (incl VAT + extra seats) + developer-hours × actual labour rate + upgrades + performance optimisation + rework across every scenario**. Illustrative scenario, not observed bid: at €30/h, 8h adapting CC0 textures costs €240 even if acquisition €0; one €55 pack with 3h integration costs €145 before VAT; a €64.40 MicroVerse licence with 12h integration costs €424.40 before VAT. Thus price alone cannot rank optimum; the pack must *actually save labour*. A free scanned library can be economically superior if appropriate material already exists; a terrain editor can win over 3–5 future scenarios if its stamps are exportable/reusable and import cleanly.

**Recommended spend gate:** €0 first: sample 3–5 Quixel/Poly Haven/ambientCG surfaces + official Unity tools + one Rowlan stamp, in temporary licensed comparison lane. If first prototype needs material convenience, approve at most one cheap Landscape Ground Pack (version 1 OR 2) after comparing its exact textures. Test Dynamic Nature/ALP only if free vegetation actually fails eye-level quality and the owner approves sample/licence. Consider MicroVerse only when second scenario shows reusable height-stamp authoring saves repeated labour; defer Gaia/Core Collection. **No approval to spend any money here.** D-032 storage/LFS/licensing remains OPEN; no purchased third-party bits committed until resolved.

## Quality gates

(1) Candidate's advertised files/licences/actual modifiability and source format inspected; (2) tiny **in-place 5×5m real uneven terrain** with roots/hummocks, nonflat collider, repeated reuse in second disposable scene with different materials/seed; (3) owner-side matched camera baseline vs candidate naturalism test; (4) absolute Unity 6000.6 URP profile/physics/scene selection/Year1/2 regression as warranted; (5) programme approves cross-scenario rollout separately.
