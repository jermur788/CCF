# Scene-level audit

Rendered ForestTest.unity through the current runtime at 1280×720, 1600×900 and 1920×1080. Captures include plantation, map, Work Plan and year-0 Annual Review at all sizes; staged juvenile/high-cover/cleared patch and inspection at 1920. Additional isolated species, thinning and deadwood views are recorded in the evidence manifest when available. Staged cover/juveniles are synthetic diagnostic setup, not a claim that ordinary year-0 ecology naturally produced that patch. Year-0 review cannot establish readability of populated long-run results.

Observed dense plantation reads as conifer forest with repeated stem/branch forms. Soil/track and ground flora are visible; prototype work structures have simple brown frames/green slab bases and abrupt ground transitions. The walking view is crowded in dense cover, so selected young trees must have a distinct action cue. The cleared fixture visibly removes patch plants and its juvenile representative; it does not show a convincing cut-stubble aftermath or deliberately bare sterilised ground. This supports targeted feedback dressing rather than replacing terrain.

Map and Work Plan remain inside the viewport at the smallest size in inspected captures. Small secondary explanatory text is low contrast over the moving forest background. Inspection occupies the left and HUD upper right without overlap in the inspected 1920 capture. No revival of the user's withdrawn old inspection-overlap bug is claimed. Dark transparent panels preserve scene context but require populated-data contrast review before broader release.

Because both capture conditions changed together, its exact cause was not isolated; do not report a released-player broken material or commission replacement assets from it.
