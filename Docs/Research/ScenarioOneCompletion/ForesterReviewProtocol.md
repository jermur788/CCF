# Forester / domain expert review protocol (Workstream T)

**Purpose:** a forestry-domain review of Scenario One's **plausibility, terminology, decision framing, causal claims and trade-offs**. It is separate from the beginner playtest.

**Explicitly out of scope:** balancing the game economy. Experts are not asked to set prices, the contractor minimum, grant values or calibration coefficients. Values are scenario calibrations ([C]/[D]); experts may comment that a value *misleads*, not tune it.

## 1. Reviewers

2–3 people with Irish or UK forestry practice. At least one with CCF/transformation experience (Pro Silva Ireland, a Teagasc or Coillte CCF practitioner, or a consultant forester) and one with ecology/regeneration experience. Remunerate if possible. Record no personal data in the repository beyond role.

## 2. Format

| Part | Duration | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Briefing | 10 min | What the game is (Game Brief summary); what is simplified (§3 handout) |
| Guided play | 45 min | The reviewer plays; the facilitator may explain controls freely (unlike the beginner test) |
| Structured questions | 40 min | §4, recorded |
| Written follow-up | optional | Annotated screenshots or copy |

## 3. Handout: what the model includes and excludes (shown up front)

Includes: individual Sitka with DBH (Hegyi competition, 8 m), Class III height curve, density-driven self-thinning; per-cell light; natural regeneration from seed (relative abundance, not stems/ha); browsing at low pressure; individual shelters; planting; clearance; pruning lifts; deadwood decay; contractor/owner execution; roadside timber assortments; a €2,500 minimum harvest visit [C].
Candidate (if in build): bramble/bracken competition (Model 2); storms (Storms v1).
Excludes: stem form/quality, harvest damage, extraction racks, soils/water, fencing gameplay, broadleaf markets, pruning premium, grants, pests.

## 4. Questions

### 4.1 Plausibility

1. Does the starting stand (age 20, 2,100 stems/ha, 0.16 ha, Class III) look and behave like an Irish first-thinning-age Sitka stand?
2. Does the stand's response over 10–25 years (growth, crowding deaths, canopy closing) look plausible after (a) no thinning, (b) light crown-tree release, (c) heavy thinning?
3. Is natural Sitka regeneration appearing where and when you would expect (road edge, openings, timing after age ~20)?
4. Are the browsing outcomes for oak/beech with and without shelters plausible *in direction*?
5. (Model 2) Is the role of bramble/bracken (smothering short seedlings; regrowth after clearance) plausible in direction? Is anything important missing (e.g. concealment from deer)?
6. (Storms) Do damage patterns (fresh openings, tall slender trees, edges) match experience? Is anything presented as more predictable than it is?

### 4.2 Terminology (`TerminologyAudit.md`)

7. Are these terms used as Irish practitioners would: Crop Tree, competitor, thinning, release, basal area, DBH, regeneration, enrichment planting, clearance / vegetation control, deadwood, pruning lift, windthrow, salvage?
8. Is any term wrong, archaic, or likely to mislead a learner? (For example "Crop Tree" vs "frame tree" vs "Q-tree"; "vegetation clearance" vs "weeding" vs "spot control".)

### 4.3 Decision framing

9. Does the game frame the first intervention the way you would teach it (start with what you keep)?
10. Is the Work Plan's "What you are leaving" a fair summary of what a forester looks at after marking? What is missing that the model could support?
11. Are the choices offered (sell / keep for use / leave as deadwood; contractor / owner; shelters) the real choices at this scale?
12. Is the second-look trigger (crowding returns / regeneration light-limited / storm) a sensible reason to revisit? What would prompt you?

### 4.4 Misleading causality

13. Show the reviewer every causal sentence in the build (ground "Why", inspection diagnosis, review lines, Model 2/storm lines). For each: "Would a learner draw a wrong conclusion from this?"
14. Does any message imply certainty the real world does not have (growth promises, storm cause, clearance benefit)?
15. Does the game ever imply that planting, clearance or heavy thinning is automatically good or bad?

### 4.5 Trade-offs

16. Present plans 1–9 from `MultidimensionalFeedback.md` (or the reviewer's own). Do the trade-offs shown match practice? Is one plan presented as more right than it should be?
17. Is the absence of a single score appropriate? Is anything you would expect to see compared absent?
18. Is the economic message (early thinning on a tiny forest costs money; you are investing in kept trees) fair, without quoting any number?

### 4.6 Closing

19. What is the single most misleading thing in the scenario?
20. What would you add first to make it more credible for learners?

## 5. Capture

Per question: summary, severity (**MISLEADING / IMPRECISE / ACCEPTABLE / GOOD**), suggested wording (if terminology), and evidence (screenshot or time).

## 6. Use of results

- **MISLEADING** findings → copy fixes (P8) or decision items.
- Model-direction findings (questions 1–6) go to the ecology owner as **supporting evidence**, not accepted calibration.
- Nothing from this review changes economy values directly.
