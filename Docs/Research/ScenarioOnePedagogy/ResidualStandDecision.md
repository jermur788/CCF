# Decision paper 4 — Residual-stand review: how much, and where?

**PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED** (scope and placement). Detail: `ResidualStandMetricAudit.md`, `MarkingAssessmentFramework.md`, `ResidualStandEvaluationPrototype.md`.

## Question

How much residual-stand information should appear in the Work Plan, the HUD forecast and the Annual Review?

## Constraints

- D-010: the Work Plan reviews; it does not design. No "suggested trees".
- No universal score (`DecisionMatrix.md` §2.5).
- Only AUTHORITATIVE or DERIVABLE NOW metrics; the wind band is excluded until recalibrated.

## Options

| Option | HUD forecast (while marking) | Work Plan | Annual Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Minimal** | Split the existing line: Crop-Tree effect first, stand second | none | none |
| **Recommended** | Split line (as Minimal) | Compact **"What you are leaving"** block: Crop Trees released (count ≥10 %), retained basal area and standing volume (before → after), cells opened and largest opening, seed-bearing trees removed, removals not near any Crop Tree. One line each, with plain sentences | "What changed since your last intervention" (from saved snapshots) |
| Full | Split line + neighbour shading | Full multi-dimensional panel (as in practice mode) | Comparison table with earlier interventions |

## Recommendation

**Recommended option**, delivered as Packet 2 (HUD line + inspection relationship) and Packet 3 (Work Plan block). Keep the **full** panel for the practice mode only, where comparing plans is the point.

Reasons:

1. The harness shows that the current single stand-wide figure can rank a clean-up thinning above a Crop-Tree release (T1 +12.2 % vs T2 +6.7 % stand-wide; Crop-Tree effect +9.3 % vs +11.7 %). The Minimal fix alone corrects the most misleading signal.
2. The Work Plan is where money is decided. Putting "what you are leaving" beside "what you take" is the single most practitioner-aligned change [PRAC §2.1, §5.2A, §19 item 6].
3. Six lines are enough. More belongs in practice mode or history.

## Example (Recommended block, harness plan T2)

```
WHAT YOU ARE LEAVING
Crop Trees: 16 of 16 get at least 10 % more space (average −20 % competition)
Growing stock: basal area 41.1 → 36.2 m²/ha · standing volume 39.9 → 34.7 m³
Openings: 23 cells touched, largest opening 3 cells
Seed trees removed: 0 (your Sitka are not yet producing seed)
Removals not near any Crop Tree: 0
```

## What the user needs to decide

1. Recommended vs Minimal vs Full?
2. Is a "≥10 %" band acceptable as a presentation choice (clearly labelled, not ecological)?
3. Should the Work Plan show the notional value of the retained stand? **Recommended: no** — it is easily misread as a market valuation. Show it in practice mode only.
