## One-paragraph summary

Six core scenarios plus two branch scenarios. Scenario One keeps both of its management cycles (the player sees the consequences of their *own* decisions). Scenario Two is not "the second intervention again" but **The Inherited Stand**: a Sitka-dominated woodland part-way through transformation by a previous owner, where the player must read management history from the forest, reassess earlier decisions (some good, some poor), decide where *not* to intervene, and meet pruning as a long-term investment, all on a uniform site with the existing species. Species and site arrive together in Scenario Three. Regeneration diagnosis and browsing become central in Scenario Four. Scenario Five (an established irregular forest) is the CCF graduation. Scenario Six is the integrated farm-woodland challenge. A storm-recovery scenario branches off after Scenario Two because Storm Model 1 already exists and it is highly relevant to Irish owners after Storm Éowyn. A native-woodland scenario branches off after Scenario Four because it needs the most new systems and tests restraint. Scenario Two can be built with today's simulation plus a small **scenario package** (a start snapshot generated by the existing engine plus a definition); the general stand-table authoring format is not needed until Scenario Three.
