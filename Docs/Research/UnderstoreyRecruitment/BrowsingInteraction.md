# Browsing remains independent

144 controlled cell/species fixtures cover Sitka/Oak/Beech, light .08/.8, browse 0/.8, protected/unprotected, cover 0/1, and seed 0/.05/20. Protected-area fixture uses an intact synthetic polygon through the existing browsing API; it is not new fencing gameplay. No protected browse loss, no zero-seed establishment, and population/request balance residuals below 0.00001 passed. No promotions occur in these short controlled fixtures.

The cover contrasts are identical under the current model. Thus HIGH vegetation / LOW browse and LOW vegetation / LOW browse currently have the same juvenile biological response; HIGH browse changes loss/growth independently. The virtual candidates expose separate vegetation and browse losses, and protection removes only browse terms. Numerical protection of a synthetic cell is distinct from shelter cost/lifespan checks in existing planting/browsing regression gates.

Irish deer exclusion can release vegetation; it does not justify collapsing herbivory and competition into one stress scalar. The browsing vegetationExposure future hook remains 1. No concealment benefit was introduced. A future ledger should show abundance before vegetation, vegetation loss, abundance after vegetation, browse loss, and remaining stock in deterministic order. Separate causes must remain diagnosable even when their sequential arithmetic multiplies.

Additional 16 actual mixed natural/planted juvenile worlds with protection and clearance timing were completed to100 years after the initial matrix. See MixedJuvenileControls.md and Evidence/mixed_juvenile_horizons.csv for the exact synthetic setup and species results. These remain unchanged-production controls, not candidate acceptance.
