# Clearance causality and timing

Production currently removes vegetation and tree regeneration; it does not confer a vegetation-competition benefit. Gap fixtures prove identical natural/planted outcomes for cover 0 versus 1 at matched light, browse, species and age. Area clearance resets ferns/grasses/forbs/shrubs, preserves moss/fungi, and removes qualifying cohorts and exact juveniles. Circular planting clearance removes the overlapping fraction and exact individuals; it persists a patch and does not zero the whole containing cell's cover. A future response must respect the partial footprint.

Actual 100-year reduced matrix: year-1 clearance matched no-clearance promotion results because the initial stands had no established juvenile stock and cover has no direct effect. Repeated year-1/every-5-year clearance generally accepted more establishment after opening capacity yet promoted fewer trees. Example moderate thinning / low browse: 398 promotions without clearance versus 182 with repeated clearance; cumulative accepted relative abundance 309.364 versus 505.027. The five-year interval is an experiment, NOT an empirical prescription. Higher accepted input is not equivalent to better recruitment.

Timing conclusions: before seedlings, there is no current vegetation benefit; after establishment, area clearance destroys the juveniles it contains; planting spots clear before adding the planted juvenile and avoid charging/crediting an invented vegetation immunity; indiscriminate later clearance can remove the planted juvenile. Repeated clearance can be harmful even if an eventual competition-relief term helps new recruits. QueryClearance's exact preview is the contract, and must remain truthful.

Fixed-environment production recurrence was measured after cover reset at light .08/.4/.8 and years 1/3/5/10/20. Positive target fractions are .30/.657/.83193/.97175/.99920, not an Irish observed recovery timescale. Target-specific zero values remain zero. The treatment-year stamp delays same-year recovery in real management; these fixed-environment measurements count updates AFTER the reset. Climate/site succession and regrowth from specific species are not modelled.

Management states sought for the future candidate: useful where a verified competitor bottleneck exceeds juvenile removal/cost; unnecessary where competitor cover is low; wasted where seed is absent or browse/light remains limiting; possibly repeat where regrowth is verified. None establishes a universal clearance schedule. Vegetation-mediated browsing exposure remains a future hypothesis; it is not a production clearance penalty or benefit.
