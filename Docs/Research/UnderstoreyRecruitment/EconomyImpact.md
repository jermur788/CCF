# Economy implications

No costs were rebalanced. Current area clearance price is the current manager contract: minutes=max(1,ceil(RemovalBaseMinutes + removed-relative-density × RemovalMinutesPerCohortDensity)); cost cents=ceil(minutes × ContractorHourlyRateCents / 60). ScenarioOne.asset specifies 6 base minutes and 3 minutes per relative cohort density; the live hourly rate is 4,500 cents (€45)/hour. A zero-regeneration-density eligible cell therefore quotes 6 minutes/€4.50; density 1 quotes 9 minutes/€6.75. This is a quote example, not the total billed value of the direct biological matrix. These are gameplay values, not a market quote. Exact planting stock and optional shelters resolve through ScenarioOneEconomyAdapter/ForestryWorkCalculator; stock is not repurchased if already held. Tree shelter material is the existing €5 D placeholder; nursery UI shows beech €4.50 and oak €5.50 per stock item, plus existing work charges.

The 100-year diagnostic direct-clearance worlds deliberately did not charge money: no 'profitable clearance' or viable long-run treatment budget is inferred from them. A separate existing economy integration gate covers wallet/approval/world restoration. Any calculated quote burden for a future trial must use live work orders and yearly owner-time/cash restrictions.

Avoided planting or shelter spend cannot be estimated by multiplying relative regeneration abundance by stock price: abundance is not physical trees and natural seedlings have different heights/ages/locations. Nor is each extra accepted band a rescued tree. Repeated clearance has a real quote/time burden and may destroy existing recruitment. Under the unchanged biology, clearing vegetation alone adds no demonstrated recruitment benefit; removing unwanted tree regeneration can still be a legitimate selected management objective.

Full future decision comparison must price no treatment, targeted clearance, repeat treatments, enrichment and shelters through the accepted approval path; record actual exact planting count, effective shelter years, cash and promotions separately. The current task found no canonical price defect that justifies economy changes.
