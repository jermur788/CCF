# Evidence review

Context: a8596df; audit starting commit 5c389cc. Research reviewed on 2026-10-07. Grades: A direct Irish/strong empirical; B transferable; C simulation calibration; D placeholder; I inference. A grade applies to the stated observation, not automatically to a game coefficient.

Primary project source: CCF_Primary_Literature_Synthesis_v1.pdf, pp. 6–7, locally held under /home/jer/Documents/CCF Game. It points to Harmer & Morgan (2007), Forestry 80(5), DOI [10.1093/forestry/cpm006](https://doi.org/10.1093/forestry/cpm006). This six-year English fenced Quercus robur study supports lower seedling survival with bramble/bracken in that experiment (B for transfer here). About 13% survived overall; annual mortality varied around 25–50%. These include other processes, so assigning that mortality wholesale to vegetation would double-count mortality. Associations do not supply a universal coefficient. The positive grass association in this context specifically argues against penalising every graminoid equally.

Perrin et al. (2011), [Irish woodland deer exclusion over 32 years](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378112711005202), DOI 10.1016/j.foreco.2011.08.028, supports vegetation release and changing community composition under exclusion (A for the Irish observation). Bramble/ivy/holly increases and ground-flora diversity changes are not an experimental juvenile vegetation-mortality calibration. Do not infer a universal bramble shelter benefit or that excluding deer is always an ecological improvement.

Secondary project sources: Understorey_Dynamics_Irish_CCF_Report.pdf, Irish_CCF_ecology.pdf, Irish_CCF_Browsing_Protection_v1_Report.pdf and Atlantic woodland/rainforest reviews. Their mechanisms and categories inform candidate design (B/I), their proposed game response values remain C/D. European_Pro_Silva_CCF_Practice_Synthesis.md supports the management interpretation: prefer effective natural regeneration, enrich where seed sources or recruitment are insufficient, and diagnose light/site/browse/vegetation. Practitioner advice is not numerical calibration authority.
