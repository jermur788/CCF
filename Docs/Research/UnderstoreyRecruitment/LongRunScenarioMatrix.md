# Long-run Scenario One baseline matrix

Actual production ecology: RNG1 / regeneration1 / growth1 / save17. Sixteen treatments × two repeats × 100 years; 3,200 annual species rows, 128 horizon hashes. All repeats matched at 10/25/50/100. Clearances call real ApplyClearance directly, with no work-plan quotes, cash settlement or history records: this is an isolated biological baseline, not a full paid management simulation. Smallest-stem removal fractions 0/.2/.4/.6 are initial experimental thinning, not repeated distributed selection or practitioner prescriptions.

Default starting stand provides Sitka seed; Oak/Beech do not acquire seed-independent infill. Their absent baseline recruitment is a seed-source condition, not comparative species success. Planted count and shelter count are zero in these 16 worlds; protection/species controls are separate short fixtures, not protected 100-year worlds. Candidate full-world, protected/enrichment, long-run planted and candidate save/load matrices remain required BEFORE implementation, because the candidate input/state decision is unresolved.

Clearance 0=none, 1=year 1 only, 5=year 1 and every fifth year. See baseline_horizons.csv for first recruit, cover, light/browse losses, and scope fields. No understorey competition loss exists in production. The current age-band representative promotion contract can create multiple exact trees from successive bands; cumulative exact promotions are not a conserved physical count from occupancy. Capacity-rejection and exported occupancy are separately retained in baseline_years.csv. The baseline ledger is species-aggregated per stand/year; the controlled ledger is per single cell/species/year. There is no complete per-cell long-world vegetation ledger for an unimplemented model.

Some high-browse baseline treatments have more cumulative exact promotions than their low-browse counterpart. This is an emergent age-band/capacity/canopy trajectory, not evidence that browsing benefits field regeneration. Interpret controlled same-state loss/growth contrasts separately from changing-stand promotion totals. Do not tune a coefficient to force a monotonic stand-level count.
