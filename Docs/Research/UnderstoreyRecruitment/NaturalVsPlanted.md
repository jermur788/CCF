# Natural and planted juveniles

48 matched-cover cases test three species × natural/exact-planted adapters × light .08/.8 × browse 0/.8 × cover 0/1. For each adapter and species, changing only habitat cover produces identical density/alive and height results. Seed is zero, natural band begins density .2/height .2 m, planted individual begins height .2 m/age 1. Fixture height equalisation isolates the gap; it is NOT the game's normal planting stock size.

Production uses shared JuvenileEcologyRules for light/site/browse response; natural bands use abundance survival while exact planted individuals use stable individual random survival. These different representations should share a proposed expected vegetation response, not be forced to identical realised mortality. Height/stature, rather than a 'planted' flag, is the defensible candidate vulnerability discriminator (I). No planted immunity selected.

Accepted exact planting performs a spatial spot-clear before installing the new individual. A future competition adapter must account for its footprint and recovery without clearing a whole cell or granting permanent immunity. Shelters modify browsing access; they do not automatically eliminate vegetation competition. Current model-1 stock remains relative occupancy, not physical stems/ha. Existing legacy cohort-managed planting stays compatible.

No candidate natural/planted save/load test is claimed because no competition model was adopted. Current planting, browsing, regeneration and save gates provide unchanged-model coverage in Regression.md.

Additional 16 actual mixed natural/planted juvenile worlds with protection and clearance timing were completed to100 years after the initial matrix. See MixedJuvenileControls.md and Evidence/mixed_juvenile_horizons.csv for the exact synthetic setup and species results. These remain unchanged-production controls, not candidate acceptance.
