## Authoritative query and safe targets

`ScenarioOneManager.QueryClearance(ClearanceFootprint)` returns the scope, cohort portions, eligible exact juveniles, affected understorey state and ground display positions. Both the player preview and the execution path call it. Footprint geometry is either the original cell square or the original planting circle. Shared `VegetationSites`/`RushSites` enumeration supplies the same positions to habitat rendering and target query; regeneration display positions use their existing deterministic jitter.

Included: all species cohorts in the affected area; exact alive, unpromoted juveniles; competing fern/bracken, grass/rush, herb and shrub displays. Cohorts remain aggregated: a circle reduces the affected area fraction, rather than pretending its representative is a physical seedling count. The label therefore names cohorts, saplings and ground patches separately.

Excluded: every standing ForestTree, including mature, crop, retained and already-promoted stems; deadwood, fungi, moss/litter; outside exact saplings/plant bases. Footprints classify vegetation by its base position, not foliage bounds; an outside plant may overhang the boundary and remains protected. No new age or DBH cutoff is invented. Natural and planted juveniles already leave their juvenile representation when promoted to ForestTree. Newly purchased stock is created after its planting clearance; overlapping same-year spot treatments do not clear stock just installed in an already-treated spot.
