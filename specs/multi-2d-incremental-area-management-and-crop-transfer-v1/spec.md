## 1. Product objective and two distinct success claims

**Owner-confirmed direction (not a signed formula):** preserve the existing single-area **Crop Tree FIRST** flow: designate retained Crop Trees, inspect their actual neighbours and competition, voluntarily choose Fell candidates with an honest reason, approve a Work Plan without felling, and separately Execute. Expand it incrementally:

- **Stage MA-1: genuine cross-area foundation.** Two distinct, synthetic and transparently labelled Sitka areas A and B, each with its *own actual tree roster, ecological state, area denominator and annual calculations*. The player manually marks Crop and Fell in BOTH areas and submits one all-or-none in-process Work Plan. One Execute affects both area rosters; one annual advance recomputes each area. No treatment is copied from A.
- **Stage MA-2: first meaningful sample → target teaching workflow.** From Area A's actual player-intended Crop Tree / selective competitor-release method, compute a **reviewable, explainable proposed method application** on Area B's real current trees; never clone IDs, quotas or blindly impose percentage BA removal. The player may accept, reject, edit or choose no action; confirmation of a prescription/selection is separate from Work Plan Approve, which is separate from Execute. The exact transfer rule and whether the preview is automatic or explicitly player requested require G5 approval.

**Non-equivalence:** MA-1 demonstrates a real multi-area engine, not transfer. MA-2 demonstrates a **synthetic teaching preview in an UNASSESSED/UNKNOWN-comparability area**, not an evidence-supported G1 COMPARABLE outcome. Neither alone satisfies [#828](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/issues/828) eventual source-qualified sample plus **at least two positively comparable target areas and a mismatch/unknown control**. That positive-G1 bar remains intact and BLOCKED by current [#851 evidence](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/blob/docs/multi-885-c-g1-real-area-evidence-gpt6-20261120-v1/Docs/Handoffs/MultiRack-Simulation-20261116/885-browser-parallel-20261120/results/C-REAL-AREA-EVIDENCE-RESULT.md).

**Minimal player demonstrations, once implemented:** MA-1: independently inspect A/B, manually select retained Crops and Fells in each, approve (zero physical Fell), execute once (both rosters changed with area-labelled receipts), Year+1 (per-area genuine growth/mortality/competition). MA-2: after a genuine confirmed A example, request/review the method on B, inspect reasons against B's live tree IDs, refuse or edit suggested candidates, separately confirm selection, Approve (zero Fell), Execute (only player-confirmed orders), and review Year+1. Both require native/owner testing later: they are not existing features.

## 3. MA-1 — foundation requirements (G5-A, G5-C, G5-E, D-4–D-6 OPEN)

### Identity and genuine differences

**Proposed separate fixture family (not a source grant):** `ccf-sitka-multiarea-fixture`, independent `mad-` ID/catalog/format and content digest; versioned deterministic synthetic teaching origin, regenerated and independently reviewed without touching `p1d-`, `P30-` or any P0 bytes. Start with exactly **two distinct areas** A/B as the minimal player case; five neutral areas to prepare later sample/T1/T2/control roles is a separate D-6 option, **not** a shortcut to comparable targets. Areas differ demonstrably in location/geometry and at least one actual age/size/stocking/competition profile; neither a duplicate scene nor a second map over the same state. A single original 30m inventory must never be split or cloned to pass.

- Stable **experiment ID**, distinct neutral **AreaId** (never permanent sample/target/control role), globally unique case-safe `TreeId` with validated area membership and nonoverlapping polygons, positive geometry-derived area hectares and independently assigned per-area RNG seeds. Reject duplicate/case-colliding/`P30-` IDs, wrong-area positions, duplicate XY, invalid polygon/area, mismatched manifest digest, malformed or extra/duplicate JSON keys. Specific regex/schema/limits from #852 `3 are candidates for owner/technical review, **not adopted constants**.
- Individual trees carry authoritative XY, age, DBH, height, life state and origin/provenance; source-missing values must not be invented. Synthetic trees are explicitly marked **AUTHORED / NOT FIELD MEASURED**. Site-index, yield class, soil, drainage, wind, thinning history and exposure begin **UNKNOWN** unless separately evidenced; values are descriptive **not active** in the existing growth formula. No area deemed ecologically equivalent because an engine currently holds the same site constant.
- **Proposed isolated annual dynamics:** call the **unchanged** deterministic 2D `AnnualRules.Calculate` separately on each area's own state/seed/areaHa, with one shared year and one shared annual revision. Hegyi competitors across area borders are ignored and the UI must say so. This is a deliberately isolated approximation, **not asserted real forestry physics**; cross-boundary interactions require a later approved model/oracle task. Per-area count, stems/ha, basal area m²/ha, mean-size/density, growth, natural death, CI, work and Year+1 reports come from each area's actual rows. Year-advance computes all results before one publish, refusing all on any area failure.

### One operation / one atomic commitment

**Proposed one immutable multi-area root** under one in-process lock; one shared plan-state/source fence and source-year/annual/work revisions, one whole-operation Pending set and Approved plan, one mint-controlled Execute key/event ledger. Each Fell order names exact `AreaId`+`TreeId`+reason+origin. Never execute N separate `AnnualContinuation` sessions in sequence, since the first may publish before later failure. In-process single-root publication protects atomicity only, **not crash durability**.

State machine: initial manual Crop designations and voluntary Fell reasons (mutually exclusive exact live-tree states) → Pending orders (zero Fell) → whole-area-group Work Plan preview → Approve (exactly zero Fell, all areas still alive) → **separate Execute** → validate every order and fence before committing any → build all resulting rosters/receipts → publish all area changes once, including one global event and area-specific before/after summaries. Orders in one, both or several named areas are allowed; a plan need not Fell in every area, but the demonstration does. Exact source/replay/fence behaviour inherited from reviewed single-area contract **only after actual source qualification**.

Refuse whole operation without mutation for wrong area, duplicate/stale/dead tree, Crop/Fell conflict, missing reason or approval, mismatched AreaIds token (compare value contents, not list-object identity), unminted/changed replay key, stale year/annual/work/plan-state revision, source ID/hash mismatch, cancelled plan, invalid step, injected fault after partial construction. Identical same-key replay returns same receipt and does not Fell twice; a changed payload with same key refuses. Pending work plus Year+1 **must** require player consent for cancellation; with consent cancel **all areas** at zero Fell before genuine annual advance. No implicit per-area success labels.

**Open D-4/D-5:** decide whether any area may be emptied by Fell (proposal: refuse whole batch if any managed area would be empty), and whether a naturally empty area may continue Year+1 (proposal: carry its empty row while other areas advance; refuse only all-empty). No silent policy adoption from the one-area P2 guard. No hidden cross-year work carried after cancellation.

### MA-1 user interface and testable behaviour

A player-accessible **area switcher** shows area identity, labelled synthetic status, each current roster and own count/BA/competition/year. Select/manually mark a retained Crop and potential Fell in each, see both areas in **one Work Plan** and once-only per-area receipt/history. Correct focus/keyboard and non-colour states, no raw execution keys in normal UI, no pseudo-economics. Core-only modules do **not** count as the requested playable MA-1 unless a separately approved UI path exposes and actually exercises the shared root. A Core-qualified subtask is interim engineering, not MA-1 gameplay completion.

## 4. MA-2 — conditional candidate method preview, not positive G1 (G5-T OPEN)

**Precondition:** approved/qualified MA-1, qualified actual #882 Crop-first source, independent narrow UI/source grant, player-visible evidence caveat, and **specific owner G5 decision allowing a comparator-UNKNOWN teaching preview**. Without this decision, MA-2 may add read-only UNKNOWN feature reporting only; **NO automatic target proposals** (#828 conservatism). Area B remains manual in that case.

**Treatment-signature proposal:** a versioned, immutable Area A sample event for a named ecological year and source fixture/revisions. It records the actual player-confirmed retained Crop IDs and their current before/after *modelled* competitor diagnostics, player-confirmed Fell IDs/reasons and exact event/source binding; cannot invent crop quality or a desired thinning percentage. **Recommended default**: require the player to execute/confirm A's sample under a normal Work Plan first, then derive from that event; a merely sketched or stale plan is not evidence of the player's practised method. Whether *approved intent before execution* suffices is an owner G5 question. The subsequent B preview binds a new current source token and must refuse changed A/B years, roots, Crop marks or action state.

**Two separated evaluators:**
1. **Scientific comparability**: initially `UNKNOWN/UNASSESSED` for every area, with missing source/site/measurement factors; `NOT COMPARABLE` and `COMPARABLE` labels require *independently accepted evidence criteria* and remain unavailable for this teaching slice. The preview must never write to or masquerade as the #828 comparator predicate.
2. **Local method demonstration**: a deterministic **Crop/competitor ranking on B's *own live trees*** using the verified existing Hegyi contribution and Crop eligibility/source views. Explain the candidate Crop-to-neighbour relationship, species/DBH/position/distance, each neighbour's contribution/relative share, current and hypothetical competition if proposed removal occurred, source year/revisions and why the rule suggested it. It may *propose* Crop candidates for explicit confirmation when B has no player Crop; user may decline them all. B's real selected/confirmed Crop(s), not A's IDs, anchor any competitor-release suggestions. Deterministic tie-break by ordinal IDs; no random picks. Exact qualifying Crop rank/spacing/DBH rules, release count, minimum contribution, tie/zero policy and any caps/guard bands are **TBD GAME TUNING** requiring owner sign-off, no default numbers silently supplied. No one can infer a Fell from high CI alone. The design must permit an explainable **NO SUGGESTION** outcome.

**Conditional interaction option A — recommended for policy safety:** after A's sample, B displays **“Comparability not assessed — Try this management method here (synthetic preview)”**; the player deliberately requests the preview. Suggestions are not automatically committed or counted as G1. **Option B:** B automatically *displays* an attention-neutral preview, still clearly UNKNOWN and uncommitted. This better matches the owner's desired automatic presentation but is **not authorised** until owner resolves the #828 policy conflict. **Option C:** keep B manual and disable all transfer pending source-qualified comparability; this obeys #828 most strictly but delays product-meaningful transfer.

Workflow if approved: *read-only preview* → player inspect/accept/reject/edit each B Crop/competitor candidate (or keep no work) → **distinct prescription/selection confirmation** binds exact candidate set and reasons and source token → turns chosen removals into PendingWork only → **independent whole-operation Work Plan Approve** (zero Fell) → separately Execute once → review B receipt/Year+1. Neither preview nor Crop acceptance alone creates Fell/PendingWork; no auto-marking, auto-Fell, auto-execute, auto-approval, hidden bulk acceptance or positive comparator label. Manual override remains possible with an honest reason and is distinguished from a preview candidate. Any edit after confirmation invalidates that confirmation; area/experiment/version/revision hashes and exact tree membership are rechecked at every boundary.

**Mandatory abstention/refusal classification:** 
- **Technical REFUSE (zero mutation):** invalid/wrong-area ID, tree dead or already Fell/Crop conflicting, duplicate ID, corrupt/mismatched manifest, source or plan stale, current sample event absent, unminted/changed key, nonfinite diagnostic, unauthorized cross-area or replay path.
- **Scientific UNKNOWN / NOT COVERED:** unavailable site/exposure/thinning history, uncalibrated similarity/size-class/density evidence, no licenced linked area treatment record; distinct density/size-class can trigger a *warning/decline preview* without being labelled scientifically NOT COMPARABLE on an invented cutoff.
- **NO ACTION / MANUAL ONLY:** zero neighbours, no eligible retained Crop, empty B, no justified release candidate, would-remove-all in B, unresolved fit, declined all suggestions, unsupported treatment method, target in a materially different qualitative structure. No one-size-fits-all benefit claim.
- All responses show whether the reason is an *engine refusal*, *missing scientific evidence*, *proposed tuning guard*, *user choice* or *model scope gap*. Never turn scientific UNKNOWN into numeric zero.
