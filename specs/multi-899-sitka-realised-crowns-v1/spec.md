## Evidence hierarchy and validity
- **Direct British open-grown potential envelope:** P. Tabbush & E. White, *Canopy Closure in Sitka Spruce*, Forestry 61(1):23–27 (1988), https://doi.org/10.1093/forestry/61.1.23 — n=73 open-grown Sitka, crown width 188.30+15.27×DBH (cm), hence radius `0.9415+0.07635×DBH_cm` metres. This is **potential canopy/closure guidance**, NOT an observed overstocked plantation crown width.
- **Direct relevant stem-mapped Sitka crown evidence:** O. Davies & A. Pommerening, *The contribution of structural indices to the modelling of Sitka spruce and birch crowns*, Forest Ecology and Management 256:68–77 (2008), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foreco.2008.03.052 — 154 Sitka and 125 birch in six Welsh plots; local spatial indices contribute to realised crown prediction, particularly dense irregular crowns. Project quantitative composite records radius model efficiency ~0.75→0.77 with spatial terms and crown-length ~0.67→0.74, but warns **pooled Hegyi radius coefficient is counterintuitive and must not be treated as a causal radial multiplier.** Obtain actual model equation/coefficients and domain before any adoption.
- **Direct UK thinning evidence:** *Respacing Sitka Spruce*, Forestry 61(1):1–22 (1988), https://doi.org/10.1093/forestry/61.1.1-a — after stand respacing, less dense stands developed greater mean crown widths and lower crown heights; does not define a one-year radius multiplier.
- Project source: `CCF_Growth_Density_and_Species_Quantitative_Evidence_Composite.md` §§5,8 (supporting research only), `GROWFOR_Technical_Evidence_Extraction_v1.md` (pure even-aged stand models, **not** CCF crown-width calibration). British transfer, not direct Irish individual-crown calibration; declare uncertainty and any missing raw crown data.
- Multiple crown disks may overlap legitimately; **crown projection union, crown shape/direction, light transmission, and stem density are different metrics**. Do not equate 100% crown union with 0% transmitted light or zero regeneration (regeneration currently NOT MODELLED).

## Candidate scientific approaches: examine before selecting
A. **Empirically fitted scalar realised-radius formula**, using diameter, height/crown length, local basal area, competitive neighbours and suitable stocking variables; potential open-grown radius remains a biologically plausible ceiling. Use proper Sitka data and residual/uncertainty information; do NOT lift a pooled counterintuitive coefficient or choose response `1/(1+CI/5)` merely because it already exists.
B. **Spatial growing-space/sector-aware crown envelope**, if credible data/shape evidence support it. Explicitly consider crown asymmetry, neighbour distance/direction/height/dominance, overlap allowed and response to thinning. A strict Voronoi-cell cap, nonoverlapping circles or dividing stand area equally by number of stems is **NOT a defensible default** without evidence. Scalar model may be preferable to an unjustified complex directional model.
C. **Separate adult initialisation from annual response**: build a plausible actual starting canopy from observed-style competition/spacing, then update radius over years using empirically bounded lateral growth/release constraints and stand age; don't instantly 'relax' decades of grown-in crown space to new synthetic target. Age and prior management history may be unobserved; state uncertainty.

Evaluate options against ability to reproduce measured Sitka crown widths in dense and thinned stands; data coverage and portability; model complexity; minimal actual code/fixture/simulation semantics change; year-to-year determinism. Make one recommended choice with equations, units, coefficient provenance, expected error/range and limitations, **then seek Owner G5 + approved quartet before writes**. If empirical evidence inadequate, prefer a transparently limited calibrated approximation over claiming ecological accuracy.
