## 1. Product intent and binding G0

The first-pass standalone synthetic **30 × 30 m, 0.09 ha, 100-Sitka 2D stand is ONE UNDIVIDED MANAGEMENT AREA**. Its full stand is in scope for player inspection and planning, **not** a command to treat all 100 trees. Valid felling, retention and sample decisions attach to exact, individually validated, live source-tree IDs. The player demonstrates judgement by manually selecting trees or selecting a representative local sample; optional comparison-based extension produces suggestions only when a separately proved G1 predicate permits it. A proposal is not Fell.

**G0 RESOLVED by latest owner:** no physical rack layout, entry, corridor, served-tree eligibility, rack Confirm, rack-clearing PendingWork or rack geometry edit/lock in this V1. **Binding Multi product purpose — owner's [#819 clarification](https://github.com/jermur788/CCF/issues/819#issuecomment-7015429935):** use a representative sample of management decisions in one area, identify **multiple genuinely comparable** target management areas through source-defensible evidence, present conservative per-tree proposals with coverage/exclusion reasons, permit explicit player review and override, commit only via the ordinary approved Work Plan, then observe true per-area authoritative execution and following-year ecological effects. ## 3. Player workflow and required state boundaries

1. **Inspect.** Show accepted source/experiment/fixture, committed year, management area (one undivided area), live/dead exact IDs and genuinely available current measurements. Read-only inspection remains available even when work is refused.
2. **Select manually or demonstrate a local sample.** The player selects individual living trees, inspects retained/crop/release/competitor relationships and records a reason per decision. A sample consists of explicit source-backed exact IDs and current-neighbourhood evidence when available; it does not create any smaller management area or rack. A sample with inadequate evidence is labelled **unqualified**; per-ID manual selection remains available.
3. **Optional treatment proposals (G1-gated).** A transparent source-based sample-derived comparison may suggest specific additional current-tree IDs **only** after independent G1 proof of similarity features, predicate, exclusions and calibration. Same species/age or membership of the undivided area does not qualify. If G1 is absent, extension is **DISABLED**, all such targets remain Manual/NotCovered; manual-only full workflow is valid.
4. **Review choices.** Show exact IDs, source year/sample, player-authored versus suggested status, reasons, exclusion/match evidence and uncertainty. Player may accept/reject proposed IDs individually and manually select alternatives. No out-of-sample CropTree declaration without separate inspection. Candidates and proposals have **zero biology, finance or executed-history effect**.
5. **Create PendingWork.** For any G1 sample-derived proposal, **separate prescription review/approval is mandatory** before it may create or reconcile source-bound ordinary PendingWork orders; editing the proposed IDs, treatment or source binding invalidates that prescription approval until reviewed and approved anew. For manual selections, an explicit reviewed **Add to Work Plan** action produces PendingWork directly; manual work does not require a fictional prescription approval. Duplicate and contradictory exact-ID orders must be reconciled explicitly before approval or fail closed; neither path can Fell.
6. **Work Plan approval: sole commitment gate.** Show consolidated pending manual/prescription work, exact current source/ID/revision, available execution path and truthful cost availability. Player must **explicitly approve the whole immutable batch**; editing already approved but unexecuted work requires visible warning, consent, cancellation/review and a new approval. Opening a view, clicking a candidate, adding an order or advancing a year never approves/executes. **Approved Stage 5 year-advance policy:** if PendingWork or an approved-but-unexecuted batch exists, warn and require explicit consent before ecological year advance. Refusal leaves current year and work unchanged; consent marks every unexecuted order stale/cancelled with **zero Fell**, then advances through genuine annual biology. New work requires a fresh source/year review and Work Plan approval; no stale order is carried forward.
7. **G2-gated actuation.** Separate explicit Execute runs a proven authoritative **whole-batch atomic** transaction in the bound current source/year. All IDs validated again at execution. One unique source-year + batch-revision + execution identity causes at most one real Fell per valid live ID, an actual Fell cause distinct from natural death, post-work state and exactly one committed result/event. Same-process identical-key replay returns already executed/no-op; changed payload or different key for dead/already-felled IDs refuses. Any invalid ID, stale source, conflict, exception or inability to commit result causes **zero partial Fell and zero history**. G2 must prove the write/rollback/event seam *before* coding.
8. **Real annual biology and review.** Explicit advance of the next ecological year consumes the actual post-Fell survivor roster, recalculates actual competition/growth/biological mortality and commits the genuine next-year source. Report actual **before/after live counts, basal area and per-ID work results**; distinguish “year y work executed” from “year y+1 ecology committed”, preserve year-specific read-only actual history and permit a fresh cycle or a no-work year. No preview, count relabel or fabricated next-year change qualifies.

### 3.1 Conceptual states (not an existing ABI)

- **READ_CURRENT** (admitted source only) → **DRAFT_MANUAL / DRAFT_SAMPLE** (player selections, no authoritative work).
- **PROPOSED_TREATMENT** (optional G1-proven suggestions, always hypothetical) → **PRESCRIPTION_REVIEWED / PRESCRIPTION_APPROVED** (**required for every proposal before PendingWork**, not required for player-authored manual Add to Work Plan; proposal edit invalidates approval).
- **PENDING_WORK** (manual “Add to Work Plan” or approved prescription), editable/cancellable by explicit action; **WORK_PLAN_APPROVED** immutable approved batch awaiting separate execution.
- **EXECUTED** is source-committed and its dead archive/history cannot be reversed; **FAILED_OR_REFUSED / CANCELLED** make no partial biological changes; **NO_WORK** creates no Fell. Stale edits invalidate pending/approval and require new current-source review. Under the **approved Stage 5 year-advance rule**, pending or approved-unexecuted orders require warning/consent to stale-cancel at zero Fell before the year advances; denial blocks advance without mutation. A future second management-area value is not instantiated here.

Screen-readable statuses must distinguish draft, proposed, pending, approved, executed and next-year committed without relying on colour. The exact event order and atomicity require G2 independent source qualification, not a UI-only state diagram.
