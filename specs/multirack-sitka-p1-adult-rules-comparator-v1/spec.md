## 1. Purpose, scope and non-goals

**Research question:** Can a separately versioned annual experiment reproduce the *existing* CCF adult Sitka rules (DBH increment, local Hegyi competition, relative height, crown relaxation and density-based natural mortality) on a restricted, closed, fully mature-equivalent, pure-Sitka synthetic stand, with deterministic per-tree replay, without modifying the accepted P0 laboratory? What aggregate **B/N/H** behaviour emerges under no-thin and controlled thinning compared with the historical Broad–Lynch 2006 stand reference?

**Approved for DRAFT development only:** develop a testable design, not an executable model. A future approved P1 layer would consume a **read-only, verified** P0 synthetic fixture/snapshot and fork a wholly separate experiment, whose model and data identity are explicit. It may offer year-by-year output and stand metrics, but not silent "better" forestry recommendations.

**Out of scope and expressly not authorised here:** adding biological growth to P0's **Advance experiment step (calendar only)**, modifying existing P0 fixture bytes/hashes/events/saves/schema1/UI behaviour; new growth coefficients or independent biological calibration; juveniles/saplings/young stages, seed/recruitment, light, browsing, storm, site variation, disease, regeneration, planted trees, Oak/Beech, mixed stands, plot slope, rack geometry/five-rack extrapolation, 3D/Unity synchronization, contractor/economy/timber effects, measured Irish marteloscope import, empirical longitudinal forecast validation, production CCF saves, frozen Reference Future or Unity/Windows Player builds. Nothing here modifies Scenario One's existing accepted adult mechanics.

**Honest output label (C-7; proposed binding upon approval):**
> **CCF adult rules — closed pure-Sitka experimental population. Synthetic inputs. NOT an Irish field forecast.**

The proposed label is accompanied by “No recruitment or storms” and domain flags. Every P1 screen, export, chart and report must retain this distinction even when compared with a 2006 empirical stand-model output. P0 continues to say **SYNTHETIC TEST FOREST — NO BIOLOGICAL GROWTH**.

## 3. Source-faithful biological and timing contract — proposal, not parity verdict

At the pinned CCF source snapshot:

- Adult DBH for the Sitka asset: potential 1.2 cm/yr, max-scale 120 cm, Ci50 5. The source's function is `ΔD=1.2·site·clamp01(1−D/120)/(1+CI/5)`; the current `site` value is **always 1** because `ForestEcologyCell.SiteProductivity` has initializer 1 and no source writer (m-2). This is **current-game parity**, not a variable empirical site class.
- Hegyi CI: sum living non-stump neighbours within **inclusive 8 m**, stable ordinal ID ordering, with term `(D_neighbour/max(1,D_target))/max(0.5,distance_m)`; source uses a slightly enlarged candidate-query radius but exact 8 m final check. Geometry uses Unity horizontal x/z metres, not P0 x/y labels or elevation. A site/coordinate policy must handle float differences and edges.
- Sitka height in growth model ≥1 uses source Class III `E(age)`, anchored at 20.4 m at age 30, and multiplies individual height by `E(a+1)/E(a)`; it is **not** the Broad–Lynch GROWFOR height/site-index function. The current tree `Height` getter is **stage-dependent** for Sapling/Young; therefore **Mature-equivalent only** is the DRAFT scope (m-1). Max 60 m clamp is game policy, not an Irish data validity limit. Source clamps in `ForestTree.ApplyGrowth` are DBH `[1, 200]` cm and height `[0.1, 60]` m; crown radius has a 0.1 m floor (`RelaxCrownRadius`). The oracle must exercise them.
- Crowns relax 15% toward a target computed from *post-growth* DBH potential `0.9415+0.07635·DBH_cm` and *pre-growth* competition; initial crown size cannot be reconstructed from P0 and must be **labelled synthetic initialisation**. No independent physiological crown-release evidence follows from this rule.
- CCF density uses **all living trees** (including P0 below action threshold), `Dq=sqrt(mean(DBH_cm²))`, `N/ha=numberLiving/standAreaHa`, and `RD=(N/ha)·(Dq/25)^2.063/1868`. The source uses a **square** world `standSizeMeters²`, not the existing non-square P0 demo polygon (m-5). The exponent and 1868 coefficient are labelled British transfer, not validated Irish stand limits.
- Density pressure `P=max(0,(RD−0.6)/0.4)` from **post-growth** RD (post-growth DBH, current living set); suppression `S=1−1/(1+CI/5)` from the **pre-growth** CI computed at the start of the year and **not recomputed** after growth (`ForestEcologyController.cs:475`, `:1088–1092`); conditional hazard `p=min(0.5,0.08·P²·S⁴/mean(S⁴))` when P and mean denominator nonzero. The ecological year, seed, full ordinal tree ID and **RNG model version**, separately from growth model version, determine each roll (m-6). Then survivors are removed by suppression priority to RD≤1 **or at most one living tree**; tiny area/singleton can remain RD>1 and must be tested, not incorrectly "fixed" in a port.
- Source schedule: committed work/felling **before** annual advance affects the **next** annual pre-growth neighbour set. Inside annual advance: increment ecological year → pre-growth competition → adult DBH/height → crown relax → post-growth density/death, with natural death batch followed by accounting. No second intra-year growth for survivors after mortality. Storm, juveniles, regeneration and other current-game systems are deliberately absent; therefore this comparator **is not full game parity**.
- New CCF games select growth model **2**; on a closed all-`Reference`-demand Sitka population, the adult ecological density route uses the same model-1 path. A non-Sitka living tree/non-Reference demand changes the route. Growth model 0 is historical/diagnostic only and must not be labelled the P1 target. Old game saves with missing/old model fields and frozen Reference Future remain outside the lab and unaltered. Source save schema was **24** while the historical Sitka verification harness expected **23** (stale assertion, not a production source defect). Any harness repair requires a separate owned/approved task, never weakening the check. That repair is **optional/conditional** for P1. It gates P1-01 only if the oracle chooses to reuse that historical harness. A new dedicated capture harness does not depend on it (R-6).

**Numerical reference of record (R-1).** Faithful parity is defined against **per-ID values captured from the pinned CCF source running in the Unity Editor (Mono) runtime**, not against a language data type. Evidence:
- **Source observation, not a new Unity run.** The CCF source records that Unity Mono's float results depend on code generation. `ForestEcologyController.cs:990–993`: routing an identical Hegyi expression through a shared function changed the 80-year anchor hash, even with `AggressiveInlining`. `:1021–1023`: legacy Mono anchors "depend on the exact floating-point evaluation". `Tools/Verification/BrowsingProtectionVerification.cs:289–291` and `Docs/ScenarioTwoSpeciesFoundation.md:143` say the same.
- **Probe, not Unity output.** The MULTI-012 reviewer compared two *plausible* float evaluation semantics on the synthetic P0 fixtures. 94/153 (demo) and 981/1,893 (perf) first-year DBH increments differ in binary32 bits. Neither semantics is verified Unity behaviour.
- **Consequence.** "Use binary32" is a **hypothesis about how to approach parity, not a guarantee of it**. The C# type does not fix the runtime's evaluation width or order. A future adapter should preserve the source's mixed float32/double steps *as far as the capture verifies them*.
- **Portable part.** The adult mortality roll (`SimulationRandom.Roll`) is pure integer hashing that returns an exact 24-bit float. It is runtime-portable when model version, ID, year and seed match. The float-sensitive quantities are CI, DBH, height, crown, RD/P/S/p and the comparisons against thresholds.

The 9 formula examples are **entry-point probes**, not enough to qualify a whole-year adapter. Update ordering, clamps, tie-breaks and rounding also need that runtime capture.

Validate every raw chain independently when histories differ.
  - **Optional normalized comparison (OPEN C-1/C-1A):** a projected-history or projected-snapshot equality rule is **not defined or approved here**. If later needed, it must be separately named and versioned, specify retained/excluded fields, approval-session treatment, ordering/renumbering, deterministic reserialization and rehashing, and receive independent review before P1-03 acceptance. Never claim that deleting a load event alone creates a comparable canonical hash.

  - **Mode A — no open APPROVED/PENDING_WORK plan at the split point.** Require matching per-ID biological state, ecological year, model/RNG/seed, mortality/felling ledger and legally equivalent executed-work outcomes. **Do not require raw canonical-history or complete-snapshot hash equality merely because no plan was open at the split.** A recorded load event changes the history; a new approval **after** the split may embed a different random session epoch even if no load event was recorded. ### 4.1 Proposed original-fixture import and file boundary (C-1/C-1A)

**Recommendation for review, not owner acceptance:** permit only an original checked-in synthetic fixture from a pinned P0 catalog. Alternatives remain (a) a validated post-intervention fork with a separately designed migration/provenance grammar, or (b) park P1 and keep P0. V1 recommends the original-only route because P0 calendar events contain no biological growth and transferred approvals carry another session's authority.

Proposed import is all-or-nothing:
1. Resolve an allowlisted fixture ID through the **P1-owned original-fixture trust table** described below. Compare the complete file SHA-256 against that table, never against a hash supplied by the imported file or by any file in the P0 data directory. Parse P0's actual strict header, validate the snapshot and recompute the P0 canonical snapshot hash against the table value. Preserve both hashes; they prove different things. Changed whitespace can preserve snapshot hash but must fail original-file identity.
2. Require `sourceClass=Synthetic`, original `experimentStep=0`, `forestRevision=0`, no plans, no event log, unmarked entries and exact catalog statuses/IDs/values. Reject a post-execution, calendar-advanced, newly marked or purportedly APPROVED/PENDING_WORK snapshot even if internally replay-valid. No silent resetting of these fields to make it importable. An original fixture containing Felled or unexplained operational history is unsupported in this v1 proposal; original Dead is supported as §5.1 specifies.
3. Preserve original inventory year, per-ID age, coordinates, dimensions, status, action threshold, polygon and source identity in immutable provenance. Never rename/case-fold IDs or import by list index. IDs remain case-sensitive ordinal strings; distinct case spellings are not aliases. Record a separate, versioned P1 initialization manifest and adapted-state hash. P1 initial plans and ecological event history start empty; a single creation event identifies the fork. P0 source remains byte-identical on disk.
4. Validate biological domain and coordinate adapter before activation. A valid P0 overflow-safe value may be out of the P1 source domain; reject unsupported dimensions/ages instead of silently clamping inventory inputs. Proposed ordinary live input bounds are DBH `[1,200]` cm, H `[0.1,60]` m, source-representable nonnegative integer age with headroom for the requested horizon. Tiny-stem/clamp function probes are separate source test vectors, not licence to rewrite an imported fixture. No arbitrary biological age upper limit is claimed. Dead dimensions remain immutable historical data, excluded from biology.

**Original-fixture trust anchor (M-2, proposal).** P0's own catalog is not a trust root. At `2659db99`, `FixtureCatalog.LoadFrom` (`FixtureCatalog.cs:20–44`) hashes whatever bytes it reads and never reads the co-located `fixtures/synthetic/MANIFEST.sha256`, so a fixture and that manifest can be rewritten together. The proposed anchor is a **P1-owned, reviewed trust table**, compiled into P1 source or shipped as immutable packaged P1 configuration, and **never read from the imported P0 data directory**. Each record binds:
- the original `fixtureId`;
- the complete original file SHA-256;
- the canonical P0 snapshot hash;
- the P0 repository revision from which the record was prepared.

The revision is **provenance metadata**: runtime compares the bytes to the embedded reviewed values and never queries Git. The P1 import owner prepares the table from the pinned P0 revision, and the independent file-security reviewer checks it before any deployment. Adding or changing a record is a new P1 version with its own review. Values are **pending**; this DRAFT invents no hash. A consistent fixture-plus-manifest forgery must still be refused, because the embedded anchor does not change. A SHA-256 match establishes integrity relative to the reviewed anchor only. It is not authorship, a signature, or evidence that any forest or Irish measurement is authentic.

**P1-owned derived capture-fixture catalog (M-1, proposal).** Source-parity runs need square stands the pinned game source can actually configure (40 m or 80 m, §5.1), and no original P0 fixture is one: `oracle-quarter-ha` is a 50 m square and the demo/performance stands are polygons. New **derived capture fixtures** therefore live in a separate P1-owned catalog with its own format, schema version, fixture identity and a trust table of the same kind as above. They are:
- designed test fixtures, never presented as original P0 inputs;
- never written into P0's `fixtures/synthetic/` directory, which `FixtureCatalog` enumerates whole;
- never imported through the original-P0 path.

| Archive layout | Rough bytes per tree-year | 2,000 trees: 1 / 10 / 40 years | 20,000 trees: 1 / 10 / 40 years |
|---|---|---|---|

### 5.2 Proposed annual transaction, work and replay grammar (C-1/C-1A)

Candidate immutable event envelope: event-schema version, contiguous sequence, kind, source/initialization identity, command ID, ecological year before/after, expected forest revision, previous-event digest, pre-state hash, ordered typed payload and post-state hash. Genesis commits the input/algorithm manifest. Proposed SHA-256 history digest hashes a `P1-HISTORY-V1` domain tag plus canonical envelope bytes excluding its own digest; state hashes use a separate `P1-STATE-V1` tag and canonical full state, including plan approval/session fields where present, excluding the history tip to prevent a cycle. Exact byte layout requires future C-1 independent review/golden examples. P0's snapshot hash is **not** retroactively redefined as this history digest. Hashes detect corruption relative to trusted input; an attacker able to rewrite the entire local chain can rehash it. No cryptographic authorship/signature claim is made.

**Hash field membership (m-D, proposal for C-1 review).** Pre/post state hashes cover state only. The history digest covers envelopes, which contain those state hashes. State never contains the history tip, so there is no cycle.

| Field | Persisted P1 state / `P1-STATE-V1` hash | Biological projection | History envelope / `P1-HISTORY-V1` digest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-ID trees (status, DBH, H, crown, age, XY, provenance) | Yes | Yes (biological fields only) | Through post-state hash |
| Ecological year, growth/RNG model versions, seed, fixture/initialization identity | Yes | Yes | Through post-state hash |
| Plans, stages, base revision and `ApprovalSessionEpoch` on approved plans | Yes | No | Through post-state hash and event payload |
| **Current** session epoch | **No**: runtime only, as in P0, where only approvals carry an epoch | No | Only where an event payload records it |
| Committed command-ID set (dedup) | Yes | No | Through post-state hash |
| Death/felling ledger (IDs, causes, years, roll inputs) | Yes | Yes | Through payload and post-state hash |

Consequences:
- An audit-only `ExecutionRejected` changes **no** biological value. It changes the state hash only if it records a command ID, and it always extends the history.
- A `PlanStale` event changes state (plan stage) and history.
- A P0-like conditional load with no open plan changes neither.
- An always-on `SessionLoaded` variant, which is not proposed here, would change history only.
- Deterministic across reload: every state field above and every recorded envelope. A fresh current epoch is not hashed until a later approval records it.

Mode A raw-history equality is still required **only** for complete identical canonical histories. No hash policy is adopted by this table.

Proposed commands and transitions:
- `CreateExperiment` records verified fork and initialization once. P1 plans follow Draft→Proposed→Approved→PendingWork→Executed, with explicit Rejected/Cancelled/Stale terminals. Marking/proposing/approving/queueing never changes living N/BA. Ordinary action eligibility is Live plus current authoritative DBH at or above the imported threshold, compared without annual integer-mm rounding; biology always includes all living. Reservation conflicts, duplicate IDs, stale revisions and absent/dead IDs reject execution atomically. A new proposal/approval is needed to replace a stale terminal plan; approval cannot be silently resurrected.
- `ExecuteWork` validates the whole exact reserved set, current forest revision and current session epoch, then records felling at **current DBH and current ecological year**, with plan provenance. No partial execution or natural-death timber/reward is introduced. An audit-only `ExecutionRejected` may append if safe; its pre/post biological state is identical. Invalid files cannot append to an untrusted chain.
- `AdvanceAnnual` stages a copy, validates expected revision/year and command ID, then records one atomic **`P1AnnualAdvanced` compound event**: increment year; compute competition from post-work living set; grow and age; relax crowns; calculate post-growth density/hazard using stored pre-growth CI; gather hazard then boundary victims using source suppression/ID priority; apply each natural death once; finish metrics; stale open approved/pending plans by reason. Death entries are child records of this event, **not additional mutation events replayed a second time**. Source biological cause stays `self-thinning`; `hazard`/`boundary` are mechanism annotations. Natural-dead reserved IDs invalidate affected draft/proposed plans as well; record ID-specific `ReservedTreeDied` before the general annual-revision stale reason, at most one terminal transition per plan, sorted by plan ID. These plan rules are lab design, not a claim that CCF/P0 already implements them.
- `Save` serializes a consistent committed snapshot+full ledger+tip. `Load` first validates/replays in detached state from trusted genesis, recomputes annual results from pinned model and verifies all payload/state/digest fields, compares saved snapshot, then atomically activates. No fallback to unknown model or fresh seed. Failed validation/commit leaves year, trees, plans and active tip unchanged. A command retry with identical ID/payload returns the existing result without another advance; same ID/different payload refuses. Record committed IDs in replayed state. Atomic file save failure reports failure and preserves the prior disk file; it must not imply a preceding in-memory advance was rolled back.

**Proposed P0-like conditional stale-on-load option (C-1A Option 1, owner still OPEN; M-4):** after successful detached validation, create a fresh session epoch and append exactly one `PlanStale(SessionLoaded)` per open Approved/PendingWork plan, in ordinal plan-ID order. **If no such plan is open, the load appends zero events.** This matches P0 `LabSession.FromFile` (`LabSession.cs:76–85, 144–149` at `2659db99`), which appends no other load event; P0 code is unchanged. No duplicate stale is appended for a plan already terminal. Loading the same old file with open plans in two new independent sessions creates two distinct valid histories, not duplicate deaths. An unconditional `SessionLoaded` audit event is **not** part of this option. If a future design wants one, it is a separate unapproved alternative that makes raw-history equality across any load impossible, and it must be put to the owner as such. Epoch is approval authority only, never biological RNG seed. Reapproval requires a fresh legal plan path. Alternative persistent approvals require a separate binding/revocation/security contract and owner decision. P0 policy is unchanged.

Replay of an identical complete event stream restores the recorded session fields; it does **not** simulate another user load or append load events. Live activation on load is a separate operation. Mode A/B compare biological values and **legally equivalent executed work**, not equal event counts or full snapshot/history hashes. In Mode B an uninterrupted branch that executes a still-valid plan cannot be compared as equivalent to a loaded branch where that plan is stale and unexecuted. Use no execution in both branches, or documented fresh legal approval paths producing the same felling set/time. Preserve both actual chains. No normalized/projected history digest is proposed; no deletion of load events or epochs to manufacture equality.

Annual reconciliation on **all living** trees: `Nend=Nstart−fell−naturalDeaths`; total BA `BAend=BAstart−BAfell_at_execution+ΣBAgrowth_on_post_work_living−BAdead_at_post_growth`. Per-hectare values use declared source area. Source-age growth applies to victims before death; no second growth/release after mortality. Pre-inventory Dead contributes zero new deaths. P0 action-eligible BA/N is a separate metric whose membership can change as small live trees grow past threshold; it must not be substituted in this conservation equation.

### 5.3 Proposed stand diagnostic and SI policy (C-5/C-7)

For a reproducible **P1 diagnostic only**, propose `k=min(livingN,max(1,ceil(100·areaHa)))` for nonempty stands, select descending current DBH with ordinal-ID ties, average those heights in declared numeric order; empty stand H=null, N=B=0, Dq=null, source RD=0. Report k, actual k/ha and a small-plot/rounding flag. This rounding convention is a synthetic choice, **not a verified original Broad–Lynch inventory definition**. The rule resembles the conventional UK/Irish yield-model **definition** of top height (mean height of the 100 largest-DBH stems per hectare). Field **estimation** commonly measures one largest-DBH tree per 0.01 ha subplot instead, and the two need not agree in clumped or small plots. That distinction is reviewer domain explanation (m-E), not checked against the Broad–Lynch primary text. Equivalence to the original field method stays **NOT VERIFIED**, and synthetic H is never a validated Irish measurement. A source-CCF diagnostic comparison must identify any different source sample rounding rather than call the two identical. Mortality/thinning may change diagnostic H by membership even though each survivor retains its Class III height ratio.

For P1-04 propose a separate deliberately even-aged synthetic comparison cohort; do not replace heterogeneous imported ages/heights. Declare reference age, B/N/H definitions, SI30 and historical thinning. A candidate **synthetic inferred SI30** is the value solving the pinned Broad–Lynch top-height curve for that starting age and diagnostic H; report input values, equation version, domain and residual, and label it inferred from synthetic data. If original H definition/equation/domain or unique admissible solution is unavailable, record SI as unresolved and block a domain-matched comparison; an explicitly owner/science-reviewed exploratory declared-SI scenario remains an alternative. Never substitute CCF productivity=1 or E(30)=20.4 for an observed Irish SI, force-rescale heights, or claim sample-method equivalence. Compare stand B/N/H model trajectories only; individual hazards/crowns/selection have no GROWFOR truth target. The 113 plots remain volume-equation calibration evidence, not a held-out full growth validation set.
