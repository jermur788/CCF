5. **No double writes:** the same living tree cannot be included in two prescriptions, completed removals cannot be reopened, accepted user marks/orders outrank proposals, corridor/clearing/site/headland/track exclusion takes precedence, and both coverage changes and saved unit identifiers must be reproducible or safely invalidated.

A pre-existing saved confirmed-but-invalid rack plan must **load without destructive normalization** where otherwise valid; mark new affected proposals/approvals suspended with a readable reason, preserve executed work and offer a guarded reopen/repair path. Do not claim all old saves are now invalid or use failed load as a migration strategy.
7. **Version semantics:** stable rack IDs are not sufficient if the unit polygons/meaning change. Record/reconcile rule-calibration version and unit identity, previous prescriptions/order links, source sample, world hash/round-trip and approval history as distinct proofs. No new serialized field alone does **not** prove hash stability or save compatibility. All invalid and valid historical scenarios need deterministic, fail-closed tests.
