## 1. Intent, boundaries and non-goals
The first external playable Scenario One is a **new, genuinely compact, bounded Irish Sitka-plantation forest** whose ONE genuine extraction rack serves a deliberately manageable **standing parcel on both sides**. A rack is a machine-access corridor, not a one-tree teaching exhibit or the entire treatment polygon. The player directly visits, assesses and makes reversible individual-tree decisions throughout the practical parcel, commits actual work in the normal Work Plan, advances real ecological years, reviews outcomes, returns for materially distinct later interventions and completes the scenario. The first demo is **complete Scenario One**, not a single thinning, static marteloscope, first-year tutorial or regression screenshot.

Retain the authoritative individual-tree/cell simulation and Sitka spruce, sessile Oak and Beech at comparable **modelled depth** (not equal empirical coefficient precision). Retain competition/crowns, growth and mortality where implemented, natural regeneration, planted juveniles, site/understorey/browsing, deadwood/habitat, timber assortments, contractor or owner execution, budgets, history, completion and visibly changed 3D conditions. Prioritize intelligible cause/observation over a universal optimum treatment.

**Deferred for compact first demo only:** five-rack player lesson, first-sample-to-four-target MFM extrapolation, candidate halos, multi-rack coverage prescriptions and #439/#448 future whole-rack transfer mathematics. Keep the old five-rack implementation, approved MFM spec, sources/tests and OPEN-1 through OPEN-6 questions intact; do not delete, weaken, counterfeit or mark their tests as compact PASS. Future enlargement is a separate spec, not a hidden requirement of this world.

**Outside scope:** manual harvesting physics, seasons, multiplayer, new species, land-uses/fruit economy, generalized eight-scenario engine, unmeasured photorealism, new climate or biological equations, automatically selected Crop Trees, fictional data from static marteloscopes, universal forestry thinning quotas, retroactive old-save migration, Windows build, source/Unity changes from this DRAFT.
