### D84-PJ — `PlantJuvenile` order → juvenile resolution transaction (#663, M-1; PROPOSED, Compact-only, not implemented)

**SOURCE FACTS** (static read of `main`; `Assets/` unchanged from `500ef7ac` at `f6d8a223`; not compiled or run):
- `ResolvePlanting` validates offer, species, cell, stock and quote. On any of those failures it calls `Fail`, which sets `Failed` and `resolvedYear` only. Stock, cash and juveniles are not touched.
- On success, exact-position orders deduct stock and then add one `PlantedJuvenile` with `juvenileId = "PJ" + nextJuvenileId++` and `plantingYear = report.year`, plus a shelter if one was chosen. After that, cash is settled and the order is set to `Completed` with `resolvedYear = report.year`. No `Fail` path follows the juvenile creation.
- The `AdvanceYear` loop then records `WorkResolved` with `workOrderId`, `speciesId`, position and `stockUsed`, but **no `juvenileId`**.
- `ScenarioOneWorkOrder` stores no `juvenileId`, and `PlantedJuvenileSaveData` stores no `workOrderId`.
- On load, `nextJuvenileId` is re-derived as the maximum stored PJ number + 1. Order IDs come from the saved `nextWorkOrderId`.
- Completed orders stay in `workOrders`. Only `RemovePendingOrderCore` and `CancelApprovedOrderCore` remove an order, and both record `OrderCancelled`.
- **Conclusion: nothing today persists the pairing.** It cannot be recovered afterwards without inference.

**PROPOSED transaction (Compact profile only; historical profiles unchanged):**
- **PJ-1 Designation.** During T1-C, the player designates a Pending exact-position order at one of the four G1 sites through the existing designation path. Its order ID is bound into G by that explicit act. The site match uses the **zero planar placement tolerance** required by signed #677 and by D-085 OD-12 A — the order uses the selected G1 site's exact stored x/z; the exact coordinates themselves remain **unsigned** and owned by the separate #676 world lane. Actor and shelter are per-tree Pending choices with their own quotes (CIP-016, W-5).
- **PJ-2 Approval.** Approval freezes type, site, species, stock, actor, shelter and quoted cost (W-A, W-1). There is no automatic execution and no synthetic Year event.
- **PJ-3 Settlement.** Settlement happens only in the ordinary annual resolution for report year Y, through the existing validation of stock, cash/quote and a valid site.
- **PJ-4 Success point (the only point that creates a planting row).**
  - On truly successful resolution of an order in G, the juvenile identity is the value returned by that same creation call, captured directly. It is never read back later from a list position.
  - In the same `AdvanceYear` mutation, before `WorkResolved` is recorded and before any save can run, exactly one §4.2 planting row `{workOrderId, speciesId, juvenileId, completionYear = Y}` is appended.
  - On failure (`Failed`), or for a Pending/Approved order removed by cancellation (`OrderCancelled`), no juvenile, no row, no stock or cash settlement and no review credit is produced.
  - **PJ-4a Atomic effect cluster (signed #677 T-06).** One successful resolution must settle, in one logically atomic commit scope: the created juvenile identity, cash, stock, labour/time, order status `Completed`, the real `resolvedYear`, the chosen shelter and outcome, exactly one linked teaching row, and exactly one `WorkResolved` **after** the completed facts. Validation of species, site, guard, stock, quote, funds, labour, unique IDs, counters and row capacity happens **before** any mutation. On error, staging/rollback preserves the pre-attempt real state; no fake earlier success event. Re-entry, save/quit/reload or a callback must never see or persist a half-commit, and must never pay, order, plant or credit twice. **Fault-injected settlement evidence is required before this counts as implemented:** an injected failure before or during the commit must leave a complete **old** state or a complete **new** state, never a half-plant, phantom revenue, duplicate row/event or extra year (NOT RUN). **#673 inspected the pinned `ScenarioOneManager` and found the current code is not exception-atomic; this remains an implementation requirement, not a claimed result.**
- **PJ-5 Idempotency.**
  - A second row for the same `workOrderId` or `juvenileId` is refused.
  - Load and reload never create, repair or drop rows.
  - Replaying the same year from the same pre-advance save yields the same order, juvenile IDs and rows, because the counters are restored or derived deterministically. That is static reasoning only; native replay is a future witness.
- **PJ-6 Linkage is explicit only.** Pairing is never inferred from nearest or equal coordinates, child Transform order, `plantedJuveniles` index, list adjacency, timestamps, `nextJuvenileId` arithmetic or event adjacency. Equal order and juvenile positions may serve as an *extra consistency check that can refuse*, never as the link.
- **PJ-7 Load reconciliation (admission).** Each row must match all of the following, or admission refuses:
  - a retained `Completed`, exact-position `PlantJuvenile` order in G with the same ID, species and `resolvedYear == completionYear`;
  - one `PlantedJuvenile` with that `juvenileId`, the same species, `plantingYear == completionYear` and the order's `stockItemId`;
  - one `WorkResolved`/`Succeeded` event with that `workOrderId` and year.

  A `Completed` order in G without its row does **not** refuse. **Signed #677 T-05 admits it as a bare incomplete state**: the genuine `Completed` order and its history are retained, no row is reconstructed, and **T1 is permanently unreachable for that teaching group** because a lawful backdated row does not exist and only `Failed`/cancelled group references may be replaced, not `Completed` ones. A false positive row or `reviewAcknowledgedYear>=0` claim in that state is still refused. Later ordinary death or promotion of the juvenile does **not** invalidate the row: §4.2 requires existence only at creation, and the juvenile ID is kept with `promotedTreeId`. Other G members must be `Pending`, `Approved` or `Failed`, or be absent with an `OrderCancelled` event.
- **PJ-8 Multi-year Y\*.** Rows may carry different completion years. Y\* = max(`completionYear`) over the final four. A replacement completed in a later year raises Y\*. Reviews acknowledged for earlier years then do not satisfy CT-2.
- **PJ-9 Negatives.**
  - A failed planting leaves no juvenile, no row, no stock spend and no cash change.
  - Cancel and re-designate gives a new order ID, a new quote and a new approval. The old ID is never reused or resurrected.
  - Save/reload between approval and settlement, and after settlement before the review, changes nothing.
  - Two orders at the same site are refused at designation.
