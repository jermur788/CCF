## 1. Intent, boundaries and non-goals
The first external playable Scenario One is a **new, genuinely compact, bounded Irish Sitka-plantation forest** whose ONE genuine extraction rack serves a deliberately manageable **standing parcel on both sides**. A rack is a machine-access corridor, not a one-tree teaching exhibit or the entire treatment polygon. The player directly visits, assesses and makes reversible individual-tree decisions throughout the practical parcel, commits actual work in the normal Work Plan, advances real ecological years, reviews outcomes, returns for materially distinct later interventions and completes the scenario. The first demo is **complete Scenario One**, not a single thinning, static marteloscope, first-year tutorial or regression screenshot.

Retain the authoritative individual-tree/cell simulation and Sitka spruce, sessile Oak and Beech at comparable **modelled depth** (not equal empirical coefficient precision). Retain competition/crowns, growth and mortality where implemented, natural regeneration, planted juveniles, site/understorey/browsing, deadwood/habitat, timber assortments, contractor or owner execution, budgets, history, completion and visibly changed 3D conditions. Prioritize intelligible cause/observation over a universal optimum treatment.

**Deferred for compact first demo only:** five-rack player lesson, first-sample-to-four-target MFM extrapolation, candidate halos, multi-rack coverage prescriptions and #439/#448 future whole-rack transfer mathematics. Keep the old five-rack implementation, approved MFM spec, sources/tests and OPEN-1 through OPEN-6 questions intact; do not delete, weaken, counterfeit or mark their tests as compact PASS. Future enlargement is a separate spec, not a hidden requirement of this world.

**Outside scope:** manual harvesting physics, seasons, multiplayer, new species, land-uses/fruit economy, generalized eight-scenario engine, unmeasured photorealism, new climate or biological equations, automatically selected Crop Trees, fictional data from static marteloscopes, universal forestry thinning quotas, retroactive old-save migration, Windows build, source/Unity changes from this DRAFT.

## 3. World, identity and persistence contract (A)
**S-01 Distinct world:** a newly authored compact O1 profile with an explicit stable scenario/world ID, geometry ID and content-generation/version identity. **Exact ID string, version/schema number and slot filename are OPEN** until save-owner review; they must be distinct from historical scenario-one/Legacy40/Enlarged80 identities. Do not interpret absent fields in a historical save as compact. New World selector/Start/Continue clearly names both compact O1 and historic five-rack O1 when offered. No old-slot implicit conversion.

**S-02 Slots and nonmutating refusal:** new compact saves/temporary/backup files use a dedicated disjoint namespace under persistentDataPath; the current forest-save.json, .tmp, .bak and old snapshots are never overwritten, renamed or automatically imported. Select the intended world **before** reading a slot. Inspect complete JSON/schema/geometry/scenario identity/version/content/field validity and all tree/cell/order links **before any game-world, PlayerPrefs, slot, backup, objective or ID allocator mutation**. Wrong-world or unknown-world loads produce a clear rejection and safe return to the selected profile; no fallback to the other world. Detect future-schema as incompatible rather than best-effort mutate the wrong profile. Rejected load leaves the entire live/disk state byte-identical, including work/order counters.

**S-03 Compatible existing histories:** preserve every genuinely old v1–v24 save supported by current contracts, Legacy40 fallback rules (missing/pre-v20 geometry), Enlarged80 identities, old five-rack ID/lesson/MFM/prescription records, frozen Reference Future v1 schedule and hashes, model-0 pure Sitka anchors and the v24 historical one-way first-guided-review latch (false on old saves, no retroactive backfill). Old saves remain routed to the old profile and its original objectives, not new compact completion. Do not rebaseline frozen hashes, alter serial field layouts or history provenance to get tests green. Save-owner must explicitly approve schema append/identity placement and archive/hash effects.

**S-04 Eight-scenario coexistence:** D-078–D-081 keep O1–O8 as independent scenario cases/roles, O2 Working Mixed Forest as a separate later world, and per-scenario budgets/history. Compact O1 is the **first O1 playable profile**, not O2, not an estate compartment or shared money. The proposed shared save router is a scoped prerequisite for compact first profile; its interfaces can serve O2 later without falsely claiming full eight-scenario implementation. Existing O2 save planning is not automatic compact approval.

**S-05 Geometry candidate gate:** no numeric stand length, rack length/width, initial tree count, age structure, position, player walking minutes, release spacing or performance is approved. Historical 80 m/1,344 and 40 m/336 are **comparison fixtures only**. Pre-code fixture/route study shall enumerate at least two independently generated compact candidate footprints, map genuine existing arrival/service access and one contiguous rack, its clearance and usable management polygons both sides, tree/crown/edge/seed neighbourhood diagnostics, surveyable stem density/crop opportunities, credible basal-area/harvest/cash traces and complete marking route. ## 4. Player loop and lessons (B)

**S-07 Direct whole-parcel Objective 2:** from the actual rack-served standing-area outline, the player can physically inspect **all eligible management trees**, not merely three stems inside a sample. **S-08 Planning → orders → execution:** authored physical marks and current observations are one state; transient conditional map previews are another; derived Pending Work Plan orders and Approved orders another; Completed/Failed/Cancelled outcomes and recorded old marks/history another. **S-09 Annual progression:** inspect → mark/plan → preview/quote → approve → resolve work by existing simulation → advance one full ecological year → read annual results/history → walk the actual forest → repeat. ## 5. Work Plan, biological outcomes and economics (C–D)

**S-11 Small-stand solvency:** the existing €2,500 job minimum, €12,000 starting cash and costs are **existing gameplay calibration**, not imported Irish market truth; report their sensitivity at actual compact volume. **S-12 Same biological engine:** retain annual competition/light/crown release, Sitka adult development, species-aware mixed occupancy, Oak/Beech growth/juveniles and browse sensitivity, seed rain, establishment and promotion, site/understorey effects, natural and planted regeneration, protection, mortality/deadwood and habitat. **S-13 Three substantial cycles:** qualification witness must include **at least three genuine distinct management cycles** in the same compact world, with intervening observation/no-work years, a later decision changed by real data, accepted/failed work history as appropriate, and reviews acknowledged only after viewed. Earlier legitimate maintenance and annual stepping remain possible. Old S1-D two-cycle completion predicate cannot silently certify the new three-cycle goal.

**S-14 Dual time advance / stop:** single-year always available under normal save/work/review guards. No skip with Pending/Approved orders, unread mandatory review, active reference preview or unfinished tutorial, and stop at first new objective, outcome/failure, storm or review gate, year-25/year-100 gate or lesson shift. Persist every quiet year's actual records. No fabricated review acknowledgements. Cancel only between completed yearly steps; parity oracle is repeated annual ticks without UI acknowledgements (distinguish UI-driven acknowledged replay).

**S-15 Honest completion:** define new compact-specific objective IDs/achievement provenance and a versioned criteria policy before source; derive from actual annual/resolution/ack records and species/ecology/cash state. Completion requires a credible continuing-cover condition, functioning three-species management exposure (not every Oak/Beech grown to commercial maturity), real later substantial cycles, economic/logistical outcomes and readable year-indexed review. Area-dependent targets are calibrated separately, not copy-pasted year 25 / counts or implied 100-year result. Completion is never granted from the old five-rack objective IDs, v24 historical teaching latch, zero-action year, reloaded historical mark or instant skip. Preserve current policy of continued observation/management after completion where supported; record completion once and do not retroactively re-score historical old worlds.

## 9. Acceptance criterion
The complete demo PASS means a new compact save is demonstrably distinct, the one rack genuinely serves traversable trees, the player makes/undoes meaningful manual decisions, approves actual work, sees real Sitka/Oak/Beech biological and economic responses, revisits changed conditions for **three spaced, substantive cycles** with true no-work years and useful history, reaches an earned completion and continues appropriately; the same release still faithfully loads **every protected old-world class** without rewriting its data. No smaller game, map-only theatre, fabricated MFM, unexplained instant year or visual-only ecology satisfies this contract.
