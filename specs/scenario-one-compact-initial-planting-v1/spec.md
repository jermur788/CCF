## 1A. Owner-approved entrance clearing and winding pedestrian path — 19:09 UTC

**Direct owner direction:** Provide a clearing at the entrance where a truck could enter to collect timber and cars could park, with a simple winding path through the woodland. This replaces the indefinite “boundary access only” presentation with **a recognisable entrance and a walkable teaching route** while retaining the no-through-rack decision. The precise engineering arrangement is **not approved or proven by the owner's concept**.

**Selected layout/story for design evaluation:**
- **Entrance clearing at an appropriate parcel edge**, reached from an existing permissible road/track if one can be grounded. The Manager proposes that it be **already present when the player arrives** so early teaching starts with woodland assessment and planting rather than mandatory road-building. If the generator needs exclusions or an authored opening, the starting tree inventory must be explicitly recalculated, preserving the correct reason (“pre-existing access”, not a year-1 Fell mark or silently lost mature Sitka).
- Use the clearing as a **multi-use arrival/staging place, but not simultaneous unrestricted car parking and truck loading**. Ordinary visits may allow parked cars; during loading, the relevant unloading/turning/manoeuvre area and timber stacks must be kept clear of visitors and parked vehicles, with work-zone closure and proper material handling. Do not create a misleading public-road loading bay or guarantee a passing articulated lorry can turn inside 30m. Choose **vehicle class and turning sweep only after source/land/operations evidence**; a smaller suitable collection vehicle or an entrance partly on separately permitted land could be considered later only if properly justified and approved.
- **A winding, single pedestrian path** connects the entrance, woodland observation locations and planting areas, returning to the entrance or looping only if the exact stand supports it. Path geometry should respond to living Sitka, ground vegetation, access and teaching sightlines. Width, surface, route, swept pedestrian clearance, accessible gradient, drainage and crossings are **unselected**, not invented engineering standards. No timber truck driving along this path; modest tools or manual timber movement are separate safe-work decisions.
- The original research **189 uncut Sitka** is a **pre-layout input**. An entrance clearing and walking path may intersect source tree positions; their **pre-existing omissions or historical clearing explanation must be designed and verified**. Do not describe the final scene as both “189 live Sitka, unmodified” and “cleared entrance + maintained path” unless actual collision checks genuinely support both.
- The single proposed **G1 2+2 mixed Oak/Beech** site needs actual post-H1 light, browsing, ground vegetation, path reach and Work Plan checks. The two former G2 candidates are not adopted as a healthy starting planting lesson; any extra trees are a subsequent explicit design option, not an eight-sapling completion requirement.

**Operational evidence:** Teagasc guidance on [preparing for harvest operations](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/advice/timber-harvesting/harvesting-and-selling-timber-from-conifer-forests/prepare-for-harvesting-operations/) calls for a safe entrance, loading area and ability to turn and exit. Its [forest road guidance](https://teagasc.ie/crops/forestry/advice/management/construction-of-forest-roads/) notes that a bellmouth may be sufficient for some small roadside sites, but loading on public roads is not allowed. [Coillte timber haulage site rules](https://www.coillte.ie/driverportal/docs/Coillte-Code-of-Practice-for-Timber-Removals_2023.pdf) require turning areas to remain free of parked vehicles during loading. These are **planning and safety constraints**, not evidence that the selected 30m world already satisfies them. Appropriate site-specific forestry/entrance permissions remain unresolved.

**Stop gates:** A full-size timber lorry may be too long or difficult to reverse/load from the external approach even though **on-site turning is not required**. If the available 30m parcel, road frontage or ground cannot meet the vehicle/parking/path/planting combination, **return NEEDS CHANGES rather than scale a real lorry down invisibly**. The compact learning objective is more important than forcing every vehicle into the original 30m research plot. If operations cannot be depicted truthfully, label timber collection as a separately qualified abstraction or redesign after user/Manager agreement.

## 2. Why a provisional 30m woodland and one G1 mixed 2+2 lesson

**Direct #460 Unity evidence:** The 20×20m scratch case had **82** initial Sitka, **69** post-access inspectable retained trees and **277.61m²** model-derived net standing area; 30×30m had **189**, **170** inspectable retained trees and **691.55m²** model-derived standing area. The 8m Hegyi edge envelope held **79/82** 20m stems versus **157/189** 30m. Thus 30m offers greater prospective placement choice and a modestly less edge-dominated sampled world than 20m, at the cost of longer derived inspection tours (**498m versus 211m**). **These tours are not human playtime and do not prove the larger choice is manageable.** The #484 nearby-forest competition increase is conditional on real outside trees, not an argument to invent a buffer around a finite property.

**Direct #490 Unity research:** The source-authentic 30m all-Sitka world (189 originals) had its own Year1 19-stem rack clearance before **one sheltered owner-planted Oak at (1.7,−3.3)** and **one contractor unsheltered Beech at (3.3,−3.3)** in Year2, both alive/promoted by elapsed Year40 in **one deterministic research run**. It found no naturally recruited Oak/Beech by then. The Oak had a very narrow modelled crown by Year40 and typical outside oblique views hid broadleaves in a closed Sitka stand. This motivates **more than one individually traceable tree per species and deliberate field visibility**, not a guaranteed 4+4 success claim. **These two south-side coordinates depended on the research rack clearance and are NOT verified no-rack placements or production pins.** The #490 fixture disabled the normal ScenarioOneManager mortality handler; zero deadwood records in it do not establish normal play lacks logs. No new 40-year native run is approved here.

**Manager gameplay judgment:** Four initial planting decisions (2 Oak+2 Beech) offer more individuals than the earlier one-pair #490 research without forcing dark or vehicle-conflicting G2. **G1-only and four initial saplings are reversible gameplay calibrations**, not forestry stocking norms or guarantee of thriving trees.
