# Scenario One — Repeated Representative Rack Management V1

**Status: DRAFT — Product direction confirmed in D-076; implementation NOT authorised.**  
**Owner:** Overall Manager #200 · **Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Parent authority:** D-065 approved Multi-Scale Forestry Management V1 (MFM), D-064 Rack V2, D-071 five parallel racks; D-073 decisions and later observations; D-074 periodic CCF intervention cycles; D-075 two optional advance modes.  
**Linked drafts:** [multi-year feedback V2](../scenario-one-multiyear-decision-outcome-feedback-v2/spec.md) and [management cadence / two advance options](../scenario-one-management-cadence-and-advance-v1/spec.md).

## 1. User outcome and scope

A Scenario One player demonstrates forestry judgment in **one representative rack area during a substantive management cycle**, reviews how far that example applies to **similar current conditions elsewhere**, and approves any wider work through the established MFM / Work Plan gates. Over subsequent management cycles the player can inspect the changed stand and, when useful, demonstrate a new treatment in another representative rack/area. The effects of **work actually executed in any rack** appear in the authoritative 3D forest after each annual step; truthful rack-area and whole-stand summaries help compare later management reviews.

The representative rack is **a place in which the player demonstrates a meaningful set of tree decisions**, not one arbitrarily chosen single tree, nor a blanket instruction to fell the same number of trees in all racks. Even one rack can include materially different areas; sample similarity and coverage are measured by source-derived rack-served units, not merely rack number.

The five existing parallel racks in the 80m Scenario One remain **access and coverage geometry**. They are not five mandatory separate treatments. A single representative sample may cover many comparable areas in a uniform stand. A distinct source sample is appropriate only when the area is materially different or later changed enough to require renewed judgment. Larger future plantations may require multiple samples for different management units, but this DRAFT **does not** add terrain/slope/wet-ground systems to Scenario One.

## 3. Design requirements

**FR-R1 — Initial demonstration unchanged.** The existing fixed third-rack teaching footprint and first guided crop-tree/release lessons remain authoritative, including three Crop Trees, 8m teaching spacing, 8m Hegyi neighbourhood, 30% Share teaching and 40% BA safeguard. This proposal is a **later-cycle extension** and does not redefine first-rack lesson credit.

**FR-R2 — Representative treatment per management cycle.** When a later substantial stand treatment is considered, the player may choose one supported rack-served sample location (possibly a different rack), assess **actual trees in 3D**, manually select appropriate Crop Trees and demonstrate a bounded treatment there. A new sample is **not automatically mandatory each calendar year**, and may not inherit the first lesson's fixed numeric requirements as a universal stocking rule. Detailed second-sample geometry and eligibility remain to be qualified.

**FR-R3 — Similarity groups and coverage honesty.** The treatment can extend only to rack-served units with **similar current observable conditions**. MFM Covered / Not covered / Excluded / Manual categories are reused or explicitly versioned; no automatic stand-wide coverage by rack proximity alone. In heterogeneous areas propose an additional genuinely different representative sample, separate prescription, exclusion or manual work. Distinguish measured/modelled current density, DBH, competition, species mix and track/shelter masking from unimplemented terrain, soil/moisture, slope, exposure and wind classes. An inaccurate or unsupported similarity test fails closed.

**FR-R4 — Multiple sources without overlap or double work.** Every proposed area has one explicit source/rule/provenance for the applicable cycle; when more than one sample could cover it, the player must see a deterministic conflict/ownership resolution, or the unit stays Manual/Not covered. A previous year's prescription cannot silently be reused after growth/felling changes the stand. Old approved/past executed orders remain historical facts; no rewriting or duplicate felling. The first source sample retains its original meaning, while later samples receive genuinely new source/year identification only under an approved save/hash-safe design.

**FR-R5 — Manual player judgement and staged commitment.** Outside-sample spacing-only Crop Tree candidates remain suggestions requiring individual physical inspection/confirmation (or an explicit approved alternative in a later product decision). No automated quality/seed/wind/habitat ranking. Unapproved proposals are not Fell marks. Prescription approval is not Work Plan approval and is not annual execution. Manual marks/protective choices and existing MFM/Work Plan rules take precedence. The player can manage areas manually.

**FR-R6 — Yearly and repeated-cycle visibility.** After any treatment is actually executed, all affected trees/canopy/juveniles update via the **existing authoritative yearly simulation**, including areas outside the source rack. Annual Review and Stand Map should distinguish sample, proposed, approved, executed, previously treated and excluded areas. A selected rack/unit may show **recorded treatment per year** (if work-order provenance supports it), **current observed condition** and **derived year-to-year aggregate comparison only where the saved data supports that spatial granularity**. All reported years/rack links must be source-labelled; absent per-rack historical ecological data must say “not recorded” or avoid numerical comparisons. Do not claim causal responses or automatically translate stand-total changes to each rack.

**FR-R7 — Timing integration.** `Advance 1 Year` and `Skip to Next Management Year` both use normal annual simulation and the same recorded history. A skip may be blocked by pending/approved orders, the first mandatory review or another user choice; skipping neither automatically applies a prior prescription nor runs a fresh sample. At the next *suggested review year* the player may continue observing, manage a comparable-area sample or choose a different area. No forced treatment just because a date arrived.

**FR-R8 — Species, geometry, compatibility and evidence.** Scenario One remains Irish Enlarged80 with five parallel Rack V2 access racks; Legacy40/frozen Reference Future v1 and older no-prescription saves retain their original behavior. The first MFM V1 Sitka-adult quantitative coverage is protected. Any new multi-species treatment vocabulary, multiple source/sample state, spatial ecological history or save fields require separate explicit approval, versioning and proven world-hash exclusions/invariants. No unchecked wind/slope class or new ecological formula.

## 4. Specific decisions pending, not silently settled

1. Does *each later substantive cycle* require a newly selected sample, or may a player deliberately reuse a previously sampled rack **after a fresh current-state demonstration**? Proposed: fresh sample per distinct treatment **when changed conditions require it**, no mandatory rack rotation.
2. Which later-cycle rack-segment can serve as source without changing the first lesson's fixed third-rack sample? Proposed: one selected rack-served area in the existing geometry, selected by the player; needs authentic fixture proof.
3. How do multiple prescriptions split heterogeneous areas without overlapping double treatment? Proposed: fail-closed one-source-per-unit in each cycle, explicit Manual fallback and versioned ownership.
4. What history granularity is required for “see how all racks change each year”? Baseline: 3D world + recorded per-area work & current observations; *year-by-year per-rack ecological numbers* are a **new data/possibly persistence feature** and are not yet proven by annual snapshots.
5. Does the scenario require the player to personally **sample every rack by completion**? **No such requirement follows from the user instruction.** The learning goal is credible management of the plantation using representative areas and justified observations, not an arbitrary “visit all five racks” counter.
6. Whether additional samples are triggered by uncovered fraction or heterogeneous condition clusters, rather than a fixed number of racks. Existing `SecondSampleRequired` policy is unapproved and **must not be switched on without empirical/gameplay calibration**.

## 5. Release/test gates

**Gate A:** Manager approves exact decision R1–R8 against latest source, coordinates MFM, S1-D, multi-year feedback and two-advance workflows, and resolves minimum necessary policy choices. **Gate B:** publish four-file APPROVED/IMPLEMENTING spec and issue with disjoint source worktrees/locks. **Gate C:** small deterministic authentic-fixture coverage/overlap/provenance tests on a homogeneous Sitka stand and one deliberately heterogeneous stand with uncovered units; compare one sample versus two samples, repeat after growth. **Gate D:** focused full-cycle 3D/Map/Annual Review/Work Plan Unity qualification at both supported UI sizes, saved old/frozen world identity and genuine species-parity exclusions. **Gate E:** independent reviewer and Manager integration ruling. Whole milestone Tier-3 only once on integrated demo candidate.
