## FR-2 — Guidance scope, safety and prompt lifecycle

First inspect rendered visibility, occlusion, enabled state and the actual event-routing boundary. A surface may **own guidance** without owning all input: the rack prompts are inline cards, not proven modal input locks. Help/onboarding/session/startup/reference preview suspend background progression guidance while visible/active; only that surface's safe explicit navigation/close may be cued. Full-screen Objectives/Map/Work Plan/Review use their own active scope. Rack planning mode/drag is editing state, not a second modal.

For a single visible rack prompt, proposed primary is **Keep approved work** (`map-consent-decline`), **Keep my racks** (`map-edit-reset-cancel`) or **Keep the plan confirmed** (`map-reopen-decline`). T2 must explicitly remove destructive primary emphasis and promote the safe alternative only after D-08 approval. “Cancel approved work”, “Reset layout”, “Reopen the plan” and “Advance anyway” must never receive a guidance cue or automatic focus/activation. Work Plan advance warning should cue its safe return/review choice, not a consequential shortcut. Approval/advance in normal flow still require deliberate activation and visible cost/consequence information.

**ConsentPending ∥ ResetPending:** both cards can render in one rack card; DOM/order/Boolean priority does not establish a unique owner. Suppress ALL progression and prompt primaries, show “Two rack questions are open; keep or dismiss them before continuing” (final copy review required), retain safe choices as secondary, and do not invent a winning action. Production mutual-exclusion repair is a separate claim if approved, not a prerequisite secretly folded into guidance. The no-primary fallback must remain even if that later repair is made.

When an eligible map editing pointer-down clears prompts, or navigation/context closes Help, discard the previous cue/focus target and rederive from actual current state. Do not claim the prompt waits for an answer. The spec does not authorise changing that dismissal/gesture behaviour. A stale guidance key/click must never execute a hidden/background control or leak through to forest actions. If meeting that requirement needs broader routing or consent lifecycle changes than D-07/D-08 approve, STOP for Manager scope, do not silently add a modal system.
