## 1. Player-facing outcome and principles

**Teaching goal:** walk and learn: “Manage the standing trees served by one rack; choose the Crop Trees that merit continued growth, decide which local competitors need intervention, review that real treatment, then judge where the same approach is suitable in the other four racks.”

- One representative **rack-served standing area** is the *source*, not a 24m rectangle, the access track itself, or a single example tree. **All appropriate** means all that the player considers suitable **after surveying the eligible source**; it never means every stem, exactly one per arbitrary interval, a precomputed score, or that every Crop Tree needs a fell.
- Five persisted Rack V2 **access corridors** remain unchanged. Tree marking is in 3D. Stand Map shows source/destination/proposal boundaries and navigates; it cannot certify unseen tree health.
- The player remains free to manage other areas fully manually, opt out of extension, reject suggestions, retain ecological trees, or deliberately exclude materially different areas with an explained result.
- A proposal is **not** a Fell mark, a Crop Tree, an approved order or execution. Distinct stages, truthful provenance and no hidden AI “best trees.”

## 2. Area, identity and coverage semantics

**FR-01 Geometry source:** read persisted *confirmed* five-rack plan and its stable rack IDs/finite entry-to-far segments. Order only for teaching display, never to rewrite identity. Establish one disjoint standing working polygon W_i per rack from neighbour midline partition; clip to property polygon, **finite rack-served length and end caps**, subtract actual corridor, headland, feeder/other tracks, built/clearings, protected, immature and unreachable area footprints using authoritative world data. Mark external leftovers as explicit remainders/manual, not automatically owned by nearest rack. A rack corridor itself never contains a teaching Crop Tree. Validate degenerate, crossing, off-edge or nonparallel racks; on unsupported geometry fail closed. Boundary ties are deterministic; masks take precedence. The exact end-cap allowance and outermost-strip treatment are **OPEN-1**.

**FR-02 Source selection:** offer a guided *central representative rack* as first-time default (consistent with earlier third rack), but never claim it is statistically representative merely because it is central. Show live comparable-condition summary for all five. If source is unacceptable/non-representative, user may pick another **eligible** rack with a distinct verified source identity; choosing arbitrary rack needs **OPEN-2** ratification. A source has stable persisted rack ID, polygon/rule version and source year; its three Low/Centre/High old coverage subareas are collectively *source-owned*, not target candidates. No prescribed number of Crop Trees. Do not require five personal demonstrations.

**FR-03 Source inspection:** show accessible map shape and natural entrance-to-far traversal; in 3D indicate progress across both sides and full reachable length. Track explicit inspect/review actions or stable sector resolution (not camera proximity alone). The player inspects vigour/visible health, form, crown, competition, spacing, ecological function and regeneration; the game does **not** produce an authoritative unseen quality verdict. Confirmed player Crop Trees remain real Blue marks, never a separate surrogate list; Red remains mutually exclusive. A tree with retained habitat value or a competitor with no justified action can remain. Completion checks resolved **whole source area** + enough authentic judgement for the teaching intent; no fixed mark count. Exact sector/visit evidence is a *new design calibration*, not an Irish method.

**FR-04 Local release judgement:** for each selected Crop Tree, show its actual neighbours and CompetitionShare; the player chooses Fell/retain/no work and can explain why. Distinguish existing Hegyi 8m model neighbourhood from forestry-quality judgement and old 8m teaching-spacing calibration. The 30% Share and 40% cumulative BA old teaching safeguards must not be weakened in an existing save. For a new full-rack source, preserve an effective 40% cumulative unit cap or stronger until separately calibrated; whether 30% is a requirement for **each** tree or only an advisory teaching target is **OPEN-3**. Do not penalize legitimate no-release judgement by ordering a spurious fell; insufficient executed release may mean no transferable rule.

**FR-05 First-loop timing:** new Objective 2 source survey and selection; next guided phase addresses competitor decisions; ordinary Work Plan is used to approve/execute source treatment; sample outcomes reviewed; then comparative coverage and proposals for the other four racks are part of the same first complete *management teaching loop*, without pretending unexecuted removal is executed evidence. Current D-065 derives from **executed** sample. A pre-execution extrapolation preview may be shown as explicitly advisory and non-committal, but must not create pending work until source proof, accepted area confirmation and prescription gates are satisfied. Whether to require source execution before any durable extrapolation is **OPEN-4** (recommended yes). Existing stable saved step IDs must not be silently renamed; add versioned new sequence IDs or an explicit migration mapping.

## 3. Source-to-destination calculations

**FR-06 Current-state coverage:** reuse/version approved MFM coverage and closed-vocabulary explanations. Each of the other four rack areas is evaluated *by subarea* where heterogeneous, not claimed Covered merely by shared rack name. Measure current living Sitka adult eligible density per masked net ha, mean DBH, authoritative CompetitionShare, species composition, basal area, marks, open/completed work, juvenile/protected features and confirmed rack access. Compare against a **truthful source pre-treatment baseline** with a recorded year/observation basis; existing executed-removal stump reconstruction may be reused only after proof it covers all whole-source events. If provenance is incomplete, refuse transfer and request current new sample rather than interpolate.

- Coverage states **Covered / NotCovered / Excluded / Manual** retained and explained with exact reason. Different age/DBH, density, competition, species mix or feature conflict may be NotCovered/Excluded; unknown current unmodelled slope/soil/site exposure cannot be invented as known, and materially relevant unknowns may require Manual.
- Existing MFM03 tolerances (20% density / 15% mean DBH / 20% mean Competition; min 30 adults, 16m candidate proposal spacing) are *one old fixture's GAMEPLAY CALIBRATION*, not inherited automatically for a whole-rack source. New comparisons, minimum power/coverage and outer-strip handling must be versioned/calibrated on inspected five-area fixture before production. Never display statistical confidence intervals or Irish probabilities not supported by data.
- No external area gains a more permissive coverage class from simply changing source/target ordering or advancing time. Current player/manual decisions take precedence.

**FR-07 Derived rule:** use *the player's actual Crop Tree choices* and executed/verified neighbour treatment in the **whole** source, not the old three marker slots. Replay any candidate rule on source and display agreement/disagreement, per-Crop Tree release, removed BA, source sample size and honest limitations. Store/version rule identity and source-year basis. Allowed treatment vocabulary: site/current-state match; player-confirmed eligible Crop Trees; observable spacing-only *inspection prompts*; neighbours ranked by authoritative CompetitionShare; released Share target derived from sample under accepted minimum; cumulative BA ceiling (max 40% unless stricter); explicit exclusions and reason codes. No inferred bole health, branch defect, wind stability or ecology feature from DBH rank alone. No silent changes to pure-Sitka growth/ecology model.

**FR-08 Proposed transfer:** each destination's *actual* eligible stems and current local neighbours generate independent candidate inspection prompts and subsequent release suggestions only around **individual 3D-confirmed** Crop Trees (unless an explicitly approved alternative changes D-065). No mirrored tree IDs, positions, fixed Crop Tree counts or percentage-volume mandates. Count a competitor once even if it helps multiple Crop Trees. Apply per-unit and whole-treatment consequences, safeguards, manual marks, juvenile exclusions and current open work. Show “suggested for inspection; suitability not assessed.” Incomplete coverage leaves area NotCovered/Manual and no pending work.

**FR-09 Scope:** MFM quantitative first pass remains **adult Sitka only**; no automatic Oak/Beech Crop Tree designation or Sitka-derived release. Player may still inspect/manage broadleaves manually under existing normal mechanics. Species parity does not by itself validate multi-species extrapolation. No off-property treatment, future stand simulator or new soil/wind classifier.
