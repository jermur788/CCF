## 1. Player-facing outcome and principles

**Teaching goal:** walk and learn: “Manage the standing trees served by one rack; choose the Crop Trees that merit continued growth, decide which local competitors need intervention, review that real treatment, then judge where the same approach is suitable in the other four racks.”

- One representative **rack-served standing area** is the *source*, not a 24m rectangle, the access track itself, or a single example tree. **All appropriate** means all that the player considers suitable **after surveying the eligible source**; it never means every stem, exactly one per arbitrary interval, a precomputed score, or that every Crop Tree needs a fell.
- Five persisted Rack V2 **access corridors** remain unchanged. Tree marking is in 3D. Stand Map shows source/destination/proposal boundaries and navigates; it cannot certify unseen tree health.
- The player remains free to manage other areas fully manually, opt out of extension, reject suggestions, retain ecological trees, or deliberately exclude materially different areas with an explained result.
- A proposal is **not** a Fell mark, a Crop Tree, an approved order or execution. Distinct stages, truthful provenance and no hidden AI “best trees.”
