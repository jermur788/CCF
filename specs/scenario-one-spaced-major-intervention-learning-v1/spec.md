# Scenario One — Spaced Major-Intervention Learning Objective V1

**Status:** DRAFT — REQUIRED PRODUCT OUTCOME D-074, NOT IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORITY.  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-12, Manager #200. **Depends on:** S1-D current accepted two-cycle completion, Scenario One Year25/100 and save compatibility, D-065/D-076 representative rack management and D-075/D-077 two time-advance choices.

## Product requirement

Scenario One should teach a **meaningful repeated CCF major-management cycle**: deliberate stand-scale treatment, **observation and stand response over intervening years**, subsequent on-site reassessment and another justified treatment where appropriate. The existing `HasReviewedTwoCycleSequence` merely accepts two successful Work Plans in different years with one review between; **planting in Year X and Year X+1 can satisfy it**. This is insufficient as the full educational demonstration of periodic silvicultural management intended by the user.

**The work is not automatically required every fourth year.** Four years is the separately approved D-077 *navigation suggestion*. Whether a later objective uses a minimum elapsed interval, review observation or another condition remains a product design question and must not be conflated with a real forestry rule. The player retains genuine discretion and can plant, protect or otherwise maintain the stand in interim years.

## Required design boundaries

- **Distinguish major treatment** such as stand thinning/release around judged Crop Trees from initial rack *access clearance*, incidental felling, planting, juvenile protection, pruning and other necessary maintenance. The source needs an honest, inspectable classification grounded in authoritative work/event/provenance; no invented judgement of real tree quality.
- **Show observation years:** records for intervening years with canopy/stand/young-growth snapshots when present, the player's recorded review, and a later explicit choice; no invented previous seedling survival or intervention causality.
- **Tie to D-076 sample-to-comparable-area flow:** a new representative sample may be selected for a later cycle where conditions warrant, but no compulsory five-rack visitation objective or automatic prescription approval.
- **Preserve past gameplay:** the original S1-D/Scenario One completion predicate, old-saved outcome, Year25/100 and Reference Future v1 cannot be retroactively invalidated or silently recomputed under a new strict rule. Any new objective for *new worlds* must use a documented world/save-version or scenario-mode policy proven against authentic old saves; if unavailable, choose non-gating teaching objective or stop for another Manager decision.
- **No unverified forestry coefficient:** if a minimum interval is chosen, record as gameplay calibration and permit earlier work. Never show a rigid CCF thinning timetable.
- **Separate safe delivery:** the dual-advance controls in [Cadence V1](../scenario-one-management-cadence-and-advance-v1/spec.md) may implement first; this objective is a **distinct follow-on** with own approval and tests. Do not change `HasReviewedTwoCycleSequence` as a side effect of writing the time-skip UI.
